### Who is a DevOps engineer?

A DevOps engineer is a professional who has both software development and IT operations experience. Their primary goal is to ensure that the development process is as efficient and automated as possible, while also ensuring that the system runs smoothly in production. They are responsible for designing and implementing the tools and processes that enable teams to develop, test, and deploy software rapidly and reliably.
