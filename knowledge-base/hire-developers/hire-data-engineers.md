## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a data engineer do, and when should I hire one?

A data engineer builds and maintains the systems that collect, organize, and structure enterprise data to make it usable for reporting, analytics, AI use cases, and product workflows. You should hire data engineers if you’re working with inconsistent data, your teams depend on manual exports, or your current workflows can’t support growing data volume, new sources, or real-time needs.

### What skills should enterprises look for when they hire data engineers?

Enterprises should look for data engineers who are strong in SQL and at least one programming language (commonly Python), since these are the basics for working with data at scale. On the business side, prioritize problem-solvers who can work with stakeholders, translate messy requirements into clear data solutions, and communicate trade‑offs in simple language. Curiosity, ownership, and a good track record matter as much as any specific tool.
