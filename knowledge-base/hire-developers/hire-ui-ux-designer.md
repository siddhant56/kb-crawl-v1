UX design is an umbrella term that includes a wide range of specialized roles that are interconnected to each other. Before opting for UI UX design consultant services, know the types of designers and their roles in the process

### UX Designers

The term is for designers who make products and services usable, accessible, and enjoyable. They handle everything from research and design to providing updates once the finished product is released

### UI Designers

The minute aspects of the overall design, such as the way buttons look, where they are placed, and what they do, are what UI UX experts work on

### Visual Designers

Visual designers expand the user experience into new regions. Their task is to create a brand's visual identity, making sure that it is consistent across all marketing materials

### UI/UX Frontend Developer

A website's or web application's UI UX frontend developer implements interactive and visual design concepts for a pleasant and seamless user experience

### UX Strategists

UX strategists often instruct design teams or entire businesses to adopt a more user-centric approach to market and present their products since they are more researchers than creators

### Graphic Designers

Graphic designers promote creativity and make your digital product more appealing. Through visual graphics and animations, they will help you create a unique brand story

### What is the difference between UX design and UI design?

The UX designer plans how the user interacts with the interface and the actions they should take to achieve the goal, differentiating UX from UI. On the other hand, the UI designer ensures each page visually communicates that path.
