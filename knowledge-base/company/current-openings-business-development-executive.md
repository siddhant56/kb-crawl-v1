### How you should be?

We’re looking for a proactive and results-driven professional with strong communication skills and a customer-focused mindset. You should be organized, persistent, and comfortable managing multiple leads in a fast-paced, target-oriented environment.

### What you’ll Do?

  * Identify, research, and generate new business leads through various channels including cold calling, email campaigns, social media, and networking events.
  * Qualify and nurture leads through consistent follow-ups and tailored communication to move prospects through the sales funnel.
  * Collaborate closely with the sales and marketing teams to align outreach strategies and messaging.
  * Maintain and update the CRM with accurate lead and prospect information.
  * Conduct market research to identify potential clients and industry trends relevant to custom software services.
  * Schedule and coordinate meetings between prospects and senior sales or technical teams.
  * Prepare and deliver compelling presentations and proposals to potential clients when required.

### What Are We Looking for?

  * Proven experience in business development, lead generation, or sales, preferably in the IT or custom software services sector.
  * Strong communication and negotiation skills with a customer-focused attitude.
  * Proven ability to generate and nurture leads, managing multiple prospects effectively.
  * Familiarity with CRM tools such as Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho.
  * Self-motivated, goal-oriented, and comfortable working in a fast-paced, target-driven environment.
  * Bachelor’s degree preferred; relevant certifications in sales or business development are a plus.
