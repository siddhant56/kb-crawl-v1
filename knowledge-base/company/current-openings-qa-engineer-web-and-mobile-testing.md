### How you should be?

We are seeking a **QA Engineer** with **end-to-end quality ownership** for web and mobile applications. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with **Playwright using TypeScript** , strong manual testing skills, and the ability to collaborate closely with **UK-based clients** in a high-delivery environment.

### What you will do?

  * Perform **functional and non-functional testing** of **web and mobile applications** using **manual and Playwright-based automation** , covering UI, regression, integration, cross-browser, and responsive testing.
  * Analyse business and technical requirements and prepare **test plans, test cases, test scenarios, and test data**.
  * Design, develop, execute, and maintain **automation test scripts using Playwright with TypeScript**.
  * Perform **mobile testing** using **Playwright and manual testing techniques**.
  * Conduct **API testing** for REST services using **Postman/Swagger**.
  * Execute **accessibility testing** to ensure compliance with accessibility standards and usability guidelines.
  * Apply **AI-based testing approaches** to improve automation stability, execution efficiency, and test coverage.
  * Log, track, and verify defects using **JIRA/Azure DevOps** , ensuring clear root-cause analysis and closure.
  * Prepare and maintain **QA documentation** , including **test plans, test execution reports, defect reports, and release status reports**.
  * Provide **periodic QA status reports** to stakeholders and clients.
  * Work **independently or as part of a team** , taking **full ownership and accountability** for assigned modules and deliverables.
  * Collaborate closely with developers, product teams, and **clients** , with **daily client interaction** and clear, professional communication.
  * Support **release activities** , including smoke testing, regression validation, and **QA sign-off**.
  * Participate actively in **Agile/Scrum ceremonies** and contribute to continuous quality improvement.

### What we are looking for?

  * **Hands-on Playwright automation with TypeScript (mandatory).**
  * Solid experience in **manual testing** of **functional and non-functional requirements**.
  * Experience with **AI-based testing tools or techniques** (self-healing automation, test optimization, analytics).
  * Good knowledge of **API testing (REST APIs)**.
  * Strong understanding of **test design, execution, and defect management**.
  * Experience working with **Git and CI/CD pipelines**.
  * Excellent **client communication skills** , with experience in **daily client interactions**.
  * Strong analytical, documentation, and problem-solving skills.
  * Comfortable working in **UK shift (2:00 PM – 11:00 PM IST)**.

**Certifications:**

  * **ISTQB Certification** – Mandatory
  * **Automation testing–related certification** (Playwright, Selenium, or equivalent) – Mandatory
  * **ISTQB Test Automation Engineer** – Good to have
