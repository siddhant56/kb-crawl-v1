## What Makes Ekyarth the Most Sought-after Space?

Ekyarth is the foundational gem you’ll want your software partner to have - secured servers, data security systems, adaptive architectures and the best of amenities.

### The Foyer

Step into our building and you’ll be greeted at our reception area – a warm, inviting space that speaks of our hospitality. We believe in minimalist comfort and the décor embodies the same taste - designed to leave a firm, lasting impression on every visitor.

### The Work Floors

This is where our 650+ brilliant minds gather to deliver game changing solutions for your business. We prioritize their comfort and ease with ergonomical seating arrangements, modern devices and highly enabled systems to enhance productive time.

### Our Meeting Zones

Fascinating names like ‘Thoughtpod’ and ‘Sapphire’ will lead you to our focussed creative and collaboration zones. As a global outsourcing firm with most of our clients based overseas, we’ve facilitated our meeting and conference rooms with latest devices and secure data-sharing systems.

### Creative Walls/Corridors

We’ve turned our most frequented areas into inspirational zones. It’s not just about décor, Ekyarth is a whole vibe in its own. Our corridors and walls are inscribed with graffities and quotes that lift up mood, render minimalist aesthetics and serve the right dose of inspiration.

### The C Suite Cabins

Dedicated solo spaces for our higher ups and people leaders that reflect their personality and sense of style – the C suit cabins are carefully designed to leave an impression. Minimalist, humble designs packed with carefully planned facilities – a hub that enables determined decisioning and action drives.

### Our Cafeteria

Food is our love language; it’s the way we care. Ekyarth houses an enormous cafeteria that seats 200+ individuals at one go, offers a wide variety of culinary options with a prominent and professional catering team and hinges on maintaining thorough hygiene for our employees.

### Our Relaxation Zones

If you don’t find us at our desk, we’re most probably at this zone, unwinding ourselves in between work and deadlines. From workout equipment to proper sports corners – end your day with a workout or play a hand during a break. A coveted space built to help our people relax.
