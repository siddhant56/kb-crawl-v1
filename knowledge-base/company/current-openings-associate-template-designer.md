### How you should be?

As a Template Designer you’ll be constructing and displaying creative ideas based on industry trends.

### What you will do?

  * Create fresh, high-quality, and trendy design templates.
  * Draw design assets to use them on the templates, as required.
  * Producing and presenting creative ideas and visuals /posters/templates based on industry trends and company requirements.
  * Developing illustrations, logos, graphics, layouts, and other designs using various designing software.
  * Communicating with team members to ensure project goals and milestones are met.

### What we are looking for?

  * Prior experience as a graphic designer.
  * Must have an online portfolio of graphic design.
  * Excellent knowledge of design elements, content, typography, and design layout.
  * Ability to create high-quality, trendy templates on deadline.
  * Experience with Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop.

**Skills:** \- Template Design, logos, graphics, layouts
