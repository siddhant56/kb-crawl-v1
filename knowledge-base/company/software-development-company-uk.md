## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you approach software development projects for UK-based businesses?

Our approach for custom software development in the UK is grounded in strategic alignment first. Once you reach out to us, we work to understand your regulatory landscape (especially GDPR and ISO compliance), business goals, and operational nuances specific to the UK market. We then build a delivery model around that.

### How involved will we be during development?

We offer structured visibility without burdening your internal teams. You’ll have access to sprint reviews, weekly touchpoints, and a shared backlog but you won’t need to be micromanaging. Many UK clients appreciate our hybrid collaboration model. We can give you full decision-making transparency but save you from day-to-day execution noise. If you're hands-on, we accommodate that too. Either way, we have a feedback loop that makes your input actionable without causing fatigue.

### What does your typical project timeline look like?

Timelines vary by complexity and scope, but we avoid the “it depends” ambiguity. For example, we deliver MVPs in 8–12 weeks with clear milestone-based outcomes. Enterprise-grade systems typically unfold over 4–6 months, including architecture validation and integration planning. We’re known for our mature estimation models, strong backlog grooming, and engineering discipline. You’ll know what to expect, and when.

### Can you work with our in-house team or existing systems?

This is where we thrive as a UK software development company. Over 60% of our UK engagements involve co-development with client teams or working within hybrid infrastructures. We’re comfortable navigating partial documentation, fragmented systems, or in-progress codebases. Whether it’s aligning with your DevOps pipeline or plugging into your security protocols, we adapt. And if your internal team needs upskilling, we train while we build.

### Can we meet your team in the UK or schedule in-person workshops?

Yes, and we encourage it. We’ve run discovery sessions and architecture workshops on-site in London, Manchester, Leeds, and Edinburgh. Some of our clients prefer to kick things off face-to-face and then shift to remote model. We’re structured to accommodate both. For critical decisions, design validations, or high-stakes integration planning, we show up when it matters.

### How do you handle bugs or unexpected issues after delivery?

All our deliveries come with structured support SLAs based on urgency and business impact. We also offer post-launch stabilisation windows, so you're never left holding a product that’s “done” but not ready. Our proactive monitoring and regression testing pipelines are built to catch what manual QA might miss. And if issues arise, you won’t go through generic ticketing loops; you’ll deal directly with engineers who know your system.
