### How you should be?

A passionate **Senior Python & Generative AI Engineer** who thrives at the intersection of software engineering and AI innovation. You have strong expertise in **Python (FastAPI/Django)** and experience designing production-grade systems that integrate **LLMs, RAG pipelines, and autonomous agent workflows**. You are naturally curious, continuously exploring new AI frameworks and tools such as **LangChain, LlamaIndex, and Semantic Kernel** , and can translate complex technical requirements into scalable, reliable, and explainable systems.

### What you will do?

  * **Design and implement LLM-powered systems** leveraging OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, and other open-source models to solve complex enterprise problems.
  * **Build multi-step reasoning pipelines** including planner, executor, and validator components to enable reliable AI decision-making.
  * **Integrate deterministic reasoning engines** alongside AI outputs to enforce business rules, ensure compliance, and handle edge cases.
  * **Architect and optimize RAG pipelines** with intelligent document ingestion, metadata tagging, embeddings, vector search, and hierarchical retrieval strategies.
  * **Develop autonomous and negotiation agents** capable of goal-driven planning, tool utilization, memory management, self-evaluation, and strategy execution.
  * **Build high-performance APIs and asynchronous workflows** to connect AI models, databases, vector stores, and external systems efficiently.
  * **Ensure production readiness** by implementing CI/CD pipelines, monitoring system health, latency, retrieval accuracy, and agent behavior.
  * **Maintain traceability and explainability** of AI outputs, providing transparent decision paths that are auditable and suitable for enterprise environments.
  * **Document designs and best practices** , and actively contribute to knowledge-sharing within the team to elevate overall technical capability.

### What we are looking for?

  * **4–6 years of professional experience** in Python backend development and AI systems engineering.
  * Qualification – **BE/B.Tech/ME/M.Tech/MSc-IT/MCA** or equivalent technical degree.
  * **Strong proficiency with FastAPI/Django** and RESTful API development for scalable, maintainable services.
  * **Hands-on experience with LLM frameworks** such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, and understanding of RAG architectures.
  * Experience in **designing deterministic reasoning engines** alongside probabilistic AI outputs.
  * **Proficiency with vector databases** (Pinecone, FAISS, Chroma, Weaviate) for retrieval-augmented generation pipelines.
  * Familiarity with **containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)** , version control (Git), and **CI/CD pipelines** for production-grade deployments.
  * Strong **debugging, problem-solving, and collaborative communication skills**.
  * Passion for **AI, multi-step reasoning, autonomous agents, and building reliable, explainable systems**.
