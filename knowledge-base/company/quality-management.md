## What’s at the Core of Our Software Quality?

### Usability

To indicate the nimbleness of the solution, we focus on user adoption rates, duration of task performance, number of tasks completed in a time period, number of errors and completion rates.

### Availability

To measure the ratio of correct system performance, we account for mean time between failures, mean time for detection, mean time for recovery and failure, number of failure requests, and rate of fault occurrence.

### Performance

To weigh a solution’s abilities in meeting action time requirements, we consider latency, throughput, capacity and spare capacity, screen refresh times and ability to accommodate additional resources.

### Security

To measure the solution’s ability to safeguard data and intelligence from both internal and external vulnerabilities, we check for security incidents, data encryption efficiencies, and vulnerabilities found through regular and independent PEN tests.

### Code Quality

To indicate reliability and expertise of software solutions source code, we check the maintainability index (MI), cyclomatic complexity (CC). depth of inheritance, class coupling and Halstead volume as metrics.
