### How you should be?

A passionate and experienced **DevOps Engineer** with expertise in **cloud platforms, CI/CD automation, and containerized environments**. You enjoy automating infrastructure, improving deployment efficiency, and building scalable, secure, and reliable systems while collaborating effectively in a fast-paced environment.

### What you will do?

  * Design, implement, and maintain robust CI/CD pipelines to streamline build, deployment, and release processes
  * Automate infrastructure provisioning, deployment workflows, and operational tasks
  * Work extensively with cloud platforms such as Azure and AWS to build scalable and secure infrastructure solutions
  * Manage and optimize containerized environments using Docker and Kubernetes
  * Develop and maintain automation scripts using PowerShell, Python, or Bash
  * Ensure infrastructure reliability through proper monitoring, troubleshooting, performance optimization, and security practices
  * Collaborate closely with development, QA, and operations teams to improve release efficiency and deployment quality
  * Contribute to infrastructure standardization, DevOps best practices, and DevSecOps initiatives
  * Manage source control, branching, and deployment workflows using Git-based version control systems

### Who Are We Looking for?

  * 5+ years of experience as a **DevOps Engineer or similar role**
  * Strong hands-on experience with **Azure and AWS cloud platforms**
  * Experience with **CI/CD** tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, GitHub Actions, etc.
  * Azure certifications such as **AZ-400, AZ-204 (Mandatory)**
  * Good understanding of **Docker, Kubernetes, and container orchestration**
  * Proficiency in scripting using **PowerShell, Python, or Bash**
  * Strong understanding of **Git, infrastructure automation, and cloud deployment practices**
  * Strong problem-solving, troubleshooting, and collaboration skills

**Good to have:**

  * Kubernetes certifications (CKA or similar)
  * Experience with **DevSecOps practices**
