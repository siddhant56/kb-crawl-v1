### How You Should Be?

We are looking for a skillful social media executive who manages all social media channels and campaigns to sustain an engaging audience experience and to enhance viewers’ satisfaction results.

### What You Will Do?

  * Create engaging social media content including posts, reels, stories, short-form videos, and captions
  * Plan and manage content calendars across multiple platforms
  * Handle and grow social media presence on Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, Facebook, and other platforms
  * Research latest trends, hashtags, audience behavior, and content formats
  * Edit photos and videos optimized for different social media channels
  * Monitor comments, messages, and audience engagement regularly
  * Track content performance using analytics and insights tools
  * Collaborate with designers, marketing teams, and stakeholders for campaign execution

### What we are looking For?

  * Experience range: 0-2 years
  * Strong creativity, storytelling, and content ideation skills
  * Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  * Comfortable appearing on camera for reels, interviews, and short videos
  * Basic knowledge of graphic design and video editing tools like Canva, CapCut, Inshots, or similar platforms
  * Good understanding of social media algorithms, trends, and platform best practices
  * Strong time management skills with consistency in content execution
  * Ability to analyze performance metrics and optimize content accordingly
  * Quick learner with adaptability to new tools, formats, and platforms
  * Prior experience in content creation or social media management will be an added advantage
