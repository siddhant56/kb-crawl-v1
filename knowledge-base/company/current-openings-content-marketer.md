### How you should be?

We’re looking for a curious, proactive Content Marketer who understands that the markets change fast and can make sure we keep up with those changes. If you naturally observe trends, ask “why,” and enjoy turning insights into meaningful marketing, this role is for you.

This is a hands-on opportunity to learn, experiment, and grow within a structured marketing team that values thinking over just execution.

### What you will do?

  * Research industry trends, competitors, and changing market narratives
  * Support creation of blogs, landing pages, social posts, and marketing assets
  * Assist in shaping messaging aligned with brand positioning
  * Work closely with SEO, design, and strategy teams
  * Track content performance and suggest improvements
  * Proactively bring ideas based on market shifts and audience behavior

### What we are looking for?

  * 0–1 years of experience in content, marketing, or communications
  * Strong writing and editing skills
  * Awareness of industry trends (AI, software, SaaS, digital transformation, etc.)
  * Ability to think beyond briefs and contribute ideas
  * Eagerness to learn SEO, positioning, and brand-led marketing
  * Self-driven and comfortable taking ownership

### Why Join Us?

  * Real exposure to strategic content marketing (not just writing!)
  * Mentorship from senior marketing leaders
  * Opportunity to work on evolving tech narratives like AI and digital transformation
  * A culture that values clarity, thinking, and long-term brand building
  * Clear scope for skill development and career growth
