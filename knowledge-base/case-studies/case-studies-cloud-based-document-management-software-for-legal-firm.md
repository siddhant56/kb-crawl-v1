## Company Background

The client has a distinguished reputation in corporate law and compliance management. The legal team works with local as well as multinational companies and their expertise areas include regulatory compliance, contract negotiation, legal counseling, and more.

## The Problem

While attending a tech conference on how legal firms are increasingly becoming paperless, the COO of the company gained insights into the new-age trend of document management on the cloud. That particular event served as a stark realization of the inefficiencies within the firm and the urgent need for a more streamlined approach. So, they collectively decided to invest in document management software (DMS) to address the existing challenges and time wastage associated with the manual storage, searching, and retrieval process.

## Standalone Features

We integrated a number of features in the DMS, each with its specific functionality to streamline the overall document storing, searching, and access process.

### Full-Text Search

The system supports quick full-text search across vast document repositories. Advanced indexing and tokenization ensure high precision and recall in retrieving relevant legal information.

### Document Check In/Out

Users can check out and edit any document. Git branching mechanisms enable parallel editing, and check-in functionalities provide a systematic audit trail of document revisions.

### Document Profiling/Meta Data

The system utilizes metadata tagging and profiling through Azure Cognitive Services. It automatically extracts and categorizes metadata, which, in turn, enables users to classify documents based on content.

### Unique Documents ID

In the DMS, each document gets assigned a unique identifier. This ensures global uniqueness and provides a robust foundation for traceability, audit trails, and linking documents across various legal processes.

### Microsoft Office Integration

Integration with Microsoft Office 365 API facilitates real-time collaboration and editing. Features such as co-authoring in Word and Excel streamline collaborative document creation and editing.

### Emails Management

Leveraging Microsoft Exchange Web Services (EWS), the system seamlessly integrates with email platforms for archiving, indexing, and retrieval of emails directly within the DSM.
