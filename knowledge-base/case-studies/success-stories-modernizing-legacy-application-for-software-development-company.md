## Here's a brief of the conversation:

### Please tell us about your organization and your position

We are a software development company, and we only cater to the recruitment industry: human resources and payroll. We have our own software product, and we offer consulting services to recruitment and HR industries. In terms of size, we are about 60 people. Our head office is in Melbourne, Australia, and we have a sales office in Sydney, but most of our operations are located in Melbourne.

I work with the organization as the product lead. I am responsible for building and maintaining our systems. I work extensively with our clients and our CTO to build the roadmap of the product.

Radixweb did a fantastic job to make sure that it was done in the right way. It has been a great experience, a journey that I had never done before personally or as a company.

### Do you think Radixweb has used the most suitable technologies for your project?

Yes, of course. There is absolutely no doubt in our mind. We have selected the right software, the right technology, and the right partner. Before executing this project, we had a good idea that we wanted to build a browser-based system, and we were already beaten up by the whole bandwagon from Microsoft Silverlight. We discussed our issues with Radixweb to determine the best technology to rebuild our application. After a lot of consultation, we decided to rebuild using HTML5 with an AngularJS Framework for the frontend and web APIs and Microsoft SQL Server Database for the backend as our technology stack.

One thing I would say to anyone who likes to work with Radixweb, there are absolutely no doubts that they are fantastic guys. But, just like any outsourcing project, you will need someone to work very closely with Radixweb. This is true of any service provider, and it's fairly important that the goals are outlined very clearly.
