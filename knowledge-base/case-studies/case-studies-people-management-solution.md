# People Management Solution

Building the Most Valuable Asset of Any Company- Its People with Elegant Web-Based HRMS Solution to Seamlessly Manage Entire Life-Cycle of an Employee starting from Recruitment to Exit Process

  * Comprehensive and secure centralized HR data of globally spread employees with fluid access and retrieval without any kind of hassle
  * Attract and retain the right skilled employees with exquisite Talent Management process for better growth of the organization
  * Ease the struggles related to payroll, benefits, onboarding-off boarding, reviews and applicant tracking systems
  * Enhance employee-related policies and make wiser decisions with smart web-based HRMS, powered by big data solutions that eliminates routine paperwork.

## The Solution

Carving perfect solutions out of ideas duly reflects Radixweb’s core competence. Owing to the specifications raised by the client, our team relentlessly put the best of efforts and right attitude to devise a robust people management solution by following the below mentioned attributes:

### Technology Consulting

The team conducted thorough analysis of the requirements and the domain. Also, reviewed technology options that offer the desired advantages, curb limitations, extensibility and scalability. To conform to the needs, helped the client to choose the right technology mix.

### Custom Application Development

The client wanted a customized solution that makes recruitment information of prime importance to its diverse clients with the ease of availability. This cost effective solution also had relevant information access management in place.

### Application Integration Services

The team has devised the solution with seamless integration of Third party tools and services. Telerik controls were used to facilitate an engaging and better UI while Fastlane API were used to store user data in a central database safely.

### Testing and Quality Assurance

Following a strict work-ethic, our team adheres to a stricter high standard of overall application quality. Proficient in various forms of testing, the team verified the equivocacy of the application with Unit, Regression, Load and more testing approaches.

### Application Support & Maintenance

A fully dedicated team was assigned to handle fruitfully the configuration and deployment for end-users. In addition to this, the team from Radix worked on enhancements and fixed bugs reported by the client in the later phases.

### Data Analytics & BI

The solution was enhanced with analytics and reporting capabilities to provide actionable insights into workforce data, recruitment trends, and employee performance. This enabled stakeholders to make informed decisions, track KPIs, and improve overall HR efficiency.

## The Results

With Radixweb’s solution, the client enjoyed all the essentials of recruitment management system, performance management systems and talent management process accumulated in one people management solution. Owing the super functionality, there was a stupendous rise in the client’s growth and customer satisfaction.
