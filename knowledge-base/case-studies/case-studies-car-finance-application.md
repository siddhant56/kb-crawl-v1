# CarCapital: A Resilient Web Application for Car Finance Services

A car finance and insurance platform developed using NextJS and Node.JS (Fastify), integrated with multiple lending partners to provide users with personalized finance options tailored to their needs.

  * Smart car finance matching technology designed to help users compare and apply for car finances in a simplified and transparent manner.
  * API integration with a range of third-party services, including AWS S3, google maps, sentry, Bitly API etc.
  * End-to end security measures, such as authentication with a JWT token.
  * Customized fields with proper validations and integration of the agent user flow with existing user flow.

## Client Benefits

### Increased Efficiency

With CarCapital, the client can automate several manual processes, such as document management, affordability checking, etc., and reduce the time and effort required to process and manage car loans more quickly.

### Better Risk Management

Using different tools to evaluate borrower creditworthiness and identify potential risks associated with loans, the client is able to make more informed decisions about lending and reduce the risk of fraud.

### Enhanced Customer Experience

By providing borrowers with a user-friendly platform to apply for car loans, track their status, and make payments, the web app empowers our client to improve customer satisfaction and get positive reviews.
