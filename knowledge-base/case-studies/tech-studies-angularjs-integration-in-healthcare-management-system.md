# Transforming Healthcare Management with AngularJS Framework

This tech study examines how a healthcare management company successfully implemented AngularJS technology to modernize its healthcare management platform, enhance user experience, and streamline complex data interactions.

Our Europe-based client is one of the renowned and leading providers of Healthcare Management Services. To become competitive in this digital era, they wanted to design a website and provide remote healthcare services using modern technologies.

They wanted to create a system that could be able to facilitate various complex challenges of the healthcare sector and provide seamless user experience along with healthcare facilities.

The client approached Radixweb to develop a cloud-based healthcare management system for both web and mobile-based platforms, where patients can easily book appointments with their respective healthcare professionals. Moreover, the system could focus on precise diagnosis, medications, consultations, booking, and organized health record management.

## Considering Platform Independent Framework - AngularJS

When our development team understood the entire project scenario and market requirements, we wanted to use a technology that could clearly separate concerns about the front-end and back-end.

A back-end solution, for instance, might be applied to desktop, mobile, and web interfaces. Numerous back-end technologies can work with a front-end solution. It's a more lightweight substitute for Web Services (SOAP, WSDL, and others) and RPC (Remote Procedure Calls). It is built around a client-server, stateless coprocessor that is cacheable.

And I guess none other than AngularJS framework could be the right choice to build a healthcare management system for our client. The framework dependency is an essential feature of AngularJS.

Hence, considering AngularJS for front end development was our primary decision. On the other hand, many giant applications like Netflix, Gmail, Amazon, Snapchat, and PayPal leverage amazing features of Angular.js development.

While talking about front-end JavaScript framework – AngularJS has been instrumental in reshaping various industries by facilitating dynamic and user-centric web application development. AngularJS was chosen for its ability to create a responsive and interactive user interface that enhances the overall user experience.

## Implementation of AngularJS by Our Team

As a renowned AngularJS development company, we designed a user-friendly healthcare system that can meet each requirement. The basic front-end features of the system include:

  * Patient Dashboard
  * Appointments Booking
  * Consultation with Doctors
  * List of Doctors

Despite the complexity of the MEAN backend development process, our developers were able to create an app with the help of AngularJS, Java, .NET, and Node.JS.

### Dynamic Patient Dashboard

Utilizing AngularJS, we developed a dynamic patient dashboard that provides patients with easy access to their medical records, appointment history, and test results. The AngularJS framework’s data binding ensures that changes in patient data are reflected in real time, enhancing patient engagement.

### Appointment Scheduling

Our team of AngularJS developers leveraged various features of AngularJS in order to create an intuitive appointment scheduling module. That helped patients to view available timing slots, choose a convenient time, and book appointments seamlessly.

Moreover, we enabled the template capabilities of AngularJS to facilitate the display of dynamic appointment information.

### Real-Time Communication

To enable real-time communication between patients and medical professionals, we facilitated AngularJS’s two-way data binding feature. This allows patients to send messages, queries, and receive responses instantly. This becomes the most important feature in custom healthcare management software to foster doctor-patient interaction.

## Delivering Native-App-Like Experience with SPA

Undoubtedly, usability stands as a paramount concern in every project, significantly impacting how favorably the system is assessed by its users.

Single Page Applications enable us to provide users with an experience akin to native applications. These apps excel in their capacity to refresh specific segments of the user interface without the need for server roundtrips to fetch HTML content.

Previously, this approach posed substantial challenges, which led to boiling down to code smell architecture. Well, those days are gone. And thanks to AngularJS.

In fact, many MVC JavaScript frameworks are developed based on this concept.

## Developed Front-End Independently

Embarking on the development of an application rooted in the concept above prompted us to consider a clear separation between the front-end and back-end aspects.

This ultimately helped us to achieve our goal of growing independently and performing in different contexts. For instance, back-end development solutions can be used for mobile, desktop, and web interfaces. And front-end development solutions can work with different back-end frameworks and technologies.

Leveraging Angular development enables the creation of the front-end independently from the back-end, following the MVC (Model View Controller) concept. This proved advantageous, facilitating rapid development without sacrificing precision or user-friendliness. This segregation of tasks allows for time-efficient development while also fostering enhanced consistency across teams as they collaboratively focus on their specific components.

However, we chose REST API to create communication between the front-end and back-end of the healthcare API. Instead of other APIs like RPC (Remote Procedure Calls) and Web Services (SOAP, WSDL, and others), our experienced developers selected REST due to its lightweight feature. It is based on a client-server, stateless, cacheable communication mechanism, almost always the HTTP protocol.

## Modularization

Within the AngularJS framework, modularization entails structuring components based on functionality instead of type.

Here, our components, like filters, controllers, and directives, are organized into distinct modules.

This essentially creates separate "apps" within our application with their independent nature. They also have their own objects.

This methodology ensures the promotion of reusability, a well-defined separation of concerns and testability to our client application.

## Lessons Learned from AngularJS

While developing healthcare management software for our clients, we learned several key lessons from Angular JS.

Firstly, AngularJS provides a robust and efficient framework for building dynamic and responsive web applications. Its extensive features, such as directives, data binding, and dependency injection, enable developers to create highly interactive and user-friendly interfaces.

Secondly, AngularJS offers various tools and mechanisms for streamlining data management processes, improving efficiency, and ensuring data integrity. The use of form validation, error handling, and centralized data management features simplifies complex data entry tasks and enhances overall productivity.

Lastly, AngularJS's real-time capabilities, such as two-way data binding and web socket integration, enable the implementation of real-time updates, providing users with up-to-date information and improving their overall experience.
