### Project Summary

Reenter Enterprises, a prestigious client, based in the USA and Europe, wanted to streamline its business operations and automate workflows by implementing DevOps. Instead of adhering to the traditional approach, Reenter Enterprises leveraged Radixweb’s DevOps capabilities. Certified DevOps architects from Radixweb helped the management team from Reenter Enterprises realize the benefits of a shorter development lifecycle, faster response time, and a more collaborative work environment in the organization.

### Final Results

  * **65% reduced** deployment time – faster time to market
  * **38%** reduced infrastructure costs
  * **45 to 50% less time** in CI/CD pipeline, getting to a one-click deployment
  * **Secure** cloud architecture
  * **500 million database** records migration
