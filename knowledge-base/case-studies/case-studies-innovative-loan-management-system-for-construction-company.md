## Project Brief

The project was about creating a cloud-based software system integrated with state-of-the-art features that would be accessible through web and mobile devices. It offers lenders real-time visibility into ongoing construction projects, powered by independent fee inspectors, construction fund management tools, and risk assessment services. Stakeholders should be able to monitor the process, manage documents, and make informed decisions, among others.

## Client Background

The client, a Los Angeles-based direct private lender, used to find it incredibly difficult to manage construction projects funded through their business. So, they needed to build a sophisticated loan management software system that can aggregate and analyze complex construction project data in real time for acute fund and risk assessments.

## User Segments

In developing our construction loan management platform, our FinTech developers assessed the diverse stakeholders involved in the construction project and finance management lifecycle. Let's understand how we designed the software to address the distinct needs of each user profile:

### Borrowers

The software provides borrowers with a user-friendly dashboard to track application status and fund disbursement. They can also upload project documents and ensure compliance with the lender’s requirements.

### Banks

For banking institutions or lenders, the platform offers a range of functionalities, including risk management and monitoring, automated reporting and compliance, and optimized disbursement processes.

### Managers

Stakeholders who monitor internal and external operations are benefiting from automated workflows and a customizable dashboard presenting KPIs, financial data, and project progress. They can also track communication logs and resolve queries.
