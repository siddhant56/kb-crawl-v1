# Brainlax - Mobile App Development

A feature-rich mobile health application that teaches methods to enhance overall well-being and optimize work performance, with AI integration enabling personalized guidance and adaptive user experiences.

  * Seamless mobile application with easy-to-use navigations to guide its user from one step to other
  * Registration of users is quick, and a full-time support executive is also provided for any query
  * The feedback form is automatically generated once the users finish all the sessions, leveraging AI integration to tailor follow-up insights
  * Enables the recipients and the teacher to have flawless communications with advanced bandwidth

## What We Did?

### Define User Personas

After conducting interviews, user personas and empathy maps were created. Doing these activities, we had a better understanding of the mobile app users’ pain points and their goals and how our app should give a solution for these, supported by AI integration for refined user insights.

### Notifications

Receive instant notifications for any essential reminders, events, gym schedules, stock updates, and much more. Client can even customize notifications (i.e. can schedule the timing of the notification as per need).

### Reminders

Reminders are always at the top of the list. Users can also receive push notifications to remind them of key events. Once the user has watched a video session, a reminder of the next part is sent, enhanced by AI integration to prioritize timely and relevant reminders.

### Daily Insights

The user can get a fast overview of each crucial vital and activity on the landing screen. Users can even create a note list of their essential points in the video session by teachers, with AI integration enabling smarter summaries and highlights.

## Clients Achievement

Because of the smooth and easy-to-use UI, the application gained popularity and many downloads. Our support team is still assisting the client in optimizing the user experience for the continued success of the application, with AI integration supporting ongoing UX optimization and personalization.
