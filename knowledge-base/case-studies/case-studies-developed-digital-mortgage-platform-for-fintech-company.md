## Core Features

We wanted to ensure that the online mortgage platform delivers a high-quality experience for both borrowers and lending service providers. So, we equipped the product with a suite of modern features powered by a blend of innovative technologies.

### Mortgage Calculator

Our dedicated developers integrated a mortgage calculator that allows borrowers to perform complex financial calculations, estimated loan repayment schedules, interest rates, and loan terms based on personalized inputs.

### Automatic Document Verification

We utilized OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology to automate the lengthy document verification process to significantly reduce manual labor and speed up the verification process.

### Real-Time Application Status

By establishing real-time communication between users and automated notification systems, we enabled the app to provide consistent and timely updates on loan status, document submissions, and approvals.

### Record Keeping

The integration of blockchain technology into the mortgage system resulted in immutable and transparent transaction records. This ensures data integrity and enhances trust between lenders and borrowers.

### Risk Assessment and Credit Scoring

With the help of machine learning algorithms and third-party APIs, the system can assess potential risks and credit scores to empower the lender to make informed decisions quickly and accurately.
