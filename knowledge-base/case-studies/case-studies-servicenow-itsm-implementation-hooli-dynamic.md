### Project Summary

Hooli Dynamic is one of the emerging full-service brokerage service providers in the US, providing business insurance and retirement services to organizations and individuals across the country. And one of the biggest issues this firm was dealing with was a tedious and disorganized helpdesk system. As a result, they found a low-value proposition in customer IT branding. Radixweb took up the challenge by shedding some light on the ServiceNow ITSM tool as the organization finally decided to integrate the existing systems with ServiceNow Asset Management Module.

### Final Results

  * **62% of cost savings** with final outcomes
  * **73% of reduced** resolution time and backtracking
  * **580 work hours** saved per month
  * Easy **dashboard** and **report** generation
  * Change management implementation for **SLAs**
  * Implementation of **IT Service Desk**
