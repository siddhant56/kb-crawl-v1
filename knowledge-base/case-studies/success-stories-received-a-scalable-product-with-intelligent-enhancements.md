# Expert Dev Team Made Our Complex Environmental Software Requirement Look Easy

Simplified complex project needs for seamless execution

Hi, I am Michael, the CEO of an environmental software company based in Katy, Texas. Our association with Radixweb began in 2016 and continued till 2017. We were a startup then; the development team of Radixweb has built our principal product – a neo-modern system that monitors emissions from big industrial facilities through algorithms and operating conditions from central control systems.

## Highlights

When you work with a company on the other side of the globe, you have to find someone who is trustworthy; Radixweb is.

They did an excellent job and gave us exactly what we hoped for. It fulfills all our requirements, is easy to install, highly technical, and very accurate.

Customer-centric, Radix goes the extra mile to ensure success. The software fulfills all our requirements. Its efficacy is based largely on their contributions. It helps us know the environmental demands that large corporations have. Our clients are very happy, and it’s doing precisely what we envisioned.

## A set of complex requirements

This Texas based environmental software company deals with an algorithm to track air emissions. As a startup, they needed a software solution that constantly tracked air emissions for industrial facilities with smokestacks.

Radixweb’s environmental software allowed the firm to gain access to the central control systems of industrial facilities, download all relevant information about greenhouse gasses and hazardous pollutants.

They’re responsible for the building the product we sell. Everything we have today is thanks to what they’ve built.
