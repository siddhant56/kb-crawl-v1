## About the Client

Riley LLC. is a well-known organization in the United States specializing in the HVACR industry. HVACR stands for heating, ventilating, air conditioning, and refrigeration. This client conducts research, distributes important information, and provides on-demand services about these systems, such as consultation, installations, maintenance, repair, etc.

## Project Vision

The company wanted to make it easier for customers to buy their services and learn more about HVACR systems. The best way to do that is by building an eCommerce site and connecting it with a learning management system (LMS) so that there’s a one-stop platform for every need. This would allow customers to explore their services online while conveniently accessing educational resources at any given time.

## Top Features

The new LMS with eCommerce has been efficiently working for customers and learners alike. By integrating advanced technical features, this platform ensures that users can easily access, purchase, and engage with a wide range of products and educational materials. Here are the top features that highlight the platform's capabilities and benefits:

### Single Sign-On (SSO)

This mechanism will enable users to log in once to access both the eCommerce site and the learning management system without needing separate usernames and passwords.

### Automated Service Management

The system automatically tracks and updates service progress and levels. As a result, customers get accurate availability information and up to date insights.

### Customer Reviews and Ratings

Users can leave reviews and ratings on products and learning materials. The client can screen and approve the reviews for appropriate content and spam before publication.

### Subscription Options

The platform offers subscription plans for ongoing access to educational content and special services. The software also includes access control mechanisms that restrict content and service visibility based on subscription status.

### Live Chat Support

Customers and learners can get real-time assistance through a live chat feature. AI-powered chatbots can handle common inquiries and provide automated responses.

### Customizable Themes and Layouts

An intuitive theme editor enables real-time customization of the website’s visual elements, including colors, fonts, and styles, without requiring advanced coding skills. This WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get) interface makes it easy to see changes as the client makes.

### Advanced Search Filters

Users can use detailed filters to narrow down search results by categories such as price, rating, difficulty level, or availability. As users type in the search bar, the software offers autocomplete suggestions.
