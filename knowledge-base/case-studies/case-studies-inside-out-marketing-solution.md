## What solution Radixweb proposed?

Being certified Bronze Kentico Partners, Radixweb team crafted their best with Kentico 9 features perfectly in place. With an extensively skilled team and a vision of setting up a valuable online store – Radixweb created a custom marketing ready website with high-end UI-UX and unique features as the core of the site:

### Product Features

Syncing well with the requirements portrayed by the client, a simple static website with unusual features for online marketing and email campaigning was created. Statistics showing page visits by users was also provided to the admin.

### Highly Customized Modules

Advanced features that were specially built for the system were online marketing for handling mass e-mailing, banner management, newsletter features using Kentico 9 functionalities. Seamless content management systems for refilling forms.

### Administrator Back Panel

Admin is catered with a number of rules in Kentico. Using those, the administrator could keep a check over the details added by the registered users and that were used for improvising the website performance. Ultimately, the site branding peaked.
