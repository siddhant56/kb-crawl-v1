## Approach for developing HR Management Software

From migrating the legacy architecture to modernized one and skillfully handling all the prevalent challenges on the road, Radixweb devised a one-of-its-kind HR and Recruitment solution for the client.

### Technology Consulting

Our team of experts referred to get .NET application as the best suited technology and also suggested viable options to move the system to cloud.

### Prototype Development

To portray the client’s expectations in the right way, our team created a non-functional HTML5 prototype for the designs provided.

### Custom Application Development

We designed a new architecture and database schema backed by Big Data Engineering principles to efficiently manage large volumes of candidate and employee data while maintaining performance and scalability.

### Testing and Quality Assurance

For performance and quality of code testing, the client hired a different team to certify the product for matching high standards. It couldn’t be hacked to access any user data by a third-party security testing team. Radixweb team duly maintained high-quality standards for the entire human resource software.

### Support & Maintenance

During the development process, a licensed tool- Stackify was used to get in-depth look about server performance and maintenance notifications. To smoothen the process, weekly status reports were shared and onsite visits for training and support were conducted.

### Application Integration Services

Radix developed plugins for specific modules that can be easily integrated with other systems and APIs to integrate the client’s product were built.

## Final outcome of HR Management System

With successful migration to web-based systems, the client saved substantial amount of time and efforts through simplified product licensing, automated processes and efficient database management. Additional intuitive features and improved user interface of hr management software helped create a good brand presence among thousands of existing users. It also became a milestone to attract new customers.
