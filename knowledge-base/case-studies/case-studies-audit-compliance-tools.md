# Product Development for Audit & Compliance Processes

Delivered secure, reliable, easy-to-use, highly configurable business solution to automate traditional paper-driven audit compliance workflows, powered by Data Analytics and BI for real-time visibility, reporting accuracy, and compliance insights.

  * Interfaces to define a wide variety of Audit & Compliance processes within the system
  * Android and iOS Mobile Apps to conduct on-field audits. Offline mode to enable a seamless experience in remote locations
  * 50+ operational and analytical reports used in day-to-day reporting as well as executive analysis, enabled through Data Analytics and BI dashboards
  * Integral part of IT Systems landscape in top-notch organizations within Europe

## The Solution

### Scalable Multi-Tenant Architecture

Consultancy based approach to help managing various client expectations that help them to prioritize development. In order to provide the system to multiple businesses and users, a scalable multi-tenant architecture has been designed.

### Skilled Technical Team

Access to team of experts in design, development, training and maintenance, facilitating clear requirement understanding and customized solutions bussed on client requirements.

### SaaS/Cloud based solution

Because of the large volume and global network, SaaS/Cloud based solution was the best selection for these requirements. The solution deployed with the clear focus to cater Fortune 500 companies, leveraging fully customized features, highly scalable and readily available system.

### Two-way synchronization

Cross platform Mobile Application with offline data synchronization for auditing remote / non connected locations, enabling streamlined workflow, reduced costs and increased productivity.

### Web & Mobile Application

Intuitive web and mobile based solution leveraging mobile functionalities like Geo Location tagging of audits, Barcode scanning of locations, Signature capturing, Photo capturing of failures / non-compliances, etc. The mobile application was developed for iOS, android and windows phones where an auditor can perform an actual audit.

### Customized Features

Advanced task management modules to track and close non-compliances found during the audit. Dynamic alerting module to trigger immediate and scheduled alerts to various stake-holders for audits conducted, non-compliances, summary reports, etc.

### Quick View of Data

Advanced real-time reporting with quick views of data in graphical and statistical formats, powered by Data Analytics and BI to enable faster insights, executive reporting, and compliance-driven decision-making.

### On-going Support

On-going professional services & customization services for enterprise level end clients to incorporate their unique business needs.
