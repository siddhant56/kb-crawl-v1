## The Context

The Hygrade Business Group had a website that didn’t reflect company’s corporate identity. It was not built up using CMS so there were difficulties in managing website content. Client desired to have an enterprise CMS solution to manage massive content of his website from the back-end. As the client is in Print business, he has to mange several print products details and keep these details up-to-date to keep the customer aware about his offerings. He wanted all the advanced developement without affecting his existing system.
