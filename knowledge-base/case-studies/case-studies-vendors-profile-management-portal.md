# Vendors Profile Management Portal

Bespoke Solution for Print Industry Suppliers with Extensive Features Boosting Seamless Management That Provide Numerous Benefits of Improving Business with Safe And Secure Online Portal

  * Superior range of web-based vendor management solutions that save time with elimination of manual efforts
  * End-to-end key features and highly configurable portal for establishing an appropriate sync of roles and responsibilities of users with access management system

## The Solution

Radix has been well-versed in integrating a configurable vendor portal that will work astoundingly to bring the team together and operate smoothly. As the portal forms the core for the client’s business, it was indeed necessary to leverage the right efforts along with the right team.

### Technology Consulting

The team suggested to build a custom solution using .NET because of higher flexibility and modest technology blend including ASP.NET MVC 5 architecture with AngularJS, Web API 2.5 and Entity Framework, and Microsoft SQL Server database.

### Prototype Development

A quick walkthrough of the systems helps to eliminate doubts and improves efficiency in exact interpretation of client’s requirements and functionalities. It helps to predict how the final product will function- here we used Balsamiq tools to create mock-ups.

### Custom Application Development

The customized portal was customized with features of detailed outline, activation, reports, news and third party integrations. Search functionality, finance module to view invoices and third party online training platform integration for the portal.

### Application Integration Services

Third Party Integrations that are highly configurable for end users like to view invoices with Hansaworld accounting system to help in getting discounts, Single Sign On implementation with Freshdesk and online training platform Skyprep for users.

### Testing and Quality Assurance

Our skilled team of QA professionals are extremely proficient in Smoke Testing, Functional Testing, and Integration Testing etc. which proliferate us further to maintain high quality standards for the overall application.

### Application Support & Maintenance

For maximized relevance of the application, it requires skilled support and maintenance services. Our team provided continuous maintenance and enhancements, new features and bug fixes whenever necessary.
