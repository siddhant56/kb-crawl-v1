## Project Summary

Integration of new telehealth capabilities with the existing EHR system for improved patient experience and service delivery. For patients, it offers quick access to specialized healthcare and for medical professionals, it helps them provide informed and personalized care.

## Client Background

Leading this initiative was a Dubai-based healthcare institute that specializes in advanced diagnosis, treatment, and care for patients with health disorders. Understanding how complicated it can be for patients to access the right care, they wanted to create a unified platform with both telehealth and EHR functionalities. It would make their overall service more connected, easier to manage, and quicker for everyone involved.

## Key Functionalities

Developing a number of essential features for the EHR-integrated telehealth system was the most challenging and time-consuming part of the whole project.

### Real-Time Video Consultations

The telehealth system comes with high-quality, secure video calls that allow patients and healthcare providers to engage in real-time consultations without third-party plugins or interruptions. Optimized video streaming protocols ensure a seamless virtual clinic experience.

### Electronic Health Records Access

Through a comprehensive patient dashboard, users get access to synchronized EHR data within the telehealth portal. Authorized users gained secure access to patient records, medical histories, diagnoses, and treatment plans.

### Secure Messaging and File Sharing

We created end-to-end encrypted messaging functionality for secure communication between patients and healthcare professionals. The system offers file sharing capabilities with the transmission of different medical documents.

### Appointment Scheduling and Reminders

It's an intuitive scheduling module with calendar integration so that patients can book and manage appointments. We used calendar APIs to synchronize appointment schedules and integrated automated reminders via SMS or email to reduce no-show rates.

### Telemedicine Prescription

With this feature, healthcare providers can prescribe medications remotely following prescription guidelines. We also implemented secure authorization workflows for prescription refills and ensured patient safety and compliance with legal regulations.
