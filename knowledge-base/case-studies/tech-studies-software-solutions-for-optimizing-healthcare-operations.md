## Healthcare Compliance Check. Done!

Our dedication lies in offering safe and compliant healthcare software solutions, as evidenced by our adherence to fundamental industry standards and certifications. By upholding such measures, we provide the healthcare industry with avant-garde applications that put data security, privacy, interoperability, and efficient operations first. Our certifications inspire trust in healthcare professionals and patients by supporting digital health solutions and improving patient care and results.

**1) HIPAA:** We guarantee the privacy, accuracy, and accessibility of patient health information by adhering to HIPAA rules. To safeguard sensitive patient data, our healthcare software solutions prioritize data security, set access limits, and keep audit trails. This boosts patient and healthcare provider trust, thereby fostering the increased use of modernized health solutions.

**2) HITECH:** We strengthen the security of EHRs and the privacy of patient health information through our compliance with HITECH. To improve the entire security posture of our product, we give data encryption, safe data transmission, and strict data breach reporting methods top priority.

**3) CoPay:** Adherence to CoPay regulations guarantee patients access to clear billing and payment procedures. To simplify the payment process for patients and healthcare providers, our software solutions for the industry offer capabilities for straightforward invoicing, payment tracking, and interaction with insurance carriers.

**4) eFax:** eFax enables healthcare providers to safely emit and receive confidential patient data. Because of the encrypted eFax capabilities of our healthcare software, patient data stays secure while being transmitted.

**5) OWASP:** By following their recommendations, we can create secure online apps. To reduce the danger of cyber threats, we prioritize application security through routine vulnerability assessments, specific coding techniques, and strict security testing procedures.

**6) IEC 62443:** Our compliance with IEC 62443 standards guarantees the security and resiliency of healthcare software systems that connect with medical devices and equipment. We prioritize protecting patient privacy and safety while protecting healthcare infrastructure and data from potential cyberattacks.

**7) DICOM:** Because we adhered to DICOM standards, medical image interchange and interoperability between various healthcare systems are smooth, boosting diagnostic capabilities and encouraging teamwork in patient care.

**8) ICD-10 and CPT:** We ensure correct billing and coding of medical treatments by including ICD-10 and CPT codes in our healthcare software. This lowers errors and enhances revenue cycle management for healthcare providers.
