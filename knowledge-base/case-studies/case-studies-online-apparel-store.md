# Online Store Development for a Fashion Wholesaler

Radixweb enhances usability of Kentico E-commerce web portal by upgrading the version from 5.5 to 7.0. Full-fledged solution enables high end flexibility and rich user experience with advanced CMS features

  * Product Catalog module offering easy navigation and detailed information
  * Dynamic Photo Album feature for quick product review
  * Multilingual support for Arabic and English catering International market
  * Seamless Integration of PayPal Payment processor for quick transactions
  * Effective Public Polling of Clothing Ecommerce Website for immediate feedback for analysis
