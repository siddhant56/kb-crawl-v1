> Radixweb developed a step-by-step digital negotiation tool for procurement, boosting execution 5X and reducing errors by 90% for efficient training.

## Client Overview

Strategic Sourcing is a UK-based consultancy specializing in procurement & negotiation strategy training. Known for a **13-step negotiation framework** , they wanted to modernize their approach & build a digital toolkit that is accurate, adaptable, scalable & secure.

### Country

United Kingdom

### Team Size

4 Dedicated Experts

### Project Duration

6 Months & Ongoing

### Engagement Model

Fixed Engagement

## The Problem

The client’s paper-based negotiation framework had limited scalability, collaboration, and efficiency. It couldn’t adapt as per varying team needs, lacked governance controls, and created friction during execution. To stay competitive, the client needed a digital solution that streamlined strategy planning and enabled real-time, data-driven decision making.

## Solutions Provided

### Digitized Strategy Framework

We converted the manual 13-step negotiation process into a structured, digital toolkit that allowed users to plan and execute strategies more efficiently.

### Fast Prototyping & Agile Delivery

Rapid wireframes and iterative sprints helped us align with the client’s vision quickly, enabling continuous feedback and faster rollout of core features.

### Scalable System Architecture

Built with .NET Core, Angular, Redis, and SQL Server, the architecture supported real-time sync, multi-user access, and cloud-ready scalability for future analytics and intelligent features.

### Smart Execution & Governance

We added automated workflows, access controls, and version management, reducing manual errors and improving consistency across the negotiation process.

### Intuitive UX for Adoption

The UI was designed to mimic familiar paper-based flows, making it easy for users to adopt the platform without training or disruption to their workflow.

### Offline-First Capability

We implemented offline drafting and automatic sync, ensuring users could work uninterrupted during travel or connectivity issues, with updates applied seamlessly when back online.

## Standalone Features

### Negotiation Canvas

Visual drag-and-drop interface that helps users structure & customize negotiation strategies with ease and flexibility.

### Playbook Generator

Automatically compiles negotiation steps, decisions & notes into a structured, shareable playbook for review or collaboration.

### Offline Drafting

Enables users to draft and edit negotiation plans offline, with automatic sync and update once back online so that no progress is lost.

### Decision Support

Built-in AI prompts and analytics guide users through each step, helping them make data-backed negotiation decisions

## Business Benefits

The digital negotiation toolkit successfully transformed a manual, paper-based process into a scalable product, enhancing speed, accuracy, and adoption across teams.

### 90% Fewer Errors

Automated workflows and guided steps in the toolkit reduced manual errors and improved the strategy's accuracy.

### 5X Faster Execution

Streamlined planning and real-time collaboration helped teams move from planning to action significantly faster.

### 100% Centralized Access

All negotiation data is securely stored in the system and accessible anytime, which enables better control and visibility.

### Future-Ready Platform

Scalable architecture supports AI-led features, new modules, and seamless integration as the business grows.
