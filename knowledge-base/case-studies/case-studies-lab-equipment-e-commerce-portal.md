## About Lab Equipment eCommerce Portal Project

## The Solution

Owing to our keen-sighted expertise in eCommerce solution development, Radixweb became a trusted outsourcing partner for the client. Our team of technocrats opted NopCommerce solution for the development owing to its higher flexibility, off-the-shelf features availability to meet requirements and free and stable ASP.NET based solution.

### NopCommerce Deployment & Configuration

Owing to our expertise in developing customized eCommerce apps, we are a proud partner with nopCommerce. For a highly scalable website, our team deployed nopCommerce and did configurations required for feature-rich lab equipment e-commerce portal.

### Design and Customization

The team identified the core focus of customization and implemented listing of products based on categories and sub-categories including auto rotation of products with its relevant category and exclusive home page customization.

### Theme Design & Integration

Our skilled designing team created a custom theme to match client’s design needs, ensuring their branding requirements and user-experience are met impeccably.

### Extensions and Third Party Integration

We did integrations from NopCommerce Extensions Library: payment gateway plugin integration, authorize.NET, PayPal standard, shipping gateway integration for rate calculations and YouTube video plugin integration. Successful Third Party integration with QuickBooks.
