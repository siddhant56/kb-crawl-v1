## Developing Ecommerce Portal for Printing Industry

Our highly experienced team at Radixweb suggested nopCommerce platform for seamless execution of developing eCommerce portal for printing and its integration with client’s CRM system. The system was setup with below features:

### NopCommerce Deployment and Configuration

The team set up nopCommerce and made configurations required for site deployment. We also implemented multi-site deployment as client wanted two separate portals.

### Single Administration Panel

We configured a single nopCommerce admin panel with the help of which client can manage aspects on multi-store eCommerce Portal like: Product catalog, customer accounts, orders, blogs and other required changes.

### Theme Design and Integration

Our skilled team created a custom theme to match the design needs while ensuring branding requirements and user experience are not compromised.

### Extensions Integration

Integrated following extensions from nopCommerce Extensions Library like authorized .NET payment gateway plugin integration, shipping gateway integration with UPS, USPS and FedEx for rate calculations and banner plugin integration.

### Third Party Integrations

One of the requirements was to integrate the nopCommerce portal with client’s MS Dynamics CRM to synchronize the customer accounts and orders.
