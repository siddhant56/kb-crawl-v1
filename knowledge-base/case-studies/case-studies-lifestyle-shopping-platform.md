## The Solution

Radixweb offered nopCommerce – an advanced Microsoft ASP.Net technology platform, implementing right mixture of e-commerce functionality, web content management, and design-rich intuitive storefront with unified shopping experience to end users of client.

### Technology

While evolving the ecommerce solutions from ground up, a right mix of nopCommerce, ASP.NET, HTML5, SQL Server, MVC and, MS Visual Studio technologies was used to develop a competitive and highly compatible online shopping solution.

### NopCommerce CMS

Because of NopCommerce's compatibility with ASP.NET framework, highly configurable ecommerce features, quick application development competences, and user-friendliness NopCommerce was the ideal solution for these requirements.

### Use of Multilingual Technique

In order to deliver the solution in Multi-language, a full-featured multilingual content management system has been designed to organize product catalogs and content of public pages in English and German languages. Facilitating extremely smooth experience on global market.

### Agile Development Practices

When the client approached Radixweb, their ultimate vision was to go online and expand the business at the global level. Keeping in mind the requirements and time-line, we followed agile development methodologies to deliver seamless solution within the time limit.

### Shopping Experience

Feature-rich shopping experience to website users offering options to explore wide range of products by filtering and sorting as per the criteria and comparison, review products with detailed information, maintaining shopping cart, checkout with online PayPal payment, and PDF order receipt with notification.

### Marketing & Promotions

Flexible and SEO friendly ecommerce solution facilitating increased online presence. With the inclusion of fully enriched features like product level rating and review, discounts, coupons, promo codes, and social media sharing the solution was highly interactive.

### Back office Administration

Seamless operation process with unified automated management funcnalities for Products, stocks, purchase order, customers, configuration settings etc. The real-time statistical report functionality enabling quick results.

### Payment Gateway Integration

To provide secure ecommerce transaction mechanism and stable and user-friendly check out process, PayPal payment gateway was implemented.
