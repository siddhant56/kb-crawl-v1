# They Developed a UX Rich and Secured Website by Realizing Our Brand Landscapes

They highlighted my USPs to boost my market presence

Hi, this is Kim Ruiz, the founder and owner of a writing and content services organization based in Brandon, Florida. Our journey with Radixweb started in June 2016. Their dev experts have designed and redeveloped our website, focusing on UX, professionalism, and considering other unique selling-point features. During the project, they directly collaborated with me on the project requirements and improved security for the website.

## Highlights

They were very easy to work with and in tune with what I had in mind.

Radixweb is a polite and professional team which readily overcomes common offshore barriers through meticulous attention to detail and an uncompromising work ethics.

“The website is phenomenal. I think it matches anything probably in even a higher price range. For what I paid and what I got, it’s an excellent product.”

Owner, Kim Ruiz

## Realizing the brand USPs…

Our reputed client is a Brandon-based writing and content service provider firm and was in dire need of a functional website. Their former website had outdated features, lacked professional designs, had hacking issues and required manual maintenance.

Radixweb realized the client's creative vision and re-developed the new website from the scratch by integrating new capabilities, features and style – a prerequisite for any professional website to gain client attention.

They’re professional, creative, and responsive. It was delightful to work with them. It was fun to watch the process because that side of it was new and different for me. I’m the writing person, not the tech/development person, so I enjoyed it.

## Let’s read an extract of the conversation

### Tell us about your line of business.

We are a professional content service provider firm. We run in partnership with another business that creates websites and helps us with other publishing materials. I’m the owner.

### What tech concern drove you to Radixweb, and how did they respond to your crisis?

The time zone differences have never become an issue with Radixweb. When in need, they work more than six days in a week and even late to deliver the required solutions for the global clients.

### How does Radixweb score in terms of Project Management?

They are very easy to work with and were always in tune with what I had in mind. At the very beginning of the project, there was a Skype call about the overall requirements and the contract. Otherwise, we communicated by email. They were always super responsive and even extended their daily schedules so that the communication didn’t suffer.

Like any complex project, there was a lot of back and forth. I provided them with some initial ideas which they built on, and then they sent me some template ideas, which I would respond, “I really like this, or I want to change that.” They never said no to accommodating my iterative change requests.

## The Firm’s Final Thoughts:

While it was a little bit more than the website I was looking to have done, it was less than what my original website designer would charge me. For the cost and what I got, I’m 100% satisfied. I would definitely recommend them. In fact, if I have a client who needs a website, I’m looking to partner with them to help that client.

Kim feels the linguistic barrier could be one grey area while partnering with Radixweb, although she says, “It doesn’t make it difficult; it just requires a little more explanation. But I found them to be beyond polite and enjoyable to speak with via email, which you don’t always get with American clients.”
