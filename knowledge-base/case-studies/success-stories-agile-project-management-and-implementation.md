## Here's how the conversation went:

### Please tell us about your company and your position there.

I'm the CTO of a printing tech company based in Denmark. We provide bespoke software to varied industries and businesses. About 80% of our solutions are out of the box, and 20% are highly customized according to client needs.

### For what tech challenges did you hire Radixweb?

They approach projects with the right questions, and they’re not afraid to say ‘no’ to a project if they feel they cannot meet your expectations. They are helpful, insightful, and trustworthy.

### How would you say Radixweb performed from a project management standpoint?

We communicate with their multi-disciplinary teams at least once a week. For each project, there are between 5 to 10 team members, with a 50/50 ratio of juniors and seniors, but we don't see a big split between the two. They're all quite capable of closing that gap between knowledge and youth if that makes sense. To be honest, it depends on the nature of the project. Initially, we were communicating with seniors or project managers, but when it comes down to implementing phases, it's a mix of juniors and seniors where the distinction is based on project management skills and execution.

Sometimes our clients have certain IT systems that we have to integrate with other systems. For that, we need somebody to be present locally. Usually, this would mean myself as I would find myself giving instructions to juniors and seniors at Radixweb. I only know if they're junior or senior because I know them; otherwise, I wouldn't notice a difference. The team leaders have been pretty helpful and responsive as well.
