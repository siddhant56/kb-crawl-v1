# FinStream – A Digital Financial Web Platform Offering Quick and Easy Credit Products

A simple, user-friendly website that allows tech-savvy Australian customers to easily apply for a range of loan products and financial services

  * The UI is designed to be intuitive and easy to use. Users can quickly navigate through the information they need, and complete their loan applications within minutes
  * Low loan rates from multiple providers with a fast and hassle-free approval process and free credit score checking and tracking
  * The website uses a responsive design, which ensures that it works smoothly on all devices, including desktops, tablets, and smartphones
  * MVC architecture to communicate between the client-side and server-side, allowing for the support for multiple views and asynchronous technique
