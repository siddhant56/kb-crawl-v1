## FAQs

### What is a best example of a native app?

When considering an example of native app development at its finest, Facebook and Instagram stand out due to their widespread use and global impact.

### What language is used for native apps?

Native app development can involve leveraging multiple languages, including Objective-C, Swift and HTML 5. Alternatively, Java can be used to produce an android version of the same app that works on the different platform without losing any existing features.

### Why native app development is better?

Native app development offers many advantages over cross-platform or hybrid development approaches. From comprehensive access to hardware and software specific components to the ability to build scalable and secure application, the benefits of native app development are clear.

### How much does it cost for Native app development?

The specific pricing of native app development project depends on its size and complexity. Factors such as native app design, technology stack, engineering hours and platform on which the app is being developed needs to be taken into account.

### What kind of support will our team will provide after the native app goes live?

Our team at Radixweb provide tremendous support once an app goes live. This includes enhancements, bug fixes, diagnostics, updates and an escalation process in the event of any issues or outages. Above all, we are always available to answer questions and provide advice related to the operation of the app and its performance in order to optimize its long-term success.
