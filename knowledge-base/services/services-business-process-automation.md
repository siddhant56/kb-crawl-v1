## Business Process Automation Solutions We Provide

### Business Process Mapping

Get actionable insights on process inefficiencies and scope of automation. Our process strategy consulting services build a foundation for business automation through data.

### BPA Software

Build blazing-fast processes with minimal response times. We build cloud-native automation software with easily integrable, containerized components designed to solve operational challenges.

### Decisioning Software

Make repeated decisions automated and transparent with AI-led capabilities. We’ll help you target individual customer interactions, derive business logic independently for decision agility.

### Workflow Automation

Streamline repetitive tasks with rule-based automation for exceptions. Structure repetitive processes with AI-led recommendations. We use intuitive UI to democratize automation and simplify complex processes.

### Robotic Process Automation

Target and scale up back-office task automation in enterprise-level workflow ecosystems with AI bots. Build non-invasive integration capabilities in legacy solutions for minimal disruption.

### Process Mining

We accumulate event data from enterprise software to realign business processes. We set automation rules for deviations, variations, compliances through **Celonis** , **Signavio (SAP)** , **ProcessMaker** , **Disco (Fluxicon)**.

### AI Task Orchestration

Use AI automated rule-based system engines for task prioritizing, assigning and executing to elevate inter-department coordination, defining visibility into process bottlenecks and improved SLA adherence.

### Document Processing

We use top OCR Engines, NLP platforms, document AI suites and workflow platforms for automated data extraction, classification and contextual interpretation and triggering workflows like CRMS, HRMS, ERP.

### Compliance & Risk Management

Get automated policy enforcement, real-time alerts, audit trail generation, reporting automation for SOX, GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS. We also ensure SLA adherence, data access and access automation.
