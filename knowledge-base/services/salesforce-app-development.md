## Full-Cycle Salesforce Development Services Stages

Rectify crucial business issues promptly with the development of cloud-based Salesforce CRM applications and its numerous stages in the Salesforce development lifecycle

### Stage 1: Discovery Phase

Execute the Salesforce development process with an initial and important phase – Discovery Phase that contributes to effective development. Preliminaries for the Salesforce implementation are completed during this phase, and all relevant information for the implementation is collected, examined, and deliberated

### Stage 2: Source Control Set-up

The release manager from Radixweb sets up the source control repository in this stage. A separate Git repository for an individual project is always desirable, and the default branch can serve as the master branch for storing production details. Our release manager also comes with a package.xml manifest

### Stage 3: Development Phase

Developers create sandboxes. And they often contain a duplicate of the main production app as well as necessary Salesforce setup information. Salesforce developers from Radixweb start development in their sandboxes, using Force.com to connect to their sandboxes and retrieve metadata from the sandbox to the IDE

### Stage 4: Testing Phase

The QA team from Radixweb migrates the code from the repository and tests it in their sandbox environment. Moreover, QA teams share their sandbox environment while thoroughly testing important features. On the other hand, changes suggested at this stage will send it back to the beginning of the development process

### Stage 5: Acceptance Testing

Developers, product managers, and other involved team members from Radixweb perform the final testing on the product. The release managers create partial sandboxes for testing, which the product managers subsequently use to conduct ad-hoc testing before making a final presentation to the customers or end-users

### Stage 6: Product Release

The app's performance that has gone through all of the testing stages is evaluated in an intermediate sandbox environment with all of the data and configurations from the production environment. Since the sandbox isn't limited, it includes all of the functionalities and features of the application

### Stage 7: Patch Release

Once the app is deployed or launched, further requirements such as bug corrections, feature modifications, or minor changes may arise, which must be addressed through patch releases by our Salesforce developers. The patch release has its own cycle, although it follows a different set of procedures than the app development lifecycle

### Stage 8: After-launch Support

Being a trusted and reliable Salesforce development consultant, our experts offer after-launch support and maintenance services to your custom applications. Beyond user training, our Salesforce professionals fine-tune the functionalities of the application and ensure that users find every functionality easier and smoother
