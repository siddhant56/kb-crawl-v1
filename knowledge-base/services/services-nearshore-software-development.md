## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is nearshore software development?

Nearshore software development is the process of delegating software development to external companies located in the same country or region as yours. Nearshore software development is the right choice if you wish to eliminate cultural differences and language barriers.

### What are nearshore locations Radixweb serve?

Radixweb is distributed across the main cities of North America. Moreover, we are always available in your Time Zone.

### What’s the difference between offshoring and nearshoring?

Nearshoring means hiring a team of developers from a nearshore development company situated in the same region, whereas offshore software development means outsourcing to a different location (geographically distant country).
