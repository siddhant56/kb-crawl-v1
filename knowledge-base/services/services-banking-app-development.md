### What are the features and benefits of mobile banking apps?

The convenience of banking on the go without having to visit a physical branch is just one of the many benefits of mobile banking apps. The primary methods to create a banking app for Android and iOS are Native iOS development, Native Android Development, and Cross-platform development. Some of the most popular programming languages used for developing mobile apps for both platforms include Swift, Objective-C, Java and Kotlin.
