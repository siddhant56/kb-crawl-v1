## Salesforce Service Cloud Features You Shouldn't Miss Out

Salesforce Service Cloud enables you to effortlessly engage with each customer and give them a personalized support experience. Here are the key features of this platform that will help you do so

### Omni-Channel Routing

Get extensive customer service across multiple channels, such as via the web, mobile, email, video conference, etc., and automatically redirect customers’ cases to suitable agents with Salesforce Service Cloud

### Case Management

The case management feature allows you to track, record, and resolve customer issues. Cover a wide array of tasks like assignment, escalation, auto-response, etc., and streamline the customer service pipeline

### Service Console

Enhance the productivity of your support team and enable them to work more efficiently by allowing them to access everything at their fingertips, be it dashboards, histories, or customer profiles

### Knowledge Base

The Salesforce Service Cloud platform offers comprehensive knowledge base management solutions to help your agents find all their answers and resolve complicated cases as quickly as possible

### Service Analytics

The Service Analytics tool by Salesforce comes with critical insights and reports into agent performance, average customer satisfaction, number of cases assigned, important KPIs, channel usage, and so on

### Field Service

With Salesforce Service Cloud’s Field Service feature, organizations can seamlessly cooperate with field workers, manage their schedules and appointments, track their work, and more in real-time

### Einstein Bots

Power your chat support system with cutting-edge Salesforce Einstein bots. Free your agents from unproductive day-to-day work and let them focus on other critical tasks with chat automation

### App Builder

Utilize Service Cloud App Builders to transform your mundane spreadsheets into modern cloud-based apps, broaden your CRM capabilities, and personalize customer interactions with configurable elements

### Social Customer Service

You can monitor, manage, and respond to your customers' posts using Salesforce Service Cloud on a variety of social media sites, including Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, and other channels
