### How long does it take to build a mobile app?

A simple MVP can go live in 8 to 12 weeks. Mid-complexity app take 4 to 6 months, while enterprise app can take between 6 to 12 months. During discovery, we provide a milestone-based delivery roadmap so you have full visibility from day one.

### Should I build a native app or a cross-platform app?

Native apps (Swift/Kotlin) is best when you need maximum performance, deep device integration, or platform-specific UX. Cross-platform apps (Flutter/React Native) reduce cost by 30–40% and delivers near-native quality. These are best for MVPs, B2B tools, and eCommerce. At Radixweb, we start by helping you decide between native and cross-platform app development based on your goals and budget.

### What compliance standards do your apps meet?

We build mobile applications that are compliance with HIPAA (healthcare), GDPR (EU data privacy), PCI-DSS (payments), and SOC 2 (enterprise security). Our development process incorporates security-by-design and we are ISO 27001:2022 and ISO 9001:2015 certified.
