## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Drupal development services?

Drupal development services include the development, modification, and maintenance of websites with the help of Drupal system. These services ensure that your site is well-grown, safe from potential threats, and is user-friendly.

### How is Drupal development different from other content management systems?

In comparison with other content management systems, Drupal has more flexibility and scalability. It is a CMS offering a modular architecture enabling extensive customization - which is a perfect-fit for advanced projects.

### What kind of websites can be developed on Drupal?

Some of the websites that can be developed using Drupal include blogs, eCommerce websites, corporate portals, and community platforms. This versatility is incredibly useful in meeting diverse business requirements.

### How much time does it take to make a Drupal website?

The time frame for Drupal web development is determined by the complexity of the website, but usually a simple website can be developed in 3-4 months and advanced ones can take around 6-8 months.

### Can I integrate third-party applications with my Drupal site?

Yes, you can integrate third-party applications with your Drupal website using various forums and API’s. As Drupal supports RESTful web services, you can ensure seamless data exchange and user synchronization with external services.

### What kind of support do you offer for Drupal websites?

With Radixweb, you can have Drupal support and maintenance along with development. Our experts are a part of the Drupal community who helps you with quick support from bug fixing, functionality upgrade, integration to backup.
