## FAQs

### What is the Ansible IT automation platform?

The Ansible Automation Platform is a comprehensive suite of software tools designed for automating the software deployment process, configuration of systems, and complex development workflows. With its inclusive resources, it facilitates the creation, administration, and scalability of operations throughout the entire organization.

### What is configuration management?

Configuration management is the modern method employed to achieve and maintain desired states for software systems, applications, servers, network devices, and other IT components. It makes sure that the system's performance remains consistent, even after multiple modifications over time.

### Why choose Ansible for IT automation?

By utilizing the Ansible Red Hat platform, organizations can effortlessly share automation processes across their enterprise. This platform offers all the necessary tools for creating, executing, and managing automation within a single subscription. From automation execution environments to Ansible Content Collections and automation analytics, you can explore and benefit from the various features it provides.

### Is Ansible a tool or language?

Ansible is primarily known as a tool, specifically an open-source automation software platform. It is designed to automate IT tasks such as configuration management, application deployment, cloud provisioning, and more.
