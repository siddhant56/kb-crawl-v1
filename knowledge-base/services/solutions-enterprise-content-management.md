### What is enterprise content management (ECM)?

Enterprise Content Management (ECM) is a set of defined processes, strategies, and tools that allow organizations to store, manage, track, and extract data with utmost security.

### What is the difference between Content Management System and Web Content Management?

Content management system (CMS) comes with multiple features to manage web content, whereas web content management system extends the features from CMS into different areas like Collaboration, Workflow Management, Business Process Management, and Digital Asset Management.
