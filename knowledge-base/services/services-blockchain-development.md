## Types of Blockchain Networks We Offer

### Public Blockchain

A public blockchain is open-source, non-restrictive, and permissionless, which allows users to access a blockchain platform with equal rights to become an authorized node

### Private Blockchain

Private blockchains, known as permissioned blockchains or enterprise blockchains, work in a restrictive environment and are executed by a closed network or a single organization

### Hybrid Blockchain

A hybrid blockchain is a combination of private and public blockchains, where some part is visible as a public blockchain, and some part is controlled by an organization

### Consortium Blockchain

Consortium blockchains are permissioned blockchains managed by a group of organizations instead of a single user, which results in higher levels of security

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is blockchain development and what are the potential benefits for my business?

Blockchain development is all about creating distributed applications that are secure and transparent. It can streamline operational processes, minimize the costs, and promote some level of trust with its clients since the records will be immutable.

### How can blockchain enhance the operational efficiency in my business?

Blockchain improves operational efficiency by automating operations, cutting down the manual efforts and allowing for real-time data sharing. It results in quick transactions and informed decision making.

### What industries can take advantage of such technology?

The finance, supply chain, healthcare and real estate are some of the sectors that can take advantage of blockchain technology. It provides security and accountability in transactions.

### Can blockchain be considered business-friendly?

Owing to its distributed nature and the use of cryptographic methods, security is guaranteed on all levels where blockchain technology is applied. That’s why blockchain is highly trusted in businesses.

### Can blockchain be integrated with existing systems?

Of course, yes, it is possible to integrate blockchain within the existing systems. Our team specializes in seamless integration, ensuring zero losses in your current processes and maximum benefits from blockchain technology.

### How long does it take to develop a blockchain solution?

A basic blockchain application development can take around 3 to 6 months. On the other hand, a complex application with custom features or an application with complete overhaul can take more than 12 months.
