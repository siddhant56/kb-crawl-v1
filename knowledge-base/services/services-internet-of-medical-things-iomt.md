### What is IoMT?

### What is IoMT development?

IoMT development is the process of planning, designing, developing, delivering, and maintaining an IoMT system from scratch. As a leading IoMT development company, we will help you build a resilient IoT healthcare platform that transforms the entire healthcare ecosystem.

### What is the difference between IoT and IoMT?

The IoT, an umbrella term for networked devices and Big Data solutions, includes the Internet of Medical Things. The qualities of IoMT that are typical of eHealth and medical technology set it apart from IoT technology created for other industries, such as medical IoT security, improved reliability, and so on.

### What are examples of IoT healthcare devices?

Some of the most popular types of IoT healthcare devices existing in the market are ingestible sensors, connected inhalers, robotic surgery, heart-rate monitor, wearable biosensors, smart thermometers, etc.
