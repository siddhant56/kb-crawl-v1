## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is application migration?

Application migration is a process of moving business legacy systems from one computing environment to another. This process includes migrating legacy applications from one data center to another, such as from an organization’s on-premise server to cloud, or from public to private cloud. This is a method adapted by businesses to overcome the growth challenges in their IT systems like high maintenance cost, low scalability, security threats, and inability to quickly update as per the changing needs of the organization.
