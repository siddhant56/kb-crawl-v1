## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is rapid prototyping and how does it differ from an MVP?

Rapid prototyping services help create quick software mockups and demos, enabling fast testing, feedback, and iteration before full-scale development. Unlike an MVP, which is a minimal version of a product intended for real users to validate market demand, rapid prototypes are usually internal mockups built to test concepts, workflows, interfaces, and design assumptions.

### How long do you take to design and develop a software prototype?

Most of our rapid software prototyping projects wrap up in about 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the complexity. For slightly complex app workflows or integrations, we adjust the timeline upfront, but even then, we keep iterations tight.

### What types of prototypes do you develop for enterprises?

We mostly design low-fidelity wireframes for early concept validation, high-fidelity interactive prototypes that simulate real workflows, and functional prototypes with core features integrated to test system interactions. We also create technical prototypes for evaluating integrations, APIs, or performance constraints.

### What deliverables will we receive at the end of the prototype?

At the end of a rapid application prototyping engagement, you’ll receive a complete set of deliverables customized for immediate use and evaluation. This typically includes interactive clickable prototypes, annotated design files, user journey maps, and functional demonstrations of core workflows. We also provide technical documentation with architecture considerations, integration points, and next-step recommendations.

### Do you include user testing and validation in the prototyping process?

Yes, user testing and validation are integral to our prototype development process. Automated and manual testing sessions are executed with real or representative users to observe interactions, identify friction points, and get feedback. Alongside usability insights, we provide analytics summaries and prioritized recommendations as well.

### How do you transition a prototype to an MVP or production-ready handoff?

Once the prototype is validated, we compile all assets like interactive designs, technical documentation, user flows, and feedback summaries into a development blueprint. Our team aligns feature priorities and creates implementation-ready specifications for engineers to move directly into development with minimal ambiguity.
