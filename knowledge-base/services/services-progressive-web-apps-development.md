## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do PWAs differ from native apps?

Native apps are built specifically for iOS or Android using separate codebases. They require to be downloaded from app stores. PWAs on the other hand, provide native app-like experiences through a single codebase supported across platforms, run on browsers, work across devices. They are light weight, have modular architectures, are indexable on search engines, provide workability even on low bandwidth scenarios.
