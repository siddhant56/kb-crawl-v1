## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is included in your UI/UX design services?

Our UI/UX design and development services typically cover user research, information architecture, low- and high-fidelity wireframing, interactive prototyping, visual interface design, usability testing, and developer handoff with complete design documentation. Where required, accessibility audits and WCAG compliance support are also provided.

### How long does a typical UI/UX design project take?

Project timelines entirely depend on scope and complexity. A straightforward web interface will take as little as 3-4 weeks for us to design. For multi-platform products or systems with layered user journeys, 8-12 weeks is more typical. Time estimates are refined after the initial scoping and discovery phase.

### Do you offer user research as a standalone service?

Yes. We provide user research and analysis independently for clients who wish to validate their product idea, test early concepts, or even identify usability challenges in existing products. User research usually includes interviews, task observation, persona development, and competitive benchmarking.

### How do you ensure accessibility in your designs?

Accessibility is integrated into the design process from the wireframing stage. As a UI/UX design services company, we use WCAG 2.1 standards as a baseline, perform contrast checks, structure semantic hierarchy properly, and test with assistive technologies. Internal audits and usability reviews are also conducted where relevant.

### Can you redesign an existing product without starting from scratch?

Yes. We conduct heuristic reviews and UX audits to evaluate existing interfaces. From there, we isolate design issues, map updated user flows, and apply visual refinements selectively. This allows for incremental upgrades without discarding the functional foundation of the current system.

### What tools and platforms do you use in UI/UX design?

Our UI/UX design team typically works in Figma for design and prototyping, along with Miro for mapping and Notion or Zeroheight for design systems. Tools for accessibility, version control, and feedback management are selected based on client infrastructure and project scale.

### Do you design for both web and mobile applications?

Yes. We design for responsive web interfaces, native mobile applications, and even cross-platform frameworks. Based on each platform’s specific interaction patterns, screen constraints, navigation conventions, and user requirements, we create the UI design and UX optimization strategies.
