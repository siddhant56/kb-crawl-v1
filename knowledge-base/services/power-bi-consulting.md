## Harness the Benefits of Power BI

### Informed Decision Making

By harnessing the power of business intelligence reporting, your enterprise can gain the advantage of essential key performance indicators within a singular view. Also, BI tools deliver information accurately and rapidly.

### Data Connectivity

Utilizing the capabilities of Power BI tools, you can easily access a wide range of data sources, including cloud-based and on-premises databases, SharePoint lists, Excel sheets, and more. You can bridge the gap between disparate data sources and actionable insights.

### Custom Visualization

Charts, maps, tables, and matrices are just a few of the interactive visualizations that Power BI provides for exploring and comprehending data. It provides easy to use dashboards for speedy extraction and intelligent data displays.

### Real Time Data

Thanks to Power BI's support for real-time data streaming and visualization, your organization can obtain a real-time view of data as it arrives. Using Power BI, you may develop real-time dashboards that refresh immediately.

### Powered by AI

Utilize the most recent developments in Microsoft AI to assist non-data scientists in data preparation, machine learning model building, and the speedy discovery of insights from both structured and unstructured data, including text and images.

### Minimal Upfront Costs

One of the most important advantages of Power BI is its minimal upfront costs. Since Power BI is subscription-based, no licenses or support are required. You can use the desktop version (Power BI Desktop). They also offer two more subscriptions that can be chosen based on the needs.
