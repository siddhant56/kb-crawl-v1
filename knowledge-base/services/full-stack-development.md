## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is full stack development?

Full stack development is the process of developing both front-end and back-end of the website, web and mobile applications. However, front-end is referred to client-side and the back-end is referred to the server-side.

### What are the main benefits of using full stack web development service?

The prime benefit of full stack web development services is to work on the entire project – designing structure at any level and switching development practices between front-end and back-end. As a result, it helps you save costs with easy future upgradation.

### What is the difference between full stack web development and generic web development?

A website developed using full stack technologies is quite faster, reducing the overall cost to develop, while eliminating the testing and maintenance cost by a significant margin. On the other hand, generic web development takes time compared to full stack web development.
