## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a web portal?

A web portal, or more commonly referred to as a portal, is a specially developed website that collects and provides information in a variety of tailored ways. Search engine APIs for Internet search, email, news, forums for group discussion, dashboards, project objectives, consumer profiles, and sales strategies. and business mashups that combine data, tools, and apps are just a few examples of the services and features that may be added to a portal to make it more useful.

### What are web portal development services?

Web portal development services aim at planning, designing, developing, launching, and maintaining an online web-based platform for a number of functionalities, such as targeting users, promoting your products as services, streamlining internal workflow, providing employee training, and so on. As a leading software development company, we offer end-to-end custom web portal development solutions to create a safe and secure digital space for your partners, employees, or customers.

### What technologies fit best for developing a web portal?

The list of technologies to develop a quality web portal depends on your project requirements and the feature complexity of the web portal. However, the basic tech stack includes technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Python, Angular.js, Bootstrap, Ruby on Rails, MongoDB, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and so on.

### What is the difference between a website and a web portal?

The difference between a website and a web portal is that websites provide information to users, whereas web portals provide as well as collect information for various business purposes. Web portals are for limiting traffic to a particular audience base; websites are for generating traffic. The majority of websites ask visitors to log in so that the site can deliver more personalized content and services based on who they are. Websites are accessible to all users and typically cater to a wide range of demographics.
