## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does Radixweb take to develop a custom application?

Developing custom-built applications varies in timelines due to complexity of feature requirements, state of your tech architecture, integrations and nature of tech stack update. Generally we take 2-4 weeks to build MVPS, 4-6 weeks for simple apps while complex enterprise apps with custom features might take up to 12 months.

### When should a business choose custom application development over ready-made software?

The tech industry is evolving with rapid automation and the complexities that come with it. Off-the-shelf software cannot always deliver the efficiency and features your users need. This is where application customization services come to the rescue – from tailored features to market-leading integrations, cloud-led agility and constantly evolving security authentications – bespoke apps deliver the scalability, security and performance you need to stand out in the market.

### How do you ensure the security and compliance of custom-built applications?

Our custom enterprise application development services leverage ISO 9001, 27001 and SOC 2 verified processes. We also comply with leading regulations like HIPAA, GDPR and many more. With security-as-design approach and automated data protection capabilities, we ensure your business data is protected in complex business environments, cementing the business value you deliver through apps.

### What is your approach to integration with existing enterprise systems?

We leverage advanced custom app development consulting to ensure zero disruption modernization and integrations at Radixweb. When working with existing solutions and integration advanced capabilities into them, we ensure secure data flows for data at rest and transit. We also enforce, OAuth2, RBAC and several other security approaches that secure new features and existing solutions parallely.
