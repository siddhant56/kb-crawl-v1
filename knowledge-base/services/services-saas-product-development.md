### What is SaaS Product Development?

SaaS (Software as a Service) product development refers to the process of designing, building, and deploying software applications that are hosted on the cloud and made available to users over the internet on a subscription basis.

### Is SaaS product development suitable for startups?

Yes, SaaS product development fits with any startup to enhance productivity and scalability while being cost-effective. However, other factors like market competition funding and technical proficiency should consider as well.

### What is the difference between SaaS and traditional software development?

SaaS software development creates applications that are hosted on the cloud, which users can easily access through an online subscription. In contrast, traditional software development builds apps that need to be installed directly on devices or servers.
