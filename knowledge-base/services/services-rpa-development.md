## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do businesses identify which processes should be automated first with RPA?

The best candidates for RPA are high-volume, rule-based, repetitive tasks, but prioritization requires a structured process-discovery assessment. We evaluate operational bottlenecks, compliance risks, manual dependencies, and potential ROI to identify workflows that deliver the fastest automation payback.

### How can organizations scale RPA from a few bots to an enterprise-wide automation program?

Scaling requires governance, standardized frameworks, bot reuse strategies, security policies, and a clear automation roadmap. Centralizing automation efforts through an RPA Center of Excellence (CoE) enables resource optimization and avoids fragmented automation across departments.

### How does RPA support compliance-heavy operations without increasing audit workload?

Bots execute tasks the same way every time, creating consistent, traceable logs. This standardized behavior reduces audit effort while improving accuracy. RPA platforms also include built-in controls that help teams align with regulations like SOX, HIPAA, and GDPR.

### Can RPA coexist with existing automation tools like BPM platforms or low-code systems?

Yes. RPA complements BPM by automating task-level activities within larger workflows and enhances low-code systems by integrating processes that lack APIs or connectors. This creates a seamless automation ecosystem without replacing current investments.

### How do you measure the business impact of RPA beyond cost savings?

Key metrics include error reduction, processing speed, SLAs met, customer satisfaction, employee productivity uplift, and compliance accuracy. Mature automation programs track both **quantitative** and **qualitative** value to assess true performance.

### Can RPA and AI work together to create more intelligent automation?

Yes. AI + RPA, often called **Intelligent Automation** , combines machine learning, NLP, and computer vision with bots. This enables automation of judgment-based tasks like document classification, sentiment analysis, and image processing, expanding what businesses can automate beyond simple rules.
