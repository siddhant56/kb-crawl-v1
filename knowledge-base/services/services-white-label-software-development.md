## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are white label services?

In the business world, "white label services" refers to services or goods produced by one company and then renamed for another's use. This development strategy enables organizations to save money, time, and resources on their marketing initiatives while receiving the same high-quality product they would have received if they had built their own branded version.

### What is white label software product development?

White label software product development is an end-to-end process of planning, creating, deploying, and maintaining a software product you may rebrand. Social media apps, CRMs, SaaS products, and many other types of software can all be white labeled. You can use it for your internal teams, users, or any other objective.
