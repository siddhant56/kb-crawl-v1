## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Fastify boilerplate production-ready and secure?

Yes. The Fastify + Node.js boilerplate comes with built-in JWT authentication, role-based access control, and security best practices so you can launch your backend confidently.

### Can I customize the Fastify and Typescript boilerplate for my project?

Absolutely. You can extend the Fastify boilerplate with features like OAuth, multi-tenancy, AWS integrations, or additional APIs to fit your specific needs.

###  What are the setup requirements and complexity?

It’s simple. You need Node.js ≥18, Docker for PostgreSQL (or your own DB), and basic CLI knowledge. The starter kit’s CLI guides you step by step to get a fully working backend in minutes.
