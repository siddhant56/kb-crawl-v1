# Engineering the Future: Software Partnerships, AI & Innovation

Software is no longer just code. It is becoming the backbone of growth, resilience, and differentiation. But the way businesses build software is also changing fast.

Enterprises are moving away from transactional vendor relationships and toward deep, strategic partnerships that shape long-term success. At the same time, AI, cloud, and modern architectures are rewriting the rules of software delivery. The challenge for leaders is clear: how do you balance speed with quality, and innovation with reliability?

That’s exactly what Pratik Mistry, EVP – Technology Consulting, and Sarrah Pitaliya, VP – Digital Marketing, explore in this episode of Radixweb Unplugged. They break down what real software partnerships look like, how AI is reshaping development, and what forward-looking leaders should be prioritizing right now.

If you’re rethinking how your organization approaches technology partnerships (or simply want to see where software development is heading!) watch the full episode above or tune into Spotify for complete insights.
