## Enhancing Quality of Work for Women at Radixweb

### Inclusive Recruitment Process

We prioritize merit and skills in our unbiased hiring process. We actively seek out the underrepresented to enhance diverse perspectives in our teams.

### Gender Equality Advocacy

At the crux of our operational policy lies gender equality. With a considerable female representation in Radixweb across hierarchies, we ensure our policies support their development.

### Mindful Work-life Integration

We support women throughout the diverse phases of their lives and other roles they perform - from comprehensive support policies to flexible work schedules to help them flourish.

### Return-to-work Programs

We also have a distinct to help women transition seamlessly from career breaks. We help them re-enter the workforce with flexible onboarding, dedicated mentoring and through reskilling programs.

### Leadership Development Programs

Our focussed leadership labs prepare our female workforces for future senior roles. We simulate them through innovation, problem-solving and realistic decision-making challenges.

### Unbiased Performance Assessment

Our performance reviews are strictly data-led to avoid unconscious bias. We consciously leave resources on maternity break out of review cycles to eliminate undue pressure.
