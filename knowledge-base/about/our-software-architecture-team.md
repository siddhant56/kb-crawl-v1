## Design Principles Our Architecture Teams Uphold

### Business-first

Our teams build software foundations that directly traces to outcomes like conversion, retention, margins, and SLA adherence – aliging every design with larger organizational goals.

### Evolvability

Tech demands frequent changes to stay relevant as per market demands. We decouple architectural layers so teams can iterate independently (APIs, event-driven patterns, modular domains).

### Resilience over Perfection

Our architecture prioritizes graceful degradation, automated failover, and recovery strategies—ensuring systems remain functional under stress and recover quickly without compromising user experience or business continuity.

### Sustainability

We embed cost and carbon awareness into architecture—optimizing compute choices, storage tiers, and workload placement to reduce environmental impact while maintaining performance and financial efficiency.
