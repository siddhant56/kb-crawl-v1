## Achieving Cultural Fit with DEI

### No Unconscious Bias

We create a positive workspace by building awareness in our teams of instinctive prejudices and affinity bias. Our workplace decisions are based on merit and potential.

### Strong Talent Equality

We are 650+ perspectives strong now! We provide equal professional advancement to talent of all backgrounds, acknowledging their contribution in Radixweb’s growth as a business.

### Equality of Pay

We recognize the contribution of every resource, regardless of gender and identities when rewarding compensation packages. We regularly align our remuneration practices to avoid pay inequalities.

### Racism-Free Culture

We have a strong stance against racial prejudices. We categorically eliminate racial partiality and nurture an environment where everyone is accepted, valued and treated fairly.

### Gender-Neutral Leadership

Women form over 37% of our workforce. Our focus is on harnessing the potential of good talent and shaping them into leaders of tomorrow, no matter their gender.

### Equal Professional Growth

We offer equal mentoring, career advancement, upskilling and professional benefits as part of our inclusivity policy. We hire people from diverse streams and professional experiences.

## Promoting Inclusivity for All

### Advancing Differently Abled

Disability inclusion for us lies in constantly enhancing our culture of empathy and respect towards resources of different abilities. We empower their distinctive talent with equal opportunities.

### Promoting LGBTIQ+ Inclusion

Growth and hiring are merit and performance-based in Radixweb. Gender and sexual identities of our resources are warmly accepted so that they thrive as themselves.
