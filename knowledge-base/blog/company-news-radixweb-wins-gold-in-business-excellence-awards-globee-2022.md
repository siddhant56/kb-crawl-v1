# Radixweb Takes Gold in Annual Business Excellence Awards by Globee® - 2022

Oct 16, 2022

 _**Radixweb has been awarded yet another accolade – a Gold Globee® for IT Consultation in the 12th Annual Business Excellence Awards, 2022. This esteemed award speaks volumes about their commitment to delivering value to global clients and further validates their position in the IT industry.**_

As 2022 has almost come to an end, Radixweb has successfully added yet another jewel to its crown. As a pioneer of software development and IT consultation, Radixweb has been named a gold medal winner in the 12th Annual Globee® Business Excellence Awards, 2022. This prestigious award acknowledges organizations with avant-garde services, solutions, and products that set a higher bar for everyone in the tech industry.

To uphold the greatest standards for corporate accountability, openness, and trust, the Radixweb team listens to client ideas and transforms them into revenue streams. Organizations can utilize their technology-enabled strategies and custom software solutions to embrace the fastest time-to-market, greater scalability, and continuing growth of their venture.

"We are thrilled to be selected as the winner of the Globee Awards and to be acknowledged as a key player in the industry. This accomplishment is the result of many hours of hard work. The list would be very long if I had to name every person who is a part of this success,” said Dharmesh Acharya, the COO of Radixweb. “In short, thanks to our dynamic team that constantly pushes the boundaries of innovation and our clients whose tech requirements bring out the best in us.”

With a diverse, agile approach, Radixweb is a persistent yet responsive company committed to deliverables and puts its clients at the center stage. The firm facilitates innovation in the product-service experience through incremental, iterative steps and, as a result, provides constant enterprise transformation at speed and scale.

Radixweb is delighted to receive the 12th Annual Excellence Award for its experience-driven intelligence that rapidly adapts to business demands. They don't, however, rely solely on their accolades. In order to come up with the appropriate solutions on time, they keep playing with the latest technologies and follow tried-and-true methodologies.

"We are living in an amazing time, one in which we have seen repellent setbacks, as well as remarkable acts of goodness and empathy. It's been encouraging to see how many businesses, especially in these difficult times, are committed to improving people's lives,’ said Sarrah Pitaliya, Digital Marketing Head at Radixweb. “We are overjoyed to receive this Globee Award, which validates our own efforts to generate social good through our digital goods and services to support those battling with biz-tech stress."

The company has been winning consecutive awards from industry-leading authenticators like Clutch and Great Place to Work for their capabilities in software development services and for providing tailor-made solutions to their clients. Their extensive portfolio of 4200+ successful projects and 3000+ clients across 25 countries demonstrates their genuine and transparent approach to outsourced development projects.

Through best-in-class service delivery, Radixweb has consistently outperformed its rivals by enhancing client satisfaction. The company's dedication to teamwork and customer success has emerged as its "shining armor" in a world of fierce competition, helping the company overcome all obstacles and triumph once more.
