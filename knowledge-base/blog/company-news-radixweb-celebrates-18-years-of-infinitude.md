# Radixweb Celebrates 18 Years of Infinitude

Jul 22, 2018

Proven IT Outsourcing Partner and Software Development Leader – Radixweb Achieves a Milestone in its Remarkable Journey of Radiating Tech Intelligence

They say- It takes a village to run an awesome company. Well for Radixweb, it takes way less than that. On clocking 18 years of infinitude in the evolving world of technology, Radixweb tips the hat and raises the toast to continue radiating tech intelligence for its sterling community of clients, partners and employees. With fueled momentum gained with experience and adaptive expertise, this trusted IT Outsourcing and Software Development partner is clocking major successes under its roof.

As the company celebrates an important milestone, it showcases the grit of a skilled rider on the treacherous roads of blink-and-you-miss technology landscape. The juggernaut gains more speed now.

Spinning innovations for small to Fortune 500 companies- Radixweb delivers custom software development, IT outsourcing, web-to-print solutions and embedded software development for over 2750 global companies with a strength of 390+ brains.

Dharmesh Acharya, Chief Operating Officer- Radixweb shares his joy, “This journey began on the idea and still continues to simplify technology challenges for our global clientele. For the years, we have built a work ethic where we constantly strive to imbibe cultural differences and build strong communication channels. I believe, we have the right resources to drive and solve tough problems.”

Letting their miraculous secret out, he adds, “As a company, we are known to do what we say and say what we do.”- Quite an adage in the world of over commitment and under-delivery. With an extensive client retention ratio, Acharya says, “For us, it starts with valuing our clients’ time and expectations, by solving their most pressing business issues first and by not interrupting with something- they don’t need.”

> As a company, we are known to do what we say and say what we do

As the wishes pour in from across the globe, Michael Cybulski, CEO of USA- based Global Emission Monitoring company says, “Congratulations to the customer-centric team. Thank you for going the extra mile to ensure our success. The excellence that Radixweb provided is the reason it’s working so well. We are very happy and it’s doing precisely what we envisioned.”

Foundation days are ideal to – look back as well as look forward to what is coming next.Echoing these sentiments, Acharya says, “Our first 18 years have been incredible- we’ve empowered global businesses and people to realize their full potential. But what matters most now is what we do next. For the next 18 years- our vision and solid commitment to elevate our customer’s comfort and nurturing excellence harnesses more power. And our willingness to continue doing the same- only strengthens with each passing day.”

> Our vision and solid commitment to elevate our customer’s comfort and nurturing excellence harnesses more power.- Dharmesh Acharya, COO- Radixweb

“From the time of Radixweb’s inception- we’ve been relentlessly committed to simplifying technology, remaining infallible by grasping the pulse of the businesses and doing it with an unwavering eye. It’s an intrinsic part of all that we as a team of 390+ employees do. Generating sustained results for our clients, we are marking this important milestone in Radixweb’s journey of infinitude,” adds Pratik Mistry, Senior Business Head.

Continuing the annual tradition, the technology services company celebrates its anniversary with a class appeal. Popular as “RxConfab”, this year the teams will commemorate their infinitude- laden journey with full gusto.The next chapter in their reverberating journey begins on a note of enthusiasm, grit and grip of helping customers reach exponential success.
