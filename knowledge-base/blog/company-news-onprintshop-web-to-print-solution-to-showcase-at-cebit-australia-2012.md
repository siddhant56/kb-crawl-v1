# OnPrintShop Web to Print Solution to Showcase at CeBIT Australia 2012

Apr 23, 2012

OnPrintShop web to print solution will be showcasing at CeBIT Australia 2012 to be held at Sydney-Australia. We shall be available at **Stall M43** from May 22-24, 2012.

CeBIT Australia is the largest business event for Australia’s ICT industry. The annual event brings global business leaders, policymakers, and decision influencers on a common platform to debate issues, formulate goals, and form strategic alliances.

OnPrintShop has evolved over a span of 6 years of serving 250 plus global clients. We have worked closely with the global print industry to understand its requirements and challenges.

This would be OnPrintShop’s debut appearance at CeBIT Australia. Previously, we have successfully exhibited at CeBIT 2010, 2011, and 2012 at Germany, PrintPack India, GRAPH EXPO, & IndiaSoft. Moreover OnPrintShop will come to CeBIT Australia straight from drupa 2012, Germany.

At CeBIT Australia, OnPrintShop shall showcase web2print solutions tailored for digital printers, litho printers, print brokers, & design agencies. We shall display our newly launched online photo album and calendar solution, advanced PDF and CorelDraw engines, and advanced variable data printing solution. We shall also conduct live solution demo for our guests at our stall and entertain requests for custom features.

Listening to customers has been OnPrintShop’s biggest strength so far. Our other strengths include:

  * Ability to custom develop features to meet special business requirements.
  * Ability to integrate Microsoft technologies, open source frameworks, embedded technologies, networking solutions.
  * Ability to offer other services of designing, template creation, image editing, & SEO consultancy under single roof.
  * Ability to offer client support across different time zones.

So far the year 2012 has been very successful for OnPrintShop. During the year we:

  * Tied up with Ricoh-Canada for providing web to print solution to SME printers in Canada.
  * Had successful show at CeBIT 2012 Germany.
  * Launched new path breaking solutions like photo album, calendar, and PDF-VT for drupa 2012.
  * Launched mobile-to-print with iPhone & Android compatible designer studio and store.
  * Launched web to print consultancy for SME printers.

We also have Graph Expo coming up later in the year.

Meet us at CeBIT Australia to know what more is in store with OnPrintShop.
