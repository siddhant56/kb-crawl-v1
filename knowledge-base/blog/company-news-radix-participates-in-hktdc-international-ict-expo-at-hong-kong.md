# Radix Participates in HKTDC International ICT Expo at Hong Kong

Mar 25, 2013

Radixweb, a premium IT and technology services provider from India, is making its debut appearance at HKTDC International ICT Expo, at Hong Kong. The expo will be held at Hong Kong Convention & Exhibition Centre, Wan Chai from **April 13-15, 2013** . We shall be available at **Booth 3G-F18** Visitors can pre-register themselves and their requirements to enable us to offer them a personalized experience and project consultation at the expo.

HKTDC International ICT Expo happens to be one of Asia’s leading ICT show. It’s a powerful platform for enterprises from all sectors to come together and exchange ideas and innovations in information and communications technology. With more than 590 exhibitors from 10 countries and regions, the expo connects products and service providers with buyers from different parts of the world.

At HKTDC ICT Expo Radix will offer its specialized services in web and desktop application development, embedded technology development, mobile app development, virtualization services, and WHQL testing services. In application development we will focus on technologies like Dotnet, Kentico, Sitefinity, nopCommerce, etc. In embedded technology we will offer services like:

  * Concept to Prototype Development
  * Custom Hardware & Firmware Development
  * BSP Development
  * Windows & Linux Driver Development
  * Migration to OS or Architecture
  * 64-bit Driver Migration

We will also share details of mobile apps that we have developed like MatchMe memory game, PersonalSMS, Kites, and some other custom apps developed for clients.

At our Booth **3G-F18** , our team will discuss your project requirements and offer advisory services for the same. "Our credentials speak of our technical expertise. But in addition to that we have project management practices in place that help us deliver projects on time and within budget", states **Pratik Mistry, Sr Business Head, Radixweb** .

Radix has credentials like Microsoft Gold Certified Partner, Kentico Certified Partner, Sitefinity Partner, nopCommerce Partner, ISO certified for quality processes, and Adobe Solutions Partner.

In the field of embedded solutions Radix has put up some pioneering work for past few years. We have developed drivers for Windows & Linux systems, Device Driver for USB & PS/2 Mouse, central monitoring systems, security systems, etc. We have also worked on projects involving 32-bit to 64-bit migration.

"We would be happy to share details of our embedded technology experience with expo visitors at our booth", informs **Nitin Vaishnav, chief technology officer, Radixweb**

Other highlighting features of Radixweb as a technology services provider are:

  * 13+ years of market experience
  * A team of 200+ seasoned developers
  * 600+ projects successfully delivered
  * 400+ international clients
  * High secure and state-of-the-art infrastructure
  * Project management and resource scheduling systems
  * Intellectual property protection policy
  * Confidentiality contracts and NDAs
