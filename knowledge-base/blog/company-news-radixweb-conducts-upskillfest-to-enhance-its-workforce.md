# Radixweb Conducts #UpskillFest to Re-Equip Its Workforce for the Digital Age

May 26, 2022

 _**Radixweb, a leading enterprise software development and on-demand dedicated resource hiring firm, has launched #UpskillFest to promote proactive thinking, offer enlightening insights, upskilling, and cross-skilling.**_

Radixweb, a world leader in corporate custom software development, agile transformation, and technology consulting, is committed to speeding up business operations while improving the client's value proposition. The firm takes an agile approach to developing market-driven innovative solutions for clients in a variety of industries and sectors. Radixweb is on a mission to upskill its employees so that their clients can work with unrivaled expertise.

Radixweb is at the vanguard of the ever-changing technology world, where staying relevant is critical. Technology is a constantly changing environment, and CTOs must understand that even technology has a shelf life. Radixweb has determined that keeping its IT force ready and equipped for interruptions is critical.

Bridging the digital divide and a global skills gap is a complicated problem that requires collaboration from all stakeholders to make the world more resilient, capable, and inclusive. Radixweb has launched #UpskillFest 2022 to re-align its people to emerging positions and maintain their people, as well as the quality of services they provide, in the winner's circle.

By upscaling how its people work, engage, and add value to clients, colleagues, and communities, the firm is redefining the hire dedicated resources model. Radixweb launches UpskillFest as the golden gates to nuanced upskilling sessions that help its people navigate the complex tech market while promoting responsibility, capability, and an innovative bent of mind, all while nurturing a culture that promotes self-expression, creativity, and convergence towards the common goal of consistent augmentation.

Radixweb's VP of Business Development, Pratik Mistry, delivers a brief byte. "The goal of UpskillFest is to enable our workers advance on paths that lead to the development of future-ready systems by leveraging the skillsets they acquire." Our people's empowerment has resulted in a huge increase in customer success, which is our ultimate goal. This is a peer-reviewed accreditation that will allow Radixians to progress horizontally as well as vertically in their career graphs with us."

"One of the smartest and most strategic things UpskillFest aims for is bringing on people who don't fit just one position or demand, but can morph roles as client requirements alter," Pratik explains.

"We believe that in order to evolve in line with the present technological landscape, we must begin from the inside out." Only when creative minds come together can real innovation happen. This is where UpskillFest comes in. It's a tribute to our commitment to continuous innovation, reskilling, and, as a result, an unrivaled client experience. We offer everything from legacy application modernization to best-in-class business apps, all while keeping up with changing market demands."

Every day, the company looks for new and inventive ways to empower and engage its workers by providing them with learning opportunities that help them create world–class tech assets for customers. Radixweb has established measurements to accurately measure the program's outcomes, as well as the option of retraining. Radixweb's commitment to cultivating a culture of learning, positive competition, inspiration, and a collaborative exodus towards its people's learning goals is the seed behind UpskillFest.
