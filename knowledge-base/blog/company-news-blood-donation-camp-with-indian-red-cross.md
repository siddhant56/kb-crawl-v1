# Radixweb Extends the Spirit of Giving with a Blood Donation Camp

Jan 5, 2026

 _Radixweb reflects its people-first culture and commitment to social responsibility with a blood donation drive in partnership with Indian Red Cross society._

**Ahmedabad, India, Date: 6th January,2026**

Radixweb, our globally acclaimed tech consulting and software engineering firm, is famed for purpose-driven innovation, building market trust and accountability through responsible development and long-term business associations. Our scope of work extends way beyond simplifying the digital landscape for fast-growing businesses. Our commitment is simple, not just deploying purpose-built software, but contributing positively towards the world – making a true difference with every action.

Advancing this belief, we concluded the year 2025 with a blood donation drive at our corporate headquarters, Ekyarth. Collaborating with the Indian Red Cross Society, we drove a conscious effort towards supporting the cause of community health. This drive was in in faith of reinstating our continuous endeavours to help make lives around us better – for ourselves, our employees and beyond.

At Radixweb, corporate social responsibility is an intrinsic part of our organizational culture. We don’t treat it like a seasonal activity but as a deep organizational culture where the spirit of giving back to the society is a deeply ingrained value. The blood donation camp was one event when our teams across hierarchies came together, pledged and contributed for a cause that gives several lives a new chance at life.

Our COO, Dharmesh Acharya said, “For us at Radixweb, true success isn’t about how many innovations we delivered or the number of clients we bagged. Even in our corporate dealings, we prioritize making a positive impact in our clients’ businesses. Wrapping the year with a strong humanitarian gesture was our way of giving back – a powerful reflection of our values, our sense of responsibility, empathy and the belief that collective action leaves behind true impact.”

Joining hands with a reputed organization like Red Cross was to ensure the highest of pathological and safety standards for our donors – upholding transparency and trust throughout the process. The highlight of the initiative which our people exhibited – participating with enthusiasm, demonstrating a strong sense of ownership and compassion.

We are led by a compassionate leadership team who consider such social responsibilities essential metrics of leading people-centric teams. They wholeheartedly support such acts of service to encourage empathy, strengthen in-house culture and instill a deep sense of purpose in our people.

As our business advances as one of the most trusted names in building software partnerships, we stand thoroughly committed to driving responsibility through every innovation, every action. With our people-first values, ethical practices and community-focussed drives, we deliver impact and bring lasting changes.

So, here’s closing chapter 2025 with a very powerful note – consistency lies in efforts; in building a strong brand or a better society. And we take up equal efforts for both.
