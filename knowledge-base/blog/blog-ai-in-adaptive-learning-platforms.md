## Personalization In Education – Why Adaptive Learning Systems Gained Acceptance

Tailoring services to customer needs initially began as a trend for wider responsiveness. However, the education industry recognized the need for a tailored approach – a shift to make learning more outcome focused and competency-based.

Adaptive learning platforms are perhaps one the most positive and promising applications of AI in the era of ethical implications and other debates. The rise of Gen AI has sparked both excitement and deep-rooted concerns – about data privacy, ethics, bias etc. However, with responsible AI practices, stringent governance and human oversight, the rapidly growing AI ecosystem can be made a responsible one.

In the age of rapid customization where consumers of all age across sectors expect services and solutions to cater to individual needs, AI-led adaptive learning is helping educational businesses take bold strides. The edtech revolution is gradually changing the way businesses looked at educational software development.

In resolving the gaps of traditional education that usually follows a one-size-fits-all approach, adaptive learning adopts elements of AI subsets like ML, Gen AI to make learning experiences aligned with a learner’s progress status, needs and abilities.

The core issue with traditional learning systems is that they fail to analyze a student’s:

  * Comprehension levels
  * Learning pace
  * Interests, strengths and weaknesses

AI-led adaptive learning systems collect, process, analyze and leverage data on a learner’s behavioural aspects and educational performances. The system then uses this data to build personalized learning paths which tailors’ educational content, difficulty level of lessons and delivery patterns – fostering wider course completion.

It doesn’t just boost active learning but also boosts engagement and confidence while ensuring no learner is left behind.

## How AI Powers Adaptive Learning Platforms

Let’s understand this basic fact, AI without training is practically of no use. However, when trained to perform specific tasks, AI leverages machine learning, natural language processing and several other technologies to process, analyze and learn from diverse data sets. This information is then then made available through prompt-based inputs.

Let’s explore how AI works for adaptive learning platforms:

  * It converges several data channels and sources (like assessments, preferences, classroom behaviours, student interactions). The best-in-class software partners will help you build a comprehensive information bank using their Big Data and Gen AI expertise.
  * This comprehensive data bank can be used to build individual learner profiles. And these would be comprehensive enough to pinpoint learning gaps, suggest recommended learning activities, tailor the pace of instructions to match learner speed, adjust difficulty levels as per aptitudes and a lot more.
  * AI-led learning solutions also help instructors build several modules of instruction like audio, videos, graphic images, animations, games, quizzes and several other simulations.
  * If you though AI-led learning is only for schools, you’d be so wrong! It facilitates and boosts learning on so many non-traditional levels like – critical thinking, creativity development, skill development, language learning, knowledge acquisition etc.
  * AI-powered adaptive learning platforms also enable learners be in control of certain aspects in their learning process like choosing their goals, resources and strategies.
  * AI-led educational solutions are also known for deep learner engagement, developing metacognition, self-regulation and wider retention.
  * With automated assessment and grading, educators are relieved of routine tasks and can focus better on building student engagement as well in innovating wider with learning modules.

For building Gen AI-driven adaptive learning platforms, you need a mix of design, paedology and technical skills. Broadly, your software partner needs to have resources with expertise in deep learning, NLP, recommended systems, reinforcement learning along with instructional design, data engineering, cloud computing, API integration and several other skills.

### How secure is the use of generative AI in educational platforms?

The use of AI in any industry doesn’t guarantee security. However, you must look for a partner that prioritizes secure coding practices, follows superior and mature development processes and handles integration issues seamlessly, apart from adhering to global data security regulations.

### What is the difference between traditional AI and generative AI in education?

Traditional AI works like a prompt-based virtual assistant that leverages rules, logic and pattern recognition to deliver decisions. However, gen AI in education functions with deep learning models that delves into the core of user interactions, performs behavioural and sentiment analysis, delivers real-time quizzes, challenges, assessments. It also converges data from these diverse channels to build tailored learning paths.
