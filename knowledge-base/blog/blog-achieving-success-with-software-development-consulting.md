## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a software development consultant do?

A software development consultant evaluates your business needs, technical infrastructure, and project goals to recommend the most efficient path forward. They define project scope, select appropriate technologies, identify risks before they become problems, and oversee the development process to keep delivery on track. Unlike a developer who builds, a consultant guides the entire decision-making chain from planning through deployment.

### How much does software development consulting cost?

Costs vary based on engagement type, consultant seniority, and geography. US-based consultants typically charge between $100 and $250 per hour. Offshore or nearshore consultants range from $40 to $80 per hour. For project-based strategy and scoping engagements, most businesses budget between $15,000 and $100,000 depending on complexity and duration. The cost of consulting is almost always lower than the cost of a failed or misaligned project. Contact Radixweb for a scoped estimate based on your specific requirements.

### When should I hire a consultant instead of a full-time developer?

Hire a consultant when you need strategic direction rather than execution. If your team lacks clarity on what to build, which technology to use, how to structure the project, or why previous attempts have failed, a developer cannot solve that. A consultant addresses the upstream decisions that determine whether development succeeds. Once the strategy, architecture, and roadmap are clear, developers can execute effectively. The two roles complement each other rather than compete.

### How long does a software consulting engagement typically last?

It depends on the scope. A focused discovery or scoping engagement typically runs two to four weeks and results in a technical roadmap, architecture recommendation, and project plan. An ongoing advisory engagement alongside an active development team can run three to twelve months. Some businesses retain consultants on a part-time basis throughout the entire product lifecycle to manage quality, scope, and delivery. Radixweb structures engagements around your specific phase and budget rather than fixed timelines.

### What is the difference between IT consulting and software development consulting?

IT consulting covers the broader technology landscape of an organisation, including infrastructure, cybersecurity, hardware, networks, and operational systems. Software development consulting is specifically focused on the design, planning, and delivery of software products and applications. A software development consultant brings deep knowledge of development processes, architecture patterns, technology stacks, and engineering best practices. Many organisations need both, but they serve different problems. If your challenge is building or fixing a software product, software development consulting is the more targeted solution.

### Can a small or mid-sized business benefit from software development consulting?

Yes, and often more so than large enterprises. Small and mid-sized businesses typically do not have a senior technical leader in-house, which means critical decisions around architecture, vendor selection, and project structure are made without sufficient expertise. A consultant fills that gap without the cost of a full-time hire. Even a short two to four week engagement at the start of a project can prevent months of rework and thousands of dollars in wasted development.
