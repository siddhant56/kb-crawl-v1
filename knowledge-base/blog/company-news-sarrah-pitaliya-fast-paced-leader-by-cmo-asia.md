# CMO Asia and World Woman Leadership Congress Awards Our Leader Ms. Sarrah Pitaliya

Sep 7, 2022

 _**World Woman Leadership Congress and CMO Asia awards Sarrah Pitaliya, Head of Digital Marketing at Radixweb, the Title of Dynamic Leader for her exemplary leadership skills and iconic business expertise in charting hyper-successful brand journeys.**_

The team at Radixweb takes great joy in announcing that one of our own has won an accolade that brings great pride to Radixweb as a company. As Head of Digital Marketing at Radixweb, an industry leader in IT outsourcing, stellar tech consulting, and custom software development, **Ms. Sarrah Pitaliya** has brought yet another accolade to the home team by winning top honors as Dynamic Leader by the **World Women Leadership Council and CMO Awards, 2022**.

Accepting the award, Ms. Sarrah Pitaliya cites Radixweb's commitment to fairness and innovation as the elixir that has driven her to develop into a brand virtuoso in very short span of time. The event honored Ms. Pitaliya for her dedication to fostering innovative brand experiences and exceptional leadership abilities.

For her work in creating intuitive customer journeys with a varied team that has brought a spectacular creative edge to Radixweb's cutting-edge tech solutions, Ms. Sarrah Pitaliya is often recognized as one of the most influential women leaders in the state.

"I am immensely happy to be chosen as one of the dynamic female leaders, and I extend my heartfelt gratitude to my organization, Radixweb, for helping me push the envelope by grooming my leadership skills," Ms. Pitaliya said in her acceptance speech at the World Women Leadership Congress and CMO Asia Awards while flashing a disarming smile.

"Radixweb fosters a dynamic and unbiased work environment, which helps its employees develop their unique talents and prepares them to take on leadership roles in the company's next phase of growth. It values team development, giving everyone the chances they deserve to advance in their careers."

She continues, "At Radixweb, I lead a diverse team located in a number of different geo-locations and representing a wide range of cultural and ethnic backgrounds. We work together to provide seamless customer experiences. Our cutting-edge products have been instrumental in the digital transformation of countless companies globally. At Radixweb, everyone is given the same opportunity to develop. Because my company allowed me to explore my imagination and push the envelope. We planted the seeds for some truly ground-breaking future possibilities, and I am eternally grateful for that. Without the love and encouragement of my Radixweb family, this goal would still be a dream."

According to Ms. Pitaliya, the Great Place To Work Certified Organization shows that Radixweb is committed to its employees and provides them with equal opportunity for advancement regardless of their gender, socioeconomic status, or race. One of the company's basic values is "transparency," Radixweb works to incorporate this concept into all aspects of its operations.

Radixweb's COO, Mr. Dharmesh Acharya, had this to say about Ms. Pitaliya's latest success:

"Sarrah has always been an invigorating force, a prolific leader, and largely responsible for the many successes of our teams in building engaging brand journeys and spurring business growth. She's a natural at finding solutions and driving change, and she's been a strong voice supporting our practice of cultivating lasting professional relationships through flawless technology on numerous global stages. Creating and transforming her strong technical leadership into high-quality bespoke product development, Sarrah has developed deep-rooted relationships with internal and external stakeholders and helped them align with our value system and goals. We are delighted and proud of her success."

In addition to Ms. Pitaliya's decision-making, leadership skills, and fervor for sustainable brand hyper-growth, her neo-modern approach to curating a star line of digital influencers for the future was also honored by the World Women Leadership and CMO Asia.

_"Whenever one of us receives an award, it's not just about celebrating personal achievement. It's about celebrating the people who made it all possible. I celebrate our team, our passion for performing, and our tireless enthusiasm for innovation. This one is for you, and cheers to many more to come", says Ms. Pitaliya as she signs off._
