# HKTDC ICT Expo 2016: Radixweb, OnPrintShop Concludes Successful Participation

Apr 25, 2016

Radixweb, a global Software Development and IT Outsourcing Company and OnPrintShop, leading web-to-print solution provider announced that its 2016 HKTDC ICT show exhibit highlighting the company’s industry leadership, cutting-edge IT services and Innovative solutions, proven IT strategy to overcome new age business challenges, was the successful ICT exhibit. HKTDC ICT Expo 2016 which was held in Hong Kong from April 13-16, is one of the biggest platforms to explore the future technologies; Radixweb stand at HKTDC ICT featured the cutting-edge technology services and solutions along with OnPrintShop - most advanced web-to-print solutions.

Radixweb stand grabbed the attention of key decision-makers and senior leaders from across the globe, who have acclaimed its capabilities to create tailor-made solutions, the wide range of IT outsourcing services, embedded software development, web2print solutions portfolio, and deep industry expertise and transparent approach.

Commenting on Radixweb continued successful participation in global ICT events, Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb, OnPrintShop said: “The massive response to our wide range of innovative technology services and advanced web-to-print solutions, reflecting the increasing confidence of our worldwide customers. We focus on embracing latest technologies, as we keen to support the business automation processes and building the future technology that can achieve business goals.”

> Radixweb IT Consulting Services, Web-to-Print Solutions, Custom Application Development, Embedded Software Development, Field Service Management Hog the Limelight at HKTDC ICT 2016

Dharmesh added: “Through the range of portfolio of OnPrintShop - web to print solutions, we continue our consecutive successes in terms of forming a strong foundation to automate and simplify print operations of print service providers. Our successful participation in the HKTDC ICT 2016 gives us a new responsibility to continue the journey of excellence in developing novel solutions and introducing cutting-edge services to automate, transform and grow business operations.”

“The participation was a success for the company as it fully utilized the platform to exhibit its innovative products and solutions to show attendees. “We have thoroughly enjoyed the exhibit as more and more visitors stopped by at our booth and took an interest in our cutting-edge IT service offerings. It was great to see the key decision makers taking an interest in how to keep pace with the latest tech to stay ahead of the competition. Our participation in an exclusive executive forum where we talked “How to automate and simplify business operations” was indeed one of the fruitful times at the show. We have received immense response throughout the exhibition. During the four-day event, we could build up new relationship and also nourished the old with our clients”, said Naimish Patel, Business Development Manager, Radixweb, OnPrintShop.
