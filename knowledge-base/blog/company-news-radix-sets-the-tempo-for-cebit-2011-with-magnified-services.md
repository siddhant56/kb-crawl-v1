# Radix Sets the Tempo for CeBIT 2011 with Magnified Services

Feb 18, 2011

Radixweb, the Ahmedabad headquartered IT outsourcing giant, is back at CeBIT 2011, **G24-4 Hall 6** , with amplified gusto.

Banking on its resounding success at CeBIT 2010, Radix is all fired up with an expanded kitty of offerings to showcase at CeBIT 2011. Adding more to its expertise, Radix has deepened its roots into web applications, desktop and mobile applications, and embedded and device driver development realms. The company has further made additions to its business ready solutions suite by including real-estate and school management solutions. Besides the PHP, .NET, and Ruby on Rails developers, Radix now offers resource hiring options for Android developers, iPhone developers, and Flex developers.

Radix is a heralded Microsoft Certified Gold Partner, an Adobe Solutions Partner and a respected Intel Software Partner bestowed upon with D&B and Nasscom memberships. Certified with ISO certifications for quality processes, Radix has a bevy of services to offer ranging from application development and maintenance, system programming to enterprise services.

The company had a successful run at CeBIT 2010, world’s premier event for the ICT industry held every year at Hannover, Germany. Unveiling the company plans, Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb stated, “This is our second consecutive participation at CeBIT and we are excited about it. We are confident that our extended services and solutions will catch the eye of the market and garner industry level appreciation.” The company top brass will be present at CeBIT to debate technology issues, form strategic alliances, and share their industry vision and foresight.

While the company had presented itself as an IT outsourcing company at CeBIT 2010, this year Radix will position itself as an IT business partner that not just delivers cost savings but also adds to the bottom line.

See you at CeBIT!
