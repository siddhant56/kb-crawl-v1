## What is Inventory Management?

Inventory management is a key component of supply chain management. Inventory management helps to keep track of a company’s stocked goods and commodities. It is important for wholesale, retail, logistics, and other industries to ensure that the company has enough stock handy to meet all their customer demands. Moreover, inventory management helps solve some of the major manufacturing industry challenges. In simple terms, it acts as an automated inventory tracking system for tracing multiple business operations and collaborating with them to make better and quicker decisions.

Moreover, in these fast-tech times, developing sophisticated inventory management systems fall under crucial software development services.

Now, comes the important question - what is an inventory management system?

## What is Inventory Management System?

The inventory management system is a software solution that tracks every activity in an inventory-targeted place or warehouse. It helps guarantee complete tracking of inventory orders, sales, and deliveries among multiple vendors. Moreover, it helps trace the commodity unit and feed details into the system. Not using inventory management system software in today's tech-fast times might lead to customer disappointment, leading to sales reduction, and the stocking of unnecessary things. Thereby, inventory management system software will help you prevent these situations.

Be it warehouse management, retail management, depots, manufacturing hubs, and anything, inventory management software enhance the credibility of commodity tracking and provide the details immediately. The business or store owner is informed when a particular product or merchandise is about to go out of stock. Therefore, the automated inventory tracking system helps track the product's expiry dates and make rapid and intelligent decisions accordingly.

## Key Features of Inventory Management Software

The following are the major inventory management software features:

  * **Inventory Control**

An efficient inventory control system is one of the best inventory management software feature as it improves the storage space and appropriately allocates different financial resources to your stock.

  * **Complete Reporting and Analytics**

Reporting and analytics using advanced tools help emphasize which goods/products have the highest and lowest turnovers. These important data-based insights impact (majorly help) future stock re-orders.

  * **Purchase Order Generation**

An inventory management system (IMS) automatically builds and transmits purchase orders to vendors outlining the items it requires.

  * **Reorder Point Purchasing**

Reorder point purchasing is also among the important inventory management software features. It informs businesses to reorder inventory, so they won’t run out of stock or get overstocked. It primarily drives the acquisition of a predetermined quantity of refilling inventory.

  * **Order Management**

Inventory management systems software ensures there is adequate stock to meet clients' orders. It's among the best inventory management software features, alerting managers or other staff when stock levels go low. Moreover, it also directly automates the product order from wholesalers before it runs out and inventory gets understocked.

  * **Multi-Warehouse Syncing**

With the help of different features of warehouse software, managers can easily sync inventory data across different retailers’ warehouses and ensure that stock levels are appropriate everywhere.

  * **Multichannel Sales Alignment**

An inventory management system software helps coordinate inventory data entries across different sales channels, so there's no need to estimate stock locations.

  * **Volume Forecasting**

Using all the historical data available, the inventory management system (IMS) can make apt projections and forecasts on probable sales turnover during any specific period.

## Inventory Management Software Types

There were times when inventory management systems include paper folders or spreadsheets that managers had to update manually. Fortunately, people opted for the path of sustainability and advanced technologies, leading to the evolution of inventory management systems software. Now businesses can easily automate most of their inventory management operations with diverse types of inventory management systems.

These are the three types of inventory management systems:

  1. Perpetual Inventory Systems
  2. Periodic Inventory Systems
  3. Manual Inventory Systems

  * **Perpetual Inventory Systems** \- A perpetual inventory system helps track your inventory in real-time. It’s like an automated inventory tracking system. When any commodity is sold, firstly, its bar code is scanned and then it's deleted from the inventory database. Same way, when an inventory receives a new product or good, it’s scanned and then added to the database of the inventory management system. Moreover, every system component can then access the stock information and central database.

An important benefit of a perpetual inventory system is that it provides a detailed view of any changes in your inventory. It also delivers the exact accounting of inventory stock levels eliminating the need for counting inventory manually, which is quite time-consuming. Moreover, it operates efficiently with different business sizes and can integrate with retail software to take care of high sales volume and multiple location inventories.

  * **Periodic Inventory Systems** \- A periodic inventory system utilizes physical inventory counts at pre-established breaks to update inventory. So, companies that use periodic inventory systems will be unaware of the number of products or goods it sustains until after the completion of the physical inventory count.

However, this approach to tracking inventory is quite impractical for huge retail organizations. Wondering why? Since it's all about processing the orders, you rely solely on a manual stock count taken a few weeks or months back. So, whenever there's a client demand for any product, someone requires to physically check the inventory to look out if the item is available to sell. This is the reason a periodic inventory system only functions well with small businesses having lower inventory quantity.

  * **Manual Inventory Systems** \- A manual inventory system is an old-school way to manage your inventory. Manual inventory systems are practically based on pen and paper or even a spreadsheet. While this may be a practical option if your business's monthly sales are in the single digits, it’s not a feasible and sustainable choice for expanding businesses. Large vendors or retailers will need a more advanced and robust option.

## Disadvantages of Inventory Management Software

While there are a series of benefits coming with inventory management software, there are some drawbacks within the software as well. The following are some drawbacks of inventory management system software:

**1\. Potential System Crashes -** System crashes are the major challenge to computerized and automated inventory tracking systems. A technical issue, power outage, or a corrupt hard drive can all result in data loss and, at times, can also shut the entire inventory management process down.

**2\. Reduced Physical Audits** \- Less physical audits can also be an issue since it minimizes the overall in-person interaction with your inventory. It is no big deal to decrease the number of physical audits as computers are responsible for all the inventory management functions. However, it’s still significant to conduct physical audits occasionally to examine and identify any spoilage or breakage. Moreover, audits also help managers or vendors to identify manipulation of data in inventory control systems or any possibility of internal thefts.

## FAQs

### What are the 4 types of inventory management?

There are four types of inventory management: Just-in-Time Management (JIT), Materials Requirement Planning (MRR), Economic Order Quantity (EOQ), and Day Sales of Inventory (DSI).

### What is an example of an inventory system?

An example of an inventory system is "Fishbowl Inventory." It's a popular inventory management software designed for small and midsize businesses. Fishbowl Inventory offers features such as inventory tracking, order management, warehouse management, and reporting capabilities to help enterprises efficiently manage their inventory and streamline operations.

### Why is inventory management important?

Inventory management is vital as it enables businesses to accurately assess their inventory levels, make informed ordering decisions, and fulfill customer demands promptly. Inventory management is crucial for maintaining optimal stock levels, controlling costs, and enhancing customer satisfaction.

### How much does an inventory system development cost?

Based on Radixweb’s industry experience, the cost to develop an inventory management system may vary from $50,000 to $200,000+.

### What should a basic inventory control system include?

A basic inventory control system should include inventory tracking, item management, barcode scanning, stock alerts, inventory reports, user access control, integration capabilities, and mobile accessibility. These features help businesses to manage inventory levels, track stock movements, and streamline inventory-related tasks.

### What is the difference between inventory control and inventory management?

Inventory control focuses on the processes and systems used to maintain accurate inventory levels, track stock movements, and prevent stockouts or overstocking. In contrast, inventory management encompasses a broader range of activities, including forecasting, procurement, and optimization of inventory-related processes to maximize efficiency and profitability.
