# Radixweb Brings the House Down at COMEX 2017 with Smashing Success

Apr 4, 2017

 _**With visitors cheering for futuristic technology tailored to meet business needs, Radixweb continued the success streak with an overwhelming response at largest ICT event in Muscat from 28th to 30th March**_

Radixweb at Booth #717 became the hub spot for COMEX visitors to know how to leverage advanced technologies like Field Service Automation, Mobility and Cloud computing to achieve the competitive edge in the market.

With a great number of attendees visiting the booth with a genuine interest in the show stopper exhibits, Radixweb reaped huge benefits of being a regular and well-known brand at the event. The company was represented by versatile tech trio of Pratik Mistry (Sr. Business Head), Maitray Gadhavi and Nihar Raval (Sr. Business Development Managers).

Field Service Automation was a unanimous hit this year, as most of the visitors were actively looking for process automation and mobile solutions to ease their field operations and manage a gigantic workforce with a centralized infrastructure. With a secured field service management, it is easier to overcome the challenges of traditional or manual systems and increase ROI.

> The time in Oman is right to make smart IT investments. With the prevalent market condition, business owners are looking to cut corners in all possible ways, but not at the risk of ignoring technology.

Explaining this further, Mistry says, “Owing to the current economy slowdown in Oman, the most obvious approach for every industry stakeholder is to reduce costs. Businesses, who are here to stay, are making smart investments in technology to reduce operational costs and stay ahead in the race.”

Apart from the change in IT investment behavior, one notable change is the increased adaptation of bespoke solutions. Rather than choosing one-solution-that-fits-all, business owners in Oman are steadily moving to customized solutions to make the most out of their tech needs.

“Oman is a very closely knit market. Here your credibility can help you win the game. With a strong word-of-mouth market behavior, your long term association showcases your authenticity as an industry leader in the market. Radix having been successfully associated with many leading names in Oman like Assarain group, one of the leading conglomerates in Oman helped us to win the initial trust from our guests,” says Mistry.

Adding to the coterie of successes at Oman, was the presence of Radixweb at an event organized by Indian Embassy in Muscat. In the presence of HE Mr. Indra Mani Pandey, Indian Ambassador at Oman, Radixweb took center-stage to showcase its innovative leadership in futuristic technology to an audience that was deeply rooted in the technology market.

For Radixweb being prominent in the field of global IT Outsourcing and Services, COMEX serves to be the best platform to strengthen and expand our footprints in Middle East region.
