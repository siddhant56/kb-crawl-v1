# Radixweb Earns Global Honors: OA500 Top 200 2025 and Software Suggest Service Excellence Company

Sep 11, 2025

 _**Summary:**_ _Globally acknowledged platform Software Suggest recently recognized us as a Service Excellence Company. We’ve also been acknowledged in the Top 200 provider list by the OA500 Global Outsourcing Firm Index 2025. These recognitions speak of our strong foothold in AI-led innovation, data engineering, product development and solution excellence across 30+ industries._

**Ahmedabad, Date: 12th September 2025**

Double round of appreciations came in for us recently – hallmark labels that applaud our capabilities in leading new-age, AI-led product innovation, service capabilities for a wide range of industries like Edtech, Healthtech, Fintech, HRtech, supply and manufacturing; and excellence in meeting global outsourcing demands.

Software Suggest, the globally recognized software provider listing firm, recognized us as a **Service Excellence Company**. The second recognition comes from the Global Outsourcing Firm Index which honours us with a prestigious place in **OA500 Top 200 Providers** list among leading global names.

These globally famed industry recognitions and badge of honours represent our foundational capabilities in rapid innovation with next-gen tech, driving accurately paced shift for diverse industry segments, frictionless delivery mechanisms and building disruption-resistant software solutions.

## OA500 Top 200 Provider List – A Recognition of Our Forward-Thinking Excellence

The Outsource Accelerator indexed over 3000 BPOs globally this year and annoted the most promising names in its Top 200 list. Radixweb was indexed in 176th place with an OA score of 48.13 beside billion-dollar businesses. This represents our prominence in delivering value to businesses of various scales with tech-first, reasonably priced solutions.

The OA500 ranking methodology comprises two distinct metrics – visible footprint and quality of solutions delivered by businesses. From proprietary research, third-party information and customer satisfaction scores – the platform aggregated every parameter to identify stakeholders for its Top 200 list. Being featured on this list thus solidifies our market leadership as a next-gen product development partner helping businesses of different sizes to evolve strategically in an era of AI, multi-cloud adoption, and cloud-native solutions.

## Software Suggest Service Excellence Company – Pioneering Benchmark Standards in Service Excellence

The Software Suggest Service Excellence Award 2025 highlights our unwavering commitment to fulfilling client expectations through tailored, future-ready solutions and strong support models. This achievement underscores our company’s agile delivery approaches, trusted client collaborations, and devotion to delivering tangible business impact.

It also applauds our dedication to ethical innovation establishing us as a trusted partner in multiple industrial verticals where trust, accountability, and compliance are imperative – such as public services, finance, education, healthcare and many others.

This Service Excellence benchmark also celebrates our extensive approach in ethical AI and automation integration capabilities for building ecosystems where solutions are not only innovative but also highly secure, portable, and versatile.

These two esteemed honors highlight a breakthrough in our journey of AI-driven transformation, client-first service, and relentless innovation. While Software Suggest accelerates our reputation in service excellence for 30+ industries, OA500 Top 200 again positions us as grade 1 disruptors in technology who challenge conventional tech reasoning and yet innovate with sustainability in mind.”
