# Radixweb Celebrates 15th Anniversary as a Global Leader in Software Development & IT Outsourcing Services

Jul 20, 2015

**Radixweb, the Industry Leading Technology Services Provider, is celebrating 15 Years of Successfully Meeting and Exceeding Customer Requirements in Rapidly Advancing IT Outsourcing Industry**

Radixweb, a leading software outsourcing company, announces today the completion of 15 glorious years of journey into software development and the IT Outsourcing industry. A decade and half of experience has transformed Radixweb from a startup with big ideas into a successful holding company that leading software development companies and thousands of clients rely on for IT solutions/services that improve their business growth.

In the industry, which is recognized for its harsh competition, Radix has established its strong presence. Given its remarkable position in the industry, it is difficult to believe that Radix had an unsure beginning with only a few people. Fifteen years ago, when Radix founders joined hands together to discover the offshore development market, nobody had thought that their effort, named Radixweb, would sustain for this many years. Today, Radixweb’s innovative IT solutions and services are being used by leading businesses across the world.

Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb excitedly commented: “Radixweb’s 15th anniversary is a tremendous milestone for the company, and we are thankful to our clients for making this milestone anniversary possible. We’ve always looked for better ways to develop things, whether it’s by creating our fastest growing flagship product OnPrintShop – Web-to-Print E-commerce – implementing our deep understanding of software development into global market, or through our unique IT solutions for automating businesses. We’ve established a truly long-term partnership with our clients, which is empowering their teams to prompt their own exclusive ideas using our technology and to accomplish an extraordinary level of technical superiority. We set the bar only by simplifying IT outsourcing. I am greatly proud to have a huge team of people who work toward perfection in what they do and insistently deliver best-in-class offshore outsourcing services to our clients worldwide.”

“I would like to congratulate Radixweb for achieving 15 successful years in business. The Compliance Group has been working with Radixweb for over 5 years and we value their contribution to our overall SaaS solution. The designers, developers and project managers have provided us with excellent service in supporting the development of our HR, HS and Payroll solution, which truly sets them apart from other service providers. I look forward to continuing to work in partnership with Radixweb. Once again, congratulations on your first fifteen years in business," said Paul Jones of Compliance Group, UK.

Today, Radixweb represents an impressive portfolio of IT and technology related services, which include Application Development, Cloud porting of Business Application and Migration & Redevelopment of Business Applications. “We are proud developing superior technology, cultivating a corporate environment of partnership and innovation, and meeting commitments made to our customers. Look forward to continuing our successful journey in IT Outsourcing industry”, Nitin Vaishnav, CTO, Radixweb.
