# Radixweb Celebrates RXConfab 2022 to Ensure #InnovationYouDeserve

Jul 10, 2022

 _**At RXConfab 2022, Radixweb’s 22nd anniversary gala, the firm celebrates a shared purpose of #InnovationYouDeserve, a promise that ensures only the very best tech, service, solutions and support. With RXConfab 2022, Radixweb states it’s position as a culture capital and a market leader in the tech sphere.**_

As Radixweb's 22nd anniversary and the company's largest gala of the year, RXConfab 2022, draws near, the company is celebrating an eclectic mix of innovation, drive, and curiosity to venture beyond what is established. These are the cornerstones that define the Radixweb ethos. RXConfab 2022 marks the company's 22nd anniversary with a promise of #innovationYouDeserve. Radixweb is getting ready to commemorate its unconquerable will and the determination that has allowed the company to triumph over every obstacle.

Radixweb has, in the 22 years since it first set out to conquer the pinnacles of the software development industry, created a diverse, multicultural identity that its people, customers, and partners can depend and rely on.
