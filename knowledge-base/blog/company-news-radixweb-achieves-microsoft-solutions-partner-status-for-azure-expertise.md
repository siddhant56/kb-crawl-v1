# Radixweb Joins the Elite: Named Microsoft Solutions Partner for Azure Digital & App Innovation

Jun 7, 2023

 _And again, let’s raise the curtain from the latest milestone of our journey!_

With immense delight, we would like to announce that we have been named as one of the leading innovators in the recently launched Microsoft Cloud Partner Program. Through this program, we have strengthened our back with Microsoft Cloud Solutions Partner for Digital & App Innovation (Azure). This recognition marks a momentous achievement in our journey towards providing innovative digital solutions to the global marketplace.

Last year, Microsoft retired the prior Gold and Silver partnerships and introduced new **Microsoft Solutions Designations**. We are proud to have achieved one of these prestigious designations in digital and app innovation, aligning with Azure expertise. This newfound designation highlights our capabilities and firm determination to provide faster, more resilient, and reliable solutions our clients require to thrive.

**As Satya Nadella, CEO of Microsoft, once said, "At the end of the day, our success is measured by our customers' success. Performance and customer excellence go hand in hand."**

We, at Radixweb, a trusted Microsoft Azure development company, have been delivering tech modernization and scalable solutions since 2000. Our cross-functional teams consistently deliver cloud-based applications, leveraging the capabilities of Microsoft’s Azure DevOps with great expertise. With a track record of successfully delivered 4200+ cutting-edge solutions to global brands, we employ a unique value proposition methodology.

Becoming a Microsoft Solutions Partner signifies that we are now a part of an elite group of businesses that have met Microsoft's exact standards in delivering top-notch solutions. This partnership empowers our capabilities to accelerate digital transformation on the future-ready Azure platform, enhancing business agility and driving operational efficiency for our clients.

Moreover, this esteemed partnership expands our customer reach through the expansive Microsoft Network, acknowledging our competence in high-demand Microsoft Cloud Solutions areas. It gives us the ability to deliver innovative applications that make the most of cloud computing benefits.

Our industry-wide experience in healthcare, finance, and retail makes us an ideal partner for businesses seeking innovative cloud solutions for their challenges.

This partnership with Microsoft comes with an expansive range of rewards and prioritizations. It empowers our organization to transform legacy applications into a competitive edge, resulting in maximum ROI, and value realization for clients while delivering cost efficiency. Additionally, we gain access to premium Microsoft technical support to proactively assist our clients with their specific issues, providing customer-centric solutions with innovation.

Celebrating this esteemed designation with Microsoft Cloud Solutions for Digital & App Innovation, **our COO, Mr. Dharmesh Acharya added** , “This certification validates our expertise and zeal to deliver innovative solutions using the Microsoft Cloud platform. For over two decades, we have been on our feet to provide app modernization and create satisfactory cloud-native app experiences in ever evolving business landscape. Our teams are highly invested in simplifying complex applications with ease and leveraging security, scalability, and cloud advancements for our client’s accelerated business growth. Furthermore, we all are excited to leverage the benefits of this newfound Microsoft partnership to empower our clients with Azure innovation that drives growth, maximizes their tech ROI, and enables digital success.”

“Undoubtedly, this partnership comes from the unwavering efforts of our exceptional talent pool. We are backed by 650+ innovative minds who consistently strive to assist businesses to embark on their digital transformation journeys, standing by their side as a **trusted software development partner**,” he mentioned.

Together with Microsoft, we are exclusively positioned to assist global clientele in developing, functioning, and managing applications across multi-cloud infrastructure, on-premises, and at the edge. As an emerging talent force, our commitment to excellence is evident in our continuous investment in upskilling across modern technologies. With a deep focus on integration, innovation, and technology, we strive to bring long-run success to both us and our global clients by delivering secure, stable, and future-ready solutions.
