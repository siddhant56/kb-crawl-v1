# Radixweb Secures SOC 2 Compliance: Strengthening Enterprise Trust and Risk Resilience

May 19, 2025

 _**Summary** \- We proudly announce Radixweb’s compliance with SOC 2 under AIPAC’s Trust Services Criteria, which affirms our relentless commitment as a trusted partner in data security, privacy, and data processing integrity. This achievement solidifies our position as a secure software engineering and responsible innovation partner. By aligning with SOC 2 standards, we help our clients to strengthen their data security architecture and abide by exemplary compliance standards._

Ahmedabad, Date: 20th May,2025

Since 2000, Radixweb has been trailblazing in software product development and engineering, constantly redefining digital security for modern enterprises. In line with our commitment to streamline operational data with steadfast discipline, integrity, and transparency, we’re proud to announce our compliance with AIPAC’s SOC 2 standards—a testament to our advancing security-focused approach.

With purpose-driven strategies to optimize how data is accessed, processed, and enacted through AI-based software solutions, we maintain high-level conformity across AIPAC’s five Trust Services Criteria—security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

They extensively analysed our control environment by assessing our continuous monitoring frameworks, remediation initiatives, comprehensive gap analysis, and readiness assessment.

This certification highlights our dedication to security-focused engineering, integrating advanced, top-tier data security standards to protect us and our clients' sensitive information. As a digital transformation partner for over 3,000 global businesses, our experts acknowledge the lightning-fast speed at which technology evolves, often outpacing sustainability practices.

In response to the rising cybersecurity threats, we are dedicated to upgrading the defence mechanisms with sophisticated software development approaches, a well-defined tactic for remediation, and proactive threat detection, which ensures resilience in a growingly complex threat environment.

Our COO, Darmesh Acharya, shared, “Given the constantly elevated threat landscape for evolving digital ecosystems, data security becomes a significant component for stable innovation. The SOC 2 compliance confirms our long-standing approach to security-first designs”.

He added, “This certification is only a high-standard validation of our core policies on data privacy and maintaining confidentiality. Our clients have always appraised our stance on maintaining data integrity. But this stamp does pave the way for more comprehensive defence and innovation on the grounds of threat detection, response, and management.”

As your dedicated partner in technology solutions, we ensure your projects are supported by robust, proven security measures, all backed by globally acknowledged certifications. Our focus on developing security-first, scalable solutions is rooted in a commitment to client trust, rather than passing industry fads. Also certified in ISO 27001, we consistently abide by global and regional data protection regulations to help our clients fully comply with the standards pertinent to their field.

At Radixweb, innovation aligns with reliability. Driving SaaS transformation in over 30 industries, we don't just deliver future-proof solutions—we engineer trust into every line of code. Our SOC 2 certification goes beyond meeting compliance goals; it's a clear stand that our clients’ digital and intellectual data is protected with enterprise-level security. Driven by a legacy of reliability, we set new standards of excellence, confidence, compliance, and pioneering transformation.

We have steadily adhered to our commitment to the highest standards by strategically investing in the optimization of our security architecture and risk management practices. This includes a powerful blend of extensive in-house training, realistic incident response simulations, and regular internal audits, ensuring the supreme readiness and endurance level.

With the achievement of SOC Type 2 compliance, we are now well-equipped to amplify our global footprints. We are proficient in handling the most intricate tech problems, delivering secure, reliable, and advanced solutions to meet the needs of enterprise-grade clients while maintaining trust and dependable conduct.
