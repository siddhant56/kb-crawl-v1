# Radixweb Highlights the “Way-Forward” for Futuristic Technologies at Oman

Mar 29, 2017

Radixweb makes its footprint loud in Middle-East Showcasing its thought leadership in Cloud, IoT and Mobile by providing valuable insights on how evolving technology impacts business models and how to add greater value with mutual collaboration

The stage was all set for energy to flow in all directions at the Indian Embassy in Sultanate of Oman, last evening. With the bureaucratic big-wigs and technocrats at one single place, Radixweb took center-stage and gave words to its vision in front of an enthused and experienced audience.

Giving a glimpse of Radixweb’s association with Oman, Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Manager stated that, “Over the past few years, we have been closely associated with Oman and Middle East region owing to our evident client base. It helps us well to discern the tech needs and customer behavior in this region. The cherry on the cake is that this year marks the hat trick of our presence at COMEX, the leading ICT event in Muscat.”

He added that IT environment in Middle East region is significantly adaptive of trend-setting technologies like Enterprise Mobility, Cloud and IoT. Moving a step further, choosing the right partner like Radixweb is a prerogative to transform adaptive behaviors into maximum business value.

Speaking more about the company, Mistry stressed further that Radixweb has achieved the high pedestal of a global leader in converting disruptive technologies to growth machine business systems. “In Oman, we are looking for the right collaborations and opportunities that will take our association forward to have mutual growth between both India and Oman,” added Mistry. The event was rightfully graced by His Excellency Mr. Indra Mani Pandey, the Indian Ambassador at the Embassy.

The team comprising of Maitray Gadhavi and Nihar Raval, Sr. Business Development Managers also shared a quick discussion with His Excellency after the event.

**Radixweb at COMEX 2017:**

Tech trio of Radixweb is participating in COMEX 2017 for third time in a row from Mar 28- 30, 2017. The team is helping visitors to leverage of best of Cloud, Mobility and IoT for success in their business. With successful portfolio in various industries, Radixweb is also aiming to solve needs of legacy application modernization, software maintenance, enterprise software development and Dynamics 365 development.
