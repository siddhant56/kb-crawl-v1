## Software Testing Industry Statistics

Software testing plays a crucial role in ensuring the quality and reliability of software products. Industry statistics provide valuable insights into various aspects of software testing, such as testing techniques, automation, defect detection, and more. We can better understand the software testing industry's trends, challenges, and best practices by analyzing statistics. So, let's dive in and explore the fascinating world of software testing statistics together.

  1. Software testing is expected to reach a market size of USD 52.25 billion by the end of 2024 and increase at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 7% from 2024 to 2032.
  2. With a 28.5% share, the banking, insurance, and financial services industries dominate the European software testing market. Between 2019 and 2024, 11,000 testing agencies have grown at a CAGR of 2.9% in Europe.
  3. China's market for software testing is expected to develop at a 4.5% CAGR, while the US market is anticipated to be worth $12.1 billion. Japan and Canada are two more significant geographic markets, with growth rates of 4.1% and 3.9%, respectively, expected by 2030.
  4. Quality assurance is taking center stage in the development budget, with an expected allocation of approximately 40% of the overall cost.

## Test Automation Stats for Software Testing

Test automation has become increasingly popular in recent years regarding software testing. It offers several benefits, such as enhanced efficiency, faster time to market, and enhanced test coverage. Some exciting statistics highlight the impact of test automation in software testing. Let's look at them.

  1. 24% of the organizations that made test automation investments said that they saw an immediate return on investment. Within the first six months, another 24% of respondents saw an increase in ROI, and within a year, 28% saw a positive ROI. Merely 9% of the participants reported unsatisfactory results.
  2. 5% of the businesses sign up for fully automated testing. Plus, 73% aim to achieve a 50:50 or 25:75 balance, and almost 14% aspire to eliminate manual testing.
  3. The statistics of quality assurance testing show six typical roles in QA, each requiring a different level of expertise based on the testing needs. The prominent roles in employee software testing are test manager, test engineer, test automation engineer, software developer in test, and test architect.
  4. Exploration testing is a commonly used methodology; approximately 82% of the firms use it, while 61% use traditional scripting.
  5. From 2020 to 2030, employment prospects in software development, quality assurance, and testing will increase by 22%, outpacing the average growth rate for all occupations.

With the rapid advancements taking place, as a business, you need to note that more and more solutions are used significantly to develop high-quality software; thus, the testing landscape continues to evolve. Let us walk you through the software testing future trends.
