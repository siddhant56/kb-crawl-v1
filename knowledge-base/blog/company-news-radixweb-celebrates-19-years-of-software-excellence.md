# Get… Set… Tech: Radixweb Finishes 19 Laps on the Innovation Race Track

Jul 16, 2019

**Trusted Software Development Partner Radixweb Celebrates Neony Nineteen Years of its ‘Stunner TechRunners’**

They Say- Run often. Run long but never outrun your joy of running. Precisely signifies the current mood at Radixweb. The company, widely known for its deep focus on providing cutting edge technology solutions, is on the path to complete 19 years in technology industry- is celebrating and gearing up to move on to the Next Decade.

As another year winds down, all of the ‘Stunner TechRunners’ at Radixweb take a moment to deeply thank their sterling community of customers and partners. In an industry where changes happen in the blink of an eye, the strength and endurance of a technology company is often unpredictable.

However, Radixweb is known to have the keen eye to spot technology trends early, understanding the relevant ones and customizing it finely into their customers’ business and operational models.

As the company mounts to begin next decade, it showcases the endurance and grit of a skilled marathon runner on the tracks of ever changing technology landscape. Sprinting auratic innovations for small to Fortune 500 companies- Radixweb shapes businesses with custom software development, IT consulting, web-to-print solutions, and embedded software development for 2900+ global companies with strength of 410+ whacking brains.

Expressing his elation, Dharmesh Acharya, COO at Radixweb says, ‘It’s been 19 relentless years in the world of technology- bringing an indelible impact on global customers to achieve their goals and visions of success- while we became better with each challenge. Innovation in technology is similar to evolution- and the one who is most adaptive to change rules.’

> We will be Obsessed to Make Technology Outsourcing- Simple, Simple and Simple for our customers. – Dharmesh Acharya, COO at Radixweb

In a dynamically changing scenario, how does his company never loses sight of the horizon, Acharya says, ‘Technology is drastically changing the way businesses work- and demanding to move ahead with speed and agility. As technology partners, we provide the required momentum, confidence and grit to embrace the new in these times to our customers.’

Thanking his customers and his teams, he adds, ‘We believe that it is not about winning clients- it is about nurturing strong partnerships with customers and teams.’

Echoing similar sentiments, Pratik Mistry, Senior Business Head at Radixweb says, ‘From now for the next new decade- our vision and unwavering commitment to elevate our customer’s comfort to a new level harnesses more power. We have delivered incredible work to make a positive impact on client’s businesses and our willingness to continue doing the same- only strengthens with each passing day.’

> With your trust and our expertise- we continue to build what we are today- Radixweb

Happy and gutsy customers of Radixweb have already started pouring in more cheer. Whether it is seamless communication, adherence to deadlines and stabilizing businesses with top notch solutions- Radixweb has gained unfailing trust in its capabilities.

Listen to Doug Dratch and Jonathan Feldman, from a leading printing and packaging firm.

Inception Days are the perfect time to- pause look back at the journey and the road ahead, while anticipating what’s next. And celebrate a lot more.

Marking this milestone with neony nineteen and stunning aura, the technology services company is set to host a gala event- coined as ‘RxConfab’ on the D-day. From here- the next chapter and decade is set to begin with full gusto, zing and enthusiasm to bring customers greater success.

Pacing up relentlessly in a high pressure race without the ribbon in sight, it is vividly evident that Radixweb have enough legs to go any distance.
