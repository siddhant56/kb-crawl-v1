# Radixweb Earns Inc. Power Partner Award 2023 for Powering the Future of Software Development

Dec 13, 2023

 _**Quick Summary:** Drumrolls, please! We have reached yet another milestone in the dynamic world of software development! Inc. Business Media recognized Radixweb as a Power Partner 2023 for relentless innovation in software development. This prestigious honor showcases our commitment to strategic innovation and surpassing expectations. With 98% client retention since our inception in 2000, we proudly celebrate receiving the Inc. Power Partner Award, solidifying our position as a software partner of choice in driving advancements._

December 14, 2023 – Ahmedabad, India – With great pleasure, we announce that we, at Radixweb, have received the Inc. Power Partner Award 2023 in recognition of our consistent supremacy in the software development category. This win is not only an affirmation of our past performance but also serves as fuel to constantly push boundaries, and strategic innovation, and refine our tech prowess to help our clients maximize their tech investments.

This esteemed honor was conferred to us after a rigorous evaluation drive by Inc., where they connected with a handful of our clients from different global locations. During this evaluation process, our clients' feedback responses on how we've helped them scale, met their business needs, and helped them explore untapped possibilities, have been the driving force of being chosen for this recognition. We take pride in witnessing our endeavors and values shine through in the real-time feedback assessment by Inc., showcasing our commitment to elevating software solutions beyond client expectations.

Securing this recognition is no small feat as 389 service providers were examined across 100 categories. And we proudly emerged as one of Inc.'s "Power Partner" in the software development category. We owe this recognition to our unique value proposition that addresses customer’s problem statements through strategic innovation and frictionless solutions. The Inc. Power Partner Award not only celebrates our progress in software development but also highlights our exceptional partnership excellence and collaborative spirit for delivering 360-degree value with each tech investment.

Since our inception in 2000, we have been a dominant player in the software development outsourcing market. We leverage our deep domain expertise to develop fit-for-purpose and best-of-breed solutions that fulfill market demand and solidify our position as a trusted tech leader across all dynamic SMEs to Fortune 500 companies. We've earned the reputation of being the “software partner of choice”, a distinction we are honored to be recognized time and time again.

"Being in the spotlight at the Inc. Power Partner Awards and ranking among the most innovative and influential B2B service providers is more than just an achievement for us," says our COO, Dharmesh Acharya. “We always held a vision of innovation and thrive on understanding the unique needs and challenges of our clients, offering tailored solutions that not only meet but exceed expectations. Using our core values of collaboration, innovation, and delivering exceptional results as touchstones, we ensure our clients know they are our valued partners.”

He adds, "We hold the trust our clients place in our software development and outsourcing capabilities in the highest regard. To honor that trust, we ensure the delivery of optimal results from their tech investments. Through constantly refining our skills and expertise with emerging technologies, we position ourselves to serve our clients with ever-improving capabilities."

At Radixweb, our revolutionary innovations span across Fintech, Automotive, Real Estate, Health Tech, InsureTech, EduTech, and web app development. We are dedicated to designing seamless customer journeys, exemplified by the successful launch of Vision Customer Xcellence. Born from collaboration with clients, this initiative serves as a vast feedback mechanism, pinpointing skill gaps within our in-house resources and planning major upskilling initiatives.

_**We're not just a provider – we're your trusted partner on the journey to bigger and better successes.**_

To better understand the challenges faced by our clients, we have stationed our most versatile senior resource permanently in our USA office for in-person collaboration.

Recognizing the importance of cultural understanding, we have also appointed a Regional Head based in the USA with an impressive 20 years of experience in scaling SaaS businesses. This addition enables us to bridge any cultural gaps that may arise, ensuring seamless execution of projects.

For more than two decades, we have established ourselves as a trusted software development service provider to over 3000 clients worldwide with a 98% retention rate. Our dedication to software development innovation is second to none. We embrace a strategic and sustainable approach, harnessing the true potential of technology for ourselves and our clients.

It is with great pride that we have been recognized with the prestigious Inc. Power Partner Award, highlighting our significant contributions to the field of software development.
