# Radixweb Launches Enterprise Data Security Solutions to Help UK Organizations Stay Compliant and Secure

Feb 15, 2026

Frisco, Texas, USA – Date: 16th February 2026

With cyber threats driven by AI on the rise, and regulatory demands becoming tougher, organizations in the UK really need more than just basic security measures. The need-of-the-hour is a ready-to-go solution that fits UK compliance standards and is easy to implement, even at enterprise scale. To deliver that, at Radixweb, we are doubling down on our enterprise data security solutions tailored for the UK market.

Data security has always been central to our approach to software development. What’s new is that our services for UK businesses now include security solutions that are specifically tailored to meet UK regulations and compliance needs. This security isn't patched on later, but integrated right into our software development process from the get-go.

Industry studies have shown that just half of UK businesses fully comply with data privacy laws. That’s mainly because of problems like outdated systems, processes that don’t connect well, and not having real-time visibility. This makes them vulnerable to cyber threats, operational glitches, and could lead to serious reputational or financial issues. With regulations like the UK GDPR and ISO 27001 out there, managing risks is not optional but essential. We want to help organizations tackle these challenges head-on by providing the clarity, tools, and support they need.

## Bringing Clarity to Complex Cyber Risk

“Many organizations invest heavily in cybersecurity tools,” said Dharmesh Acharya, COO at Radixweb, “but without a clear understanding of where the real risks lie, those investments often fall short. Our enterprise data security solutions give UK businesses a complete view of their risk landscape. That way, they can make informed decisions, close compliance gaps, and protect sensitive data, all while supporting growth and innovation.”

Our data governance consulting combines technology and expertise into a single, streamlined engagement that balances compliance, security, and operational efficiency. Some of the key features include:

  * **Comprehensive Risk Assessment** : We evaluate systems, processes, and applications to pinpoint vulnerabilities and compliance gaps, down to the smallest details.
  * **Regulatory Alignment:** Ensuring your organization meets UK GDPR, ISO 27001, NCSC guidelines, and other industry-specific requirements.
  * **Advanced Data Protection Measures:** Implementing encryption, access controls, monitoring, and incident response protocols to safeguard sensitive data.
  * **Threat Detection and Rapid Response:** Proactively monitoring and quickly addressing potential security incidents.
  * **Operational Resilience and Governance:** Strengthening policies and procedures so your organization stays audit-ready, scalable, and secure every day.

## Reliable Results Across Sectors and Domains

We've seen our data security solutions create impact across industries, even compliance-heavy domains like finance, healthcare, and logistics. For example, there was a consultancy from the UK that reached out to us for a tool that aids in procurement negotiations. We developed that tool without running into any compliance issues, and it helped them speed up their processes fivefold while also reducing errors by 90%. With all their data consolidated, the team can easily manage, track, and safeguard their information whenever necessary, which not only enhances compliance but also improves their overall efficiency.

We help organizations see their risks clearly, offer practical advice, and present step-by-step options for rolling things out. This way, they can focus on the biggest risks first and build their defenses effectively across all their systems.

## Driving Trust, Growth, and Digital Resilience

Our data security solutions go beyond just checking compliance boxes. When UK-specific compliance and security are baked into our software development services, companies can feel confident as they innovate, broaden their offerings, and dive into digital projects. Plus, they can relax knowing their data is safe and that they're sticking to all the necessary regulations.

“Data security and compliance aren’t just operational necessities,” added Acharya. “They’re real enablers of trust, growth, and long-term resilience. With our solutions, UK organizations can turn risk management into a foundation for success.”

Radixweb is here to help organizations navigate the tricky landscape of cybersecurity and compliance. With our 25+ years of hands-on experience in using advanced technology and our solid track record, we are ready to help UK businesses feel secure, confident, and sustainable in the digital landscape today.
