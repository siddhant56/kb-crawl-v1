# MS Dynamics CRM in Limelight at Radixweb Booth #517 COMEX OMAN 2016

Apr 6, 2016

**Offering MS Dynamics CRM Consulting, Customizations and Implementation Tailored to Business Specific Needs and Industry Trends**

Radixweb a global leader in software development and next-generation IT outsourcing services today announced that it will exhibit its competencies for MS Dynamics CRM at COMEX 2016, Muscat, Oman during April 12-16 2016 at booth #517 to help Middle East businesses to deliver startling customer experiences across the span of sales, marketing, service and social engagements.

The business processes are experiencing a paradigm shift mainly due to a consistent pressure to meet changing customer demands and staying align to technology changes. With cloud technologies and advanced analytical tools, it is now easy to store, process and analyze a large volume of data at lower cost, which can harvest great benefits to many business processes. Today, these processes are managed through CRM platforms like Microsoft Dynamics CRM. Radixweb has been helping companies yielding better ROI from MS Dynamics CRM by tailoring it to the specific business requirement, enabling enhanced customer engagement.

Microsoft Dynamics CRM offers organizations powerful capabilities. The sales force automation, marketing modules and customer service empower customer-facing departments to stay focused on what’s most important. Its flexible architecture allows customization to mold the application based on business specific needs.

A milestone in the Microsoft’s efforts to reinvent productivity, MS Dynamics CRM 2016 is a huge leap forward in its journey to deliver intelligent customer engagement. Dynamics CRM 2016 is focused on empowering employees to provide finest customer experience and engaging customers across all channels.

How Radixweb helps companies embrace top benefits of MS Dynamics CRM 2016–Enhanced Productivity, Mobility, Intelligence and unified Service are top benefits Dynamics CRM 2016 offers to business in its journey to embrace enhanced customer engagement. Blending 15 years of industry experience, Radixweb selects the implementation approach that works best for the organization and the solution company envisions.

## Radixweb Offers the State-of-the-art MS Dynamics CRM Services like

  * MS Dynamics CRM Consulting, Implementation & Customizations
  * Third-party Software Integration
  * Version Upgrades
  * Transition from CRM On-Premise to Online and vice versa
  * Migrations to MS CRM from Other CRM Systems

“Our expertise and competencies in exploring emerging technologies and building up state-of-the-art business solutions alongside our successful MS Dynamics CRM implementations and customizations for our clients, allow us to deliver superior results. At Radixweb, we are committed to help companies reinventing business process and leverage best of emerging technologies to stay ahead of the curve. We believe when a business choose Microsoft dynamics CRM, they select the right platform to seamlessly increase customer engagement. Choosing the right IT partner to implement it based on business unique requirements is equally important,” said Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Head.“

At COMEX Oman 2016 during April 12-16 2016, along with Enterprise Mobility, Application Modernization and Enterprise software, Microsoft Dynamics CRM would be in the limelight at Radixweb booth #517. We invite COMEX 2016 attendees to drive their MS CRM Implementation, Customization or Upgradations need, and our team of experts will help to implement the best approach tailored to their business need,” he further commented.
