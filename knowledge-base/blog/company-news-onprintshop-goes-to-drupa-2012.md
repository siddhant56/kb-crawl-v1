# OnPrintShop Goes to drupa 2012

Apr 1, 2012

OnPrintShop web to print solution from Radixweb will make its debut appearance at drupa 2012, Düsseldorf, Germany. We shall be at **Booth C11 Hall 8B** from **May 03-16, 2012** .

We request guests to pre-register at https://www.onprintshop.com/drupa for us to give personalized experience.

drupa is print industry’s biggest trade fair. This once in 4 years fair attracts all kinds of print service providers from all across the globe.

OnPrintShop has evolved over a span of 6 years of serving 250 plus global clients. We have worked closely with print industry to understand its requirements and challenges. So today, we make OnPrintShop available in subscription and a highly customizable licensed version.

At drupa, OnPrintShop shall showcase solutions tailored for digital printers, litho printers, photo album printers, publishers etc. We shall display our upgraded designer studio, PDF based template engine, PDF-VT generator for variable data printing, PDF-VT online personalization, CorelDraw template engine, and mobile compatible designer studio. We shall also launch our online photo album and calendar module at drupa.

Our key growth strategy has been to listen to our clients and evolve wide range of features based on in-depth research. “At drupa, we aim to build relationships and trust with our clients and partners”, informs **Naresh Bordia, VP Business Development, OnPrintShop-Radixweb** . We shall have our senior business development and key product development managers giving live solution demo. We shall also entertain custom feature requests.

For OnPrintShop, the year 2011-12 has been very successful. Our biggest achievement for the year has been our tie-up with Ricoh-Canada for providing web to print solution to SME printers in Canada. In the year, we launched path breaking new features and improved our services through trainings. We started OnPrintShop newsletter to optimize the web to print experience of our clients. We launched web to print consultancy to help SME printers deal with the challenges of moving from traditional business setup to online model.

We are also coming up with three new subscription models to meet the needs of small, middle, and large printers. So watch out for OnPrintShop at drupa 2012.
