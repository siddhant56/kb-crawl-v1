# Radixweb Celebrates 16 Years of IT Innovation

Jul 20, 2016

## Leading Global Software Development Provider and Highly Recognized as a Trusted IT Outsourcing Partner ‘Radixweb’ - Celebrating 16 Years of Business Success

The 16th year anniversary marks an important milestone for the company as the company is continuously growing to keep its heritage of excellence and IT innovation. Today, the company became the technology powerhouse. Radixweb is continually empowering the technology and grown from its core business of providing custom software development and IT outsourcing services and embedded software development to a roster of leading small to midsize and fortune 500 companies worldwide.

Today, Radixweb is celebrating its 16th birthday – ‘RX Confab 2016’ with the theme “Shaping the Future Together.” With a spectacular event hosted to mesmerize the moments and move ahead to advance the innovation frontier, Radixweb team, clients and partners are cherishing the milestone.

Radixweb is globally recognized for its commitment to technology excellence and the robust knowledge of its employees to deliver solutions based on customer’s needs. With over 2500+ projects delivered that rely on its industry excellence, skilled employees and commitment to fulfill client’s goals. That advances the way its clients do business and drive value saving time and cost. Being a long term and a trusted IT outsourcing partner is also a hallmark of Radixweb and has been recognized over the years many times.

Radixweb is outstandingly positioned as a one-stop IT provider for businesses with a portfolio of wide range of services from IT Strategy & Consulting, Custom Application Development, Software Product Development, Embedded Software Development, Web to Print Solutions, Web Designing & Development to Software Maintenance, Application Modernization, Offshore Development Center, Enterprise Mobility and more.

“Proud to see the strong presence in IT outsourcing space Radixweb has established," commented Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb. "It’s hard to believe it's 16 years today when we entered into offshore software development market with a vision. Feels proud to achieve the trust of clients, partners and employees over the years and successfully nailed it from scratch. To be a trusted partner – that’s our principle and our aim in the future.”

“As a leader in IT outsourcing, Radixweb played a key role from ideation to innovation understanding the major outsourcing gaps and converting it to a success for our clients worldwide. In past years, we’ve understood the needs of businesses, gone beyond conventional paths in simplifying the process of outsourcing for our customers. Grown matured to deliver desired results in shortest span making client stay competitive. We’re proud of the milestone of 16 years of success in business. Our overriding aim is to continuously improve, implement what we’ve learned from experiences, and constantly explore the ways to leverage the latest technology, streamline procedure, redefine processes and pass along value to our customers”, he further added.

> ‘Radixweb anatomized our needs and produced robust solutions. We really appreciate the flexibility and efficiency. Congratulations on 16 year anniversary.’

“Radixweb anatomized our needs and produced robust solutions. We really appreciate the flexibility and efficiency. It’s great to work with a company that is professional, knowledgeable and responsive. An incredibly organized team that is easy to communicate with - Congratulations on your 16 year anniversary,” commented Happy Client - Kishan Maharaj, Technology & Development Manager.

“This is an exciting moment for the company,” commented Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Head, Radixweb. “We make sure everyone connected to Radixweb ‘Feel Special’, be it clients, team or partners. Our ongoing success would have been truly impossible without the dedication of 350+ employees who remain committed to company’s vision and goals of customers and of course the support of thousands of clients and partners worldwide who trusted us to bring their idea into reality."
