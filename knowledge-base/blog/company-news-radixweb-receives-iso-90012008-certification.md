# Radixweb Receives ISO 9001:2008 Certification

Aug 11, 2009

Radixweb, India’s premier offshore software development company proudly announces that it has received the ISO 9001:2008 certification.

Radixweb offers customized web development services to its clients, which grants them a leading edge over the competition in their industry space. The Radixweb clients can be rest assured that their web application will be first-rate and user friendly, developed with state-of-the-art technology and modern techniques.

Radixweb has always emphasized on human values in its team and has travailed to inculcate wisdom, logic and acute judgment of ethical code of conduct that delivers intelligent and commendable solutions to the clients. Benchmark Certification has awarded Radixweb an ISO 9001:2008 status for its quality in consulting and development services for website, software, e-business applications and customized development.

The BSI Benchmark Certification offers management systems and product certification, training services, publications and standards. It endeavors to create competitive advantage through improved performance, create value with sustainable business practices, and minimize disruption through effective risk management.

Radixweb believes that ISO 9001:2008 is an important driver of quality. The certification recognizes organizations capable of linking business objectives with operational effectiveness. ISO 9001:2008, a Quality Management Standard, is used to establish, upgrade, and certify an organization's quality management system. Companies awarded the certification have demonstrated effective documentation and records management, top management’s dedication to customers, establishment of sound policies, good planning and execution, effective resource management, resourceful process control, measurement and analysis with continual improvement being institutionalized.

“This certificate symbolizes every single moment of hard work that we have put in to transform Radixweb into a reliable company,” states **Dharmesh Acharya, COO** , Radixweb.

Radixweb houses tech expertise on PHP MySQL applications, open source solutions, Microsoft .Net development, Java applications, embedded applications and Web 2.0 applications. “The efficiency, consistency and dedicated quality services that result from customers’ confidence in our capabilities have been acknowledged by the certification,” affirms Vimal Dagli, VP-Sales, Radixweb. The company provides designing, developing and integrating services for business solutions based on Windows or Linux platform.

Radixweb is a trusted name for developing and delivering comprehensive solutions utilizing an entire range of state-of-the-art tools and technology. The company is fully capable of dispensing PHP, .NET and Ruby on Rails development and consulting. Radixweb also specializes in ARM and DSP processors, embedded Linux, Windows CE, Palm OS, VxWorks and QNX. The company also promises rich interaction and high performance from browser-based applications with easy to use GUI.
