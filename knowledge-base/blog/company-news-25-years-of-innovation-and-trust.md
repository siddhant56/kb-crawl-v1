# Radixweb Celebrated 25th Year of Tech Excellence, Renewed Promise of Delivering AI-led Secure, Data-driven Software Systems

Aug 6, 2025

 _**Summary:** Radixweb, the digital transformation partner for 3000+ businesses, stepped into its 25th year. Founders renew commitment to lead impact with integrity for AI-driven development._

**Ahmedabad, August 7, 2025:**

Radixweb, a globally acclaimed leader in data engineering and software product development with AI-powered capabilities, marked its 25th year in the industry recently. The milestone was marked with a flagship event RxConfab where the founders laid out the vision for Radixweb’s forthcoming years.

A trusted name in AI-first innovation and consulting, Radixweb is commanding bold technologies to deliver sustainable, auditable and resilient solutions in the era of AI-led disruptions.

‘Building for tomorrow. Building tech that lasts.’ is the grounding principle with which senior leaders at Radixweb bring clarity to realistic confusions that businesses face every day.

The management laid out 3 simple principles with which they intend to tackle AI’s implications of security, privacy and bias.

  * Prioritizing architecture and infrastructure-focused development
  * Designing solutions not just for speed – but scalability, sustainability and evolving compliances
  * Designing software systems with business intent, not for hype

“25 years is a long time where we’ve witnessed the core of tech change. As we navigate the era of AI and automation, we do it with due responsibility. We aren’t just building AI-led solutions. Our goal is to help clients thrive in the AI era with transparency, fairness, and ethical clarity” said Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb.

The senior leadership of Radixweb also holds and fosters a more classified view for security in the modern development landscape. Acharya also mentions, “AI-led development has presented us with diverse opportunities and differentiated challenges. We cannot afford to treat security like a patch anymore.”

“We operate in a world where the pressure to deliver faster is a phenomenon. With distributed systems, integrations and third-party dependencies, the threat landscape gets wider. The true value of innovation can be delivered only if we embed security-by-design across the SDLC for product engineering.”

Radixweb’s annual convention was nothing short of an eye-opener for the industry because it saw the founders, both industry veterans, talk about how businesses must drive secure and impactful user experiences with AI.

Acharya stressed on having a development framework that enables:

  * Quick and detailed visibility into vulnerabilities
  * Security directly integrated into development pipelines
  * Less noise, more clarity in results
  * Governance control that doesn’t necessarily stall innovation

He also spoke a checklist for fellow development partners – the importance of automating with caution, modernizing incrementally, constantly innovating while being informed about the latest security updates, and involving security teams right from where the architectural build starts.

Radixweb is now heavily exploring deeper capabilities in:

  * AI explainability and observability tools
  * Sustainable DevOps and eco-conscious engineering practices
  * First-party data ecosystems to support privacy-first software architectures

The firm strongly believes that trust is emerging as the true competitive advantage for the tech industry. As new-age development moves into more automation and data-heavy experiences, Radixweb is taking the right strides in the right direction for balancing speed and innovation with integrity, inclusion, and transparency.
