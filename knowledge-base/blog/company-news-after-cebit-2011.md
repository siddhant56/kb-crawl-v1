# After CeBIT 2011

Mar 10, 2011

**CeBIT 2011 held at Hannover, Germany** lived up to all the hype and ballyhoo surrounding it. It was the magnum opus of the digital world showcasing awe inspiring innovations and deliberating on path breaking concepts. Radix with its rich assortment of IT services, business ready solutions, and resource hiring models won the hearts of its visitors.

**The Radix Stall G24-4 in Hall 6** saw continuous action as visitors thronged the place for different reasons. While some had come down with previously booked appointments, we had many on-the-move visitors stopping by and inquiring about our services. Our mobile application development service turned out to be the real hot cake with visitors picking up detailed discussions on our expertise, experience, and the range of our mobile applications. We also witnessed an overwhelming response for our web to print storefront OnPrintShop. The Radix team held long talks with the printers of the European and Nordic region to identify their challenges and to explore how OnPrintShop can be customized to address them.

The Radix top brass was also actively involved in B2B networking. We held across-the-table discussions at length with potential resellers and business partners. We struck gold with some of these interactions and have few mutually beneficial strategic alliances in the pipeline. We further have few inquiries that would shape up during the **upcoming IndiaSoft 2011** .

We thank all the visitors who visited Radix at CeBIT 2011. We hope your visit was a pleasant one. For all those who had detailed discussions with our team, it’s time for us to now make the move. The Radix team shall walk the talk now and deliver on your expectations.

For those who could not visit us at CeBIT, you can still get in touch with us and we can explore your requirements.
