# OnPrintShop and Xerox Russia Announce Reseller Partnership to promote Web2print Solutions

Apr 25, 2013

Xerox Russia and Radix have entered into reseller partnership to offer OnPrintShop web to print solution to Russian print market. This partnership will help Xerox Russia sell print equipment as complete web-to-print solution. This will be a win-win relationship where in Xerox Russia will use its existing network to market and provide training and support in local language to its existing and new clients, and Radix will offer customized web2print solution and technology support to consistently evolve the solution to meet end client needs.
