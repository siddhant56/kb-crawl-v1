# Radixweb Grabs Prestigious Spot in ‘The 2020 Global Outsourcing 100 List’ by IAOP®

Feb 26, 2020

**Ahmedabad, India, Feb 26, 2020-** Radixweb, leading custom software development company has been recognized by the International Association of Outsourcing Professionals® (IAOP®) as the top 100 Global outsourcing companies worldwide. Consisting of a spectacular list of the world’s best outsourcing service providers and advisors, this recognition signifies Radixweb as a diligent technology leader, great employer and environmentally responsible company.

The 2020 Global Outsourcing 100 recognizes the world’s best outsourcing service providers and advisors. In its fourteenth year, The GO100, is revered as a global standard for companies seeking long term and reliable outsourcing partnerships.

This list is based on applications received, and judging is based on a rigorous scoring methodology that includes an independent review by an independent panel of IAOP customer members with extensive experience in selecting outsourcing service providers and advisors for their organizations.

“Radixweb’s extensive industry knowledge and software outsourcing capabilities helps to uniquely position our company to help our customers operate with greater agility in an increasingly transforming landscape,” says Dharmesh Acharya, Chief Operating Officer, Radixweb. “Our presence on this coveted list GO100 is a rewarding testimony of Radixweb’s success in empowering customers’ with improved efficiencies, productivity, and cost effectiveness.”

“We have been involved in this pursuit for over 19 years. Our team of 410+ performers consistently help us to deliver market-leading solutions that best meet our clients’ needs. This accreditation demonstrates our continuous commitment to devise path breaking software solutions and harnessing smart talent to help customers move faster, respond quickly to new opportunities and create unmatched outcomes,” adds Acharya.

“Companies that outsource, not only in the traditional sense but also through the wide array of the ever-changing collaborative business models, are scrutinizing their providers very closely, which is why choosing the right partners is more important than ever,” said Debi Hamill, IAOP CEO. “The GO100 is the definitive guide to help companies research and compare service providers with whom they are considering relationships."

“Buyers understand there are hundreds of qualified service providers and advisors out there, but what they need to understand now is what makes each one exceptional,” said IAOP CEO, Debi Hamill. “The Global Outsourcing 100 list has done just that. We’re proud to recognize Radixweb for being among these companies this year.”

“Every business is becoming more about technology today. And we’ve seen new challenges sprouting up with adoption of smart technologies. Radixweb is committed to rapidly innovate, evolve and improve our value based and genuine outsourcing services to become an active driver of business growth for our customers. Our approach is echoed with this recognition,’ says Pratik Mistry, Senior Business Head, Radixweb.

### About IAOP

IAOP is the global association that brings together customers, providers, and advisors in a collaborative, knowledge-based environment that promotes professional and organizational development, recognition, certification, and excellence to improve business service models and outcomes.

Our members and affiliates worldwide are digging deep at IAOP conferences, learning at IAOP chapter meetings, getting trained and certified at IAOP courses and workshops, and connecting through IAOP social media, all with one goal: better business results. Whether you are a customer, provider or advisor, new to collaborative business models like outsourcing, or you are an experienced professional, IAOP connects you and your organization to our growing global community and to the resources you need to get the results your company deserves and demands. For more information and how you can become involved, visit www.IAOP.org.
