# Radix Signs Up For Global Sourcing Forum + Expo

Nov 2, 2009

Radix, a leading IT services provider from Ahmedabad, India, has signed up for this year’s Global Sourcing Forum + Expo due to be held on November 11 and 12, 2009 at New York. Radix has been allotted booth No 401 at the forum that is scheduled to be held at Jacob K Javits Convention Center, New York.

The Expo will draw industry bigwigs and leaders who will analyze various aspects of outsourcing and its global repercussions - particularly the pros and cons of outsourcing - its future, and its cultural, political and human impact. The Expo will provide a stage to the participants to discuss latest trends and developments in outsourcing and its effect on the information and communication technology.

The Expo will be a congregation of academicians, thought leaders, ITO/BPO service providers and suppliers from over 25 countries who will examine and discuss the challenges and opportunities posed by changes taking place in the industry. The event will prove crucial for companies that are increasingly adopting outsourcing and offshoring as a competitive tool. The Expo allows visitors to meet face-to-face with over 100 vendors and discover how best to take advantage of a globalized marketplace.

The Expo will provide Radix an international audience to demonstrate its experience, expertise and technological capabilities in software development, embedded development and business-specific software solutions for varied industry verticals. Announcing its participation, the company stated that it was looking forward to demonstrating its expertise and competence to the world.

Radix has been in the outsourcing IT services and development business for a decade and caters to a global client base. An ISO 9001-2000 certified company and a Microsoft Certified Gold Partner, Radix is a trusted name for software development, technology solutions, custom application development and IT consulting.
