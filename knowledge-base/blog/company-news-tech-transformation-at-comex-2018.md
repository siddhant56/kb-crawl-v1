# Radixweb Thumps Tech Transformation by Bringing Visions to Life at Comex 2018

May 3, 2018

Leading IT Outsourcing and Software Development Company- Radixweb Steers Middle East Businesses to Deliver Customer Delight in an Emerging Market Economy at Oman

Radixweb showcased raw power of technology at Booth #609 at the highly coveted COMEX Technology Week in the Sultanate of Oman from 23rd to 25th April, 2018. In its consecutive fourth yearly visit to the exhibition, Radixweb consistently emerged as a favourite among the attendees who thronged the booth for strategic discussions to find the right technology combination.

The company was aptly represented by a dynamic duo of Maitray Gadhavi and Jaydeep Patadiya, Senior Business Development Managers at Radixweb. The booth hosted about 100+ visitors during the course of the event- making it a bee-hive of conversations.

Radixweb’s show-stopper exhibit- Simplest to use Audit Management Software sparked most interest among the attendees. Their technology-based audit management tool enables businesses to embed precise processes, strengthen compliance, reduce costs and gain a competitive edge. Apart from this, enterprise mobility and application modernization services were unanimous hits at the show.

> Fourth Successful Stint at COMEX: Radixweb transforms businesses with growth-oriented approach, futuristic apps and adaptive technology expertise, ultimately delivering high performance for enriching value

Sharing his experience, Maitray Gadhavi says, “In our fourth visit to COMEX, we have clearly surpassed several initial collaborative milestones with Oman. Radixweb doesn’t not remain strange; rather represents credibility, comfort and expertise for Middle East companies. With the economy stabilizing again, COMEX visitors’ affinity towards becoming more mobile and applying better app modernization for their business is on a significant rise.”

“Our mobile app development services and application modernization received huge traction at the event. It was equally amazing to see how businesses are becoming more aware to adapt cloud-based audit management solutions to shun traditional methods of auditing. Our audit management software- ECAT became an instant hit in the region,” he quips.

Adding to this, Jaydeep Patadiya says, “We understand what sits on the top of several boardroom agendas here- it is cost optimization, digitalization and enterprise mobility by bringing complete Tech Transformation. Also, there is a fresh urgency to diversify revenue streams and identify cost reductions using custom software development, mobile app development and application modernization among others. That’s where Radixweb comes- we open new channels of success to help businesses focus more on their Core!”

A seasoned tech leader for the emerging Oman market, the company has been helping several top-notch Middle East businesses resolve their most pressing challenges through years.

On the continued collaboration with Middle East region, Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Head, Radixweb says, “As the Omani economy gains momentum, it’s more important than ever to have a well-versed technology partner on the side. Whether the need is to devise a new strategy or put one into action, Radixweb continues to unravel the best tech to solve businesses’ toughest needs- no matter the industry.”

This year’s magnanimous digital and offline presence of COMEX worked as an enhancer for Radixweb to expand and strengthen its footprints in the Middle East region.
