# Radixweb Expands Cloud Modernization Services to Help US Businesses Overcome Legacy Bottlenecks

Feb 4, 2026

Frisco, Texas, USA, Date: 5th February, 2026

Radixweb, a global technology services company, has announced an expansion of its cloud modernization services. The move is designed to help US businesses overcome challenges caused by older IT systems. With these services, companies can modernize their technology, improve efficiency, and prepare for future growth.

Many companies in the United States still rely on legacy software and infrastructure. These systems were reliable in the past. But today, they are slow, costly, and hard to scale. They can also make it difficult to adopt new technologies. Cloud-native applications, automation, and artificial intelligence often remain out of reach. Radixweb’s legacy app modernization services aim to remove these obstacles.

## Why Cloud Modernization Matters

Cloud modernization is more than moving data to the cloud. It is about updating systems to work faster, smarter, and safer. Modern IT systems can reduce costs, improve performance, and allow new technologies.

“Legacy IT systems can act as bottlenecks,” said Mr. Pratik Mistry, EVP of Technology Consulting, at Radixweb. “They slow growth and limit a company’s ability to respond to market changes. Our expanded services help businesses create flexible, secure, and efficient IT systems.”

## Radixweb’s Approach to Cloud Modernization

Radixweb focuses on planning and careful execution when modernizing legacy systems and taking them to the cloud. The goal is to minimize disruption to daily operations. We ensure that by working with clients to understand their current systems. We first identify problem areas and then develop solutions to fit the unique business needs.

Key elements of our cloud modernization services include:

  * **System Assessment:** Reviewing applications and infrastructure to spot bottlenecks and inefficiencies.
  * **Cloud Strategy Planning:** Designing a strategy with public, private, or hybrid cloud options.
  * **Application Modernization:** Updating or refactoring software for better cloud performance.
  * **Security and Compliance:** Adding robust security and governance measures.
  * **Operational Optimization:** Making systems scalable, reliable, and easier to manage.

This method allows cloud modernization for scalability, with security, without disrupting everyday work.

Our cloud modernization services based on this approach have yielded successful outcomes for businesses across logistics, healthcare, finance, and many other niches.

For a healthcare client, for example, we strategized and implemented rapid modernization within 7 months, while keeping sensitive patient data secure. These updates improved efficiency and allowed the healthcare services company to adopt new technologies safely.

## Supporting Digital Transformation

Our cloud modernization services help companies adopt digital-first strategies without major disruptions. Modernizing legacy systems improves performance, reduces friction, and lays the foundation for growth.

Radixweb’s modernization services also offer flexibility. Companies can prioritize key applications first. Other systems can move gradually. This keeps modernization aligned with business goals and minimizes risk.

“US businesses are focusing on overall IT modernization as a driver of growth,” said Mistry “Our expanded services show Radixweb’s commitment to helping companies navigate complex technology changes. We are helping build systems ready for the future.”
