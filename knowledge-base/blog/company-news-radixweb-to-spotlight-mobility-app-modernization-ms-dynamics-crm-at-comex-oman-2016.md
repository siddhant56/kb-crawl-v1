# Radixweb to Spotlight Mobility, App Modernization, MS Dynamics CRM at COMEX Oman 2016

Apr 12, 2016

**Radixweb is up to Help Companies Reinvent Business Approach, Accelerate Revenue Growth and Outperform the Competition by Embracing Latest Technologies and Deep Expertise in Digital Transformation at Booth #517 April 12-16 COMEX Muscat Oman**

Radixweb, a global IT Outsourcing and Software Development Company announced today to spotlight Enterprise Mobility, Application Modernization, MS Dynamics CRM at COMEX Oman 2016 during April 12-16 2016. Radixweb booth is once again a place for COMEX 2016 attendees to witness the top industry’s trends and get the consultation from Radixweb experts to leverage IT and transform business. Team Radixweb will also showcase its expertise in creating tailored solutions to perfectly fit the unique business need in multiple domains like Insurance, HR and Recruitment, Field Service Management, and Printing.

The hottest technology trends and industry specific solutions will once again be on display at COMEX Oman 2016, booth #517 running from April 12-16 2016. COMEX 2016 would be highlighting the industry technologies of today and tomorrow for delivering the competitive edge to Middle East businesses. Team Radixweb to help show attendees to leverage technology against business disruption.Radixweb will spotlight and demonstrate how organizations that embrace latest technologies can revolutionize its business approach and pave the way to attain greater flexibility and sustainable growth.

## Radixweb Experts will Exhibit on Key Topics that Include:

**Enterprise Mobility – Experience a Fluid Enterprise Which Has No Edge**

**Application Modernization – Shift IT Spend from High Maintenance to Greater Innovation**

**MS Dynamics CRM – Experience Intelligent Customer Engagement**

“Businesses must keep pace with the changing technology to meet challenging customer demands and stay competitive. Radixweb is offering industry-leading IT consulting and strategy services across the globe that help companies meet their client’s changing demands and stay ahead of the curve. Focusing on enabling flexibility and enhanced performance to our customers, Radixweb invites all COMEX Oman 2016 attendees to join us in the growing circle to embrace some of the most efficient and innovative IT solutions in the industry. Clients trust Radixweb to help them envision how the latest technology can help them achieve their needs and drive greater value that gives shape to their vision. During this week-long event, we look forward to interacting with COMEX 2016 attendees and help to accelerate business growth with our enriched IT solutions and services”, Maitray Gadhavi and Jay Patadiya, Business Development Manager, Radixweb jointly commented.
