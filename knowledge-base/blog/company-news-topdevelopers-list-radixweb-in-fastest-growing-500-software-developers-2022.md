# Radixweb Ranks #15 on the List of TopDeveloper’s Fastest Growing 500 Software Developers 2022

Dec 25, 2022

 _TopDevelopers, a leading directory, research, and review platform, has ranked us in the Top 15 on the Fastest Growing 500 Software Developers 2022 List. Experts at TopDevelopers have recognized our time-tested best practices with cutting-edge technologies and world-class dedicated teams._

Another ecstatic and euphoric moment to celebrate for the Radixweb team. It is a matter of immense pride and jubilation for us, that Radixweb has ranked 15th in the TopDeveloper’s Fastest Growing 500 Software Developers List 2022.

This notable feat is the culmination of our time-tested best practices, the hard work of dedicated teams and the support and cooperation extended by our esteemed clientele. The accreditation has surely motivated us to continue the journey for excellence in the area of software development and pivot businesses towards the pinnacle of sustainable growth.

Celebrating this grand accomplishment, our COO, Mr. Dharmesh Archarya, said, “We are humbled to have been honored with this massive accreditation, ranked among the top 15 of 500 highly esteemed global companies. It is not a common feat to be included in this prestigious list. This recognition strongly validates our unwavering dedication to excellence in delivering digital solutions and the sustainable growth we’re bringing to the software development world.”

“Continuing the arduous journey to provide bespoke functionalities and equip businesses with 21st-century relevant modern technologies, we strive to help them understand the power of innovation as they take their next step forward”, he added.

Since 2000, Radixweb has emerged as one of the most trusted outsourcing software and web application development companies. Our strength lies in our team’s expertise in integrating 360-degree value towards business processes and designing transformative digital strategies. We at Radixweb strive to consistently develop exceptional processes and innovative solutions integrated with innovative technology to help organizations overcome business-critical situations.

We are proud of the fact that we have been recognized for our excellent work by several global platforms like Globee Business Excellence Awards in IT Consulting, Titan Business Awards honoring us as the Most Innovative IT Company of The Year, a leading status for Top Custom Software Development by Designrush. Apart from this, our COO, Mr. Dharmesh Acharya, has been selected as a contributing member of the esteemed Forbes Tech Council Member, where he has been sharing business insights, and learnings gathered over two decades as a senior C-suite member.

These crowning accolades have further motivated and encouraged us to deliver high-quality results and high-performing software and strengthen businesses with cutting-edge technology to optimally align their processes with long-term objectives.
