# Radix Attains Kentico Partner Status

Apr 17, 2012

Radixweb, India’s premier IT technology services provider, has attained Kentico Partner status. This partner recognition places Radix in a rather elite network of more than 1200 partners at global level.

Kentico is an enterprise content management system widely used to develop self-sustaining e-commerce solutions. This ASP.Net based CMS offers a wide set of features to develop websites, intranets, online communities, and online business portals. Applications developed using Kentico CMS can be mobile compatible. They can also support online marketing tools, multiple languages, multisite management, and user friendly interface.

The Kentico Partner status comes along with a rich set of benefits for Radix and our customers. Like mentioned before, the certification makes us a member of 1200 plus network. The partner status certifies the superior quality of our Kentico development, integration, and consulting services. Being Kentico partner, we get better marketing opportunities and access to Kentico’s knowledge base. We have case studies and whitepapers readily accessible. This gives us a competitive advantage when we develop applications. We also get priority access to Kentico upgrades and components. We get licenses to different Kentico releases which help us add value to the applications that we develop.

In addition to this our development efforts are guided by some of Kentico’s very own technical team. We receive guidance and technical assistance from Kentico’s technical team and wider partner network. This makes our development efforts cost effective and compatible with global development standards.

Radix is also a Microsoft Gold Certified Partner and Sitefinity Partner. We are certified experts in Dotnet development platforms like ASP.Net, VB.Net, & C#.Net. We also offer COTS customization services and Microsoft enterprise integration services. Our application development includes enterprise applications, web-based business management systems, e-commerce applications, and networking communities.

To know more about our Dotnet application development services, visit us at https://radixweb.com/microsoft-app-development
