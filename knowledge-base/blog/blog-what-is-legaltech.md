## FAQs

### What technology do law firms use?

Law firms use legaltech which includes Machine Learning, Big Data, Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, and many more budding technologies that help automate legal work.

### What is a legal technology professional?

A legal technology professional, also known as a legal technologist, is a person who enables organizations to use legal technology to deliver the best legal services. Legal technologists don't need to come from a law background or be legal professionals.

### How has technology changed the legal profession?

Legal technology helps increase the efficiency and productivity of law firms and legal professionals. With the help of different legaltech tools, individuals from law backgrounds can complete their tasks rapidly in a more accurate way and with minimal errors.

### Is legal technology expensive?

Legal technology comes in different shapes and sizes, from artificial intelligence-powered contract review software to cloud-based practice management systems. However, the cost of legaltech stack can vary, as some solutions can be used for free, while others can cost up to tens of hundreds of dollars.

### What does legal software do?

In simple words, legaltech software is any software used to handle and manage the daily operations of a law firm. This can include A-Z everything from client details to document and case management to time billing and case progress tracking.

### What is the difference between legal tech and law tech?

Legal tech consists of technologies that lawyers use to simplify their work, like AI/ML document review, e-discovery, etc. On the contrary, law tech is a software solution used to deliver products for clients, such as legal chatbots, online marketplaces, etc.
