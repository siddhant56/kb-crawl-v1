## Statistics on the Global Fintech Market Size

  * **Tencent is One of the Biggest Fintech Companies in Asia**

When talking about the Asia region, Tencent’s Fintech division leads the pack in China. As a listed regulatory technology firm, the company has surpassed a market value of USD 500 billion and is today the most valuable publicly traded company.

  * **Visa is the Largest Listed Fintech Company in North America**

Topping the list of North America’s most significant Fintech organizations, it specializes in payment technology. This publicly traded firm boasts an astounding market capitalization of USD 570.31 Billion.

  * **Stripe is the Most Valuable Private Fintech Startup in the US**

Stripe Inc. is an Irish-American multinational financial services company that is valued at USD 65 billion following its staff share deal in February 2024; however, its valuation has declined by 47% from its peak of USD 94.4 billion in 2021.

Still, it remains one of the US's most valuable private Fintech companies. Last year, Stripe handled transactions amounting to USD 817 Billion for big conglomerates like Ford, Microsoft, and OpenAI.

  * **Nigeria is the Most Active Fintech Country in Africa**

Nigeria, Africa's biggest economy and most favored nation, has been at the forefront of the continent's Fintech revolution. In 2024, Nigeria's Fintech sector remains one of Africa's most dynamic and promising. The country is witnessing the emergence of multiple top startup players valued at over USD 1 Billion - such as OPay, Interswitch, and Flutterwave.

  * **Switzerland Turns out to be the Crypto Valley**

Ranked as Europe’s second most flourishing Fintech nation, Switzerland remains solid in the financial service domain, particularly for its city Zug, often designated the “crypto-valley.” The city is growing, solidifying its role in tech-forward Fintech solutions and digital currency.

Moreover, recognizing the swift transformation of the Fintech sector, Switzerland prioritizes bringing fresh startups and industry professionals.

  * **The Fintech Center of Asia is Singapore**

While China has some of the biggest Fintech firms, Singapore shines in the Fintech field, specifically outshining in Payment, Insurtech, and Lending. Estimates imply that Singapore now hosts more than 1,600 Fintech companies, the highest in the ASEAN region.

Moreover, Singapore rivals other crucial players globally, such as the United States and the UK, in terms of fintech ventures per capita.

Among these organizations, the leading subsectors are payments (31% of all Fintechs in Singapore belong to this category), Fintech infrastructure providers (18% of total Fintechs), and regulatory technology (17%).

  * **The USA and China - Giants in the Fintech World**

The majority of Fintech organizations hail from China and the US. In fact, 7 of the world’s top 10 most incredible Fintech organizations are based in these two nations.

  * **Worldwide Fintech Investments in 2024**

Global fintech investment falls to USD 51.9 billion in the first half of 2024, says KPMG data. The first half of 2024 saw the global Fintech sector navigate a challenging investment domain. And the global investment in Fintech in 2024 reached USD 15.9 billion across 1,566 deals.

## Latest Trends in the Global Fintech Industry

  * **Fintech is Getting More Advanced**

Tech advancements are changing the Fintech domain. The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic is still fueling a rising need for managing finances with almost every digital platform, including e-commerce, healthcare, hospitality, and more.

  * **Machine Learning and AI: The Two Favorites**

Two of the most popular trends in the Fintech industry are the integration of AI and Machine Learning. These technologies, encompassing the capabilities for machines to examine and act based on their environment, can transform FinTech sectors like risk management and banking.

Machine Learning, also a subset of AI, specializes in acknowledging any data patterns. Hence, by adopting these innovations, Fintech organizations can automate a range of manual, complicated tasks.

  * **Embedded Finance Will be the Leading Trend**

Embedded finance is making waves in Fintech and is all set to be the leading trend. And the embedded finance market is estimated to value around USD 115.8 billion in 2024, and you can expect it to reach USD 251.5 billion by 2029 at a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 16.8%.

Among these, the “Buy Now Pay Later” model is particularly catching the eye of users by helping them defer payments.

  * **SaaS is a Big Name in Fintech**

From the beginning of 2024 till September, Fintech solutions involving SaaS raised about USD 2.22 billion in equity funding across 119 rounds. With the help of SaaS, financial businesses can use cloud-hosted software without direct installation, cut expenses, and simplify operations.

  * **Open Banking Paves the Way for Fintech**

The demand for open banking is anticipated to record a splendid growth of 19.7% during the forecast period. And it can rise from USD 22,160 million in 2024 to USD 133.5 billion by 2034, changing the dynamics of Fintech industry statistics. This trend helps users share their financial data more securely, through improved flexibility and control. It presents excellent potential for businesses by facilitating seamless data sharing.

In the end, open banking can help transform interactions with different financial essences by encouraging transparency and choice.

  * **IoT is One of the Most Important Advances for Fintech**

IoT (Internet of Things) helps link everyday devices to the internet by allowing them to collect data and act without human intervention. This transforms Fintech by making banking more user-friendly, faster, and safer.

Considering IoT in the banking, financial services, and insurance (BFSI) market, the global Fintech market size is expected to value around USD 116.27 billion by 2026, exhibiting a CAGR of 26.5% during the forecast period.

  * **Blockchain is Still Going Strong**

With the rapid advancement of technology, Blockchain stands out as one of the best Fintech trends, and it also plays a crucial role in increasing Fintech adoption rates. It works as a digital playbook that documents multiple transactions with complete accuracy. It encourages businesses and people to exchange digital assets, and thus, eradicates any requirement for a middleman or central figure.

Despite the crash of multiple cryptocurrencies in the last few years, you can expect Blockchain to grow by more than tenfold in the next decade.

## Statistics Related to Fintech App Development Costs

  * **Fintech Application Development Cost Can Rise to USD 300,000**

Developing a Fintech app is quite an investment, with the cost going up to USD 300,000. For instance, building a custom Fintech app that offers consumers a simple and secure online transaction approach may start with a minimum range of USD 40,000.

To be specific, developing a Fintech application with a minimal user interface and basic features can cost between USD 30,000 and USD 100,000. And if you want to incorporate modern technologies in your Fintech app, it can cost anywhere between USD 60,000 and USD 300,000 or even more, depending on the app’s size and complexity.

  * **Fintech Developers in the US Have the Highest Average Earnings**

Due to the high demand for skilled developers, Fintech app developers in the United States often earn more competitive salaries than in other regions. The average salary of a Fintech developer in the USA is USD 136,900 per year or USD 65.82 per hour. Entry-level positions start at USD 100,000 annually, while most experienced professionals make up to USD 195,000 annually.

Cities like New York and San Francisco, well-known for their flourishing Fintech ecosystems, offer better salaries than other regions.

  * **Developers' Hourly Rates Varies According to Location**

Where your Fintech solution is built is crucial to the overall app cost. The pricing often depends on the hourly rate of your development team, usually consisting of professional experts and the time it takes for app development within a given country. For instance, Fintech app developers in the US would likely charge higher rates compared to India.

A Fintech app developer's global average hourly rate is about USD 32.50/ hour.

  * **Fintech App Development Can Take Up to 12 Months or More**

The timeline for the Fintech app development process can range from 3 to 12 months, depending on your app's complexity, your development team’s expertise, and the extent of regulatory compliance needed.
