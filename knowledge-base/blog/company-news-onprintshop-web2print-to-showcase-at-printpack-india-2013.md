# OnPrintShop Web2Print to Showcase at PRINTPACK India 2013

Jan 30, 2013

OnPrintShop web to print solution will make its second appearance at PRINTPACK India 2013 to be held at Noida from February 23-28, 2012. We will conduct live demo of our web2print solution at **Stall H-15/12** . We have also invited **Mr. Morten Reitoft from Denmark** to conduct interactive session on “Print Export Opportunities in Europe” on **23rd Feb 2012 at 2 pm and 24th Feb 2012 at 12 pm** .

Interested printers can book their seat for interactive session at https://www.onprintshop.com/printpack. You can also book a personal meeting with Mr. Morten for one-to-one discussion.

Today web2print is becoming a critical part of Print Service Providers (PSP’s) business strategy worldwide to get new clients, retain existing retail and corporate clients, improve productivity and increase profitability. OnPrintShop is a highly affordable web2print solution with wide range of features and customizations for SME printers. We are recognized by our clients and partners as a highly user-friendly, affordable and customizable solution. At Printpack we will showcase our key solution features like:

  * Personalized Website and Web store with wide range of products
  * User friendly Store Admin Panel to manage store
  * CorelDraw & PDF template engine to generate high resolution print ready files.
  * Variable Data Printing, Online Photo Album, Trade printer solutions
  * Regional language support in designer studio
  * Manage orders & unlimited products online
  * 30+ preset products with 5000+ ready to use designs
  * Store Set up Services, Solution Training & Support
  * Flexible investment options – Subscription & License plans

Solution Features List: https://www.onprintshop.com/solution-features.html

We have invited Mr. Morten Reitoft from Print Provider, Denmark to conduct an interactive session to help Indian printers explore direct and indirect export opportunities in European market. Reports have suggested a 40% rise in print exports from India by 2014. Mr. Morten’s session will focus on how Indian printers can take advantage of print outsourcing and what IT and printing technologies they need.

“If an American company runs campaigns worldwide today it does not make sense to print all at one place and then distribute. Today jobs are printed and distributed locally. Operating this way gives customers significant advantages like lower prices, faster turnaround time, better environment, and lower shipping cost”, says **Mr. Morten** from Print Provider.

Print Provider is a Danish company that aims to offer its services worldwide. It is building - together with OnPrintShop-Radixweb - an amazing purchasing and planning platform that enables professional buyers to select the right partner for the right job. The vast range of printed products makes it almost impossible to choose the right vendor - who has the capacity, who has the right skills, who has the machinery to support your print need, etc.

“At PRINTPACK we will share our vision. We will explain the complexity of sourcing print and deliver precise information about the market, the buyers and what they need”, revealed Mr. Morten.

At OnPrintShop we are committed to understand the requirements and challenges of SME printers and help them achieve their online goals.
