# Radixweb celebrates 20 years of accelerating Global Businesses with Xcellence

Jul 5, 2020

High Performers take a more sophisticated approach when leveraging technology to improve business results. It’s been two decades for Radixweb – renowned software development and IT consulting, helping global businesses tap the benefits of the latest technologies to outperform at a speed and scale.

Our clients come with a vision of their company’s growth. We have been providing actionable strategies aligned on how to achieve it. Its time to double the power to keep delivering a unique, interactive experience that enables enterprises, and its leadership team, to assess its company’s capability to transform and accelerate growth that outpaces the speed of disruptions.

Our journey to the big leagues began with our inception, and we have stayed on course ever since. With 5500 clients spread across 29 countries, 450+ talented team Radixweb is now a force to be reckoned with.

As we celebrate our 20th anniversary, we look back at the years gone by with pride. Where even when a setback faced us, Radixweb powered through to the winner’s podium every time. Today we have carved out a niche for ourselves in a technology sector known for its hurdles and change being the only constant. But we have claimed our chunk of the pie, and we are just getting started.

Driven by the thought leadership of our c-suite, empowered by our management and team leaders and mobilized by our teams, the Radixweb saga of success is just beginning.

“Two decades of enabling the digital transformation of enterprises across the globe is a fantastic milestone. I am incredibly proud of Radixians, who have made this extraordinary journey possible. I am grateful that they have brought their skills and expertise together to help our clients achieve their goals,” commented Divyesh Patel, CEO Radixweb.

Even with the ever-changing scenario of the new normal, Radixweb has emerged as an industry leader in a business sense as well as in social responsibility. Even in pandemic and lockdown, our leaders who had already foreseen what was coming were prepared. A 100% remote workforce was deployed in a matter of hours, and business continuity was maintained without even the slightest glitch.

Throughout the uncertainty, we continue to provide intuitive and innovative digital transformation assets to our clients come rain or shine. And now, even more so as Radixweb celebrates 20 years of Xcellence.

We have continuously pushed the boundaries of what is possible and have helped our clients reimagine their business in today’s digital world. As COO of a company that feels more like a family, I would like to say Kudos to all who have made Radixweb grow from strength to strength.

“I have great pride in yesterday and high confidence in tomorrow. But how does one condense 20 years of digitally transforming businesses?

Radixweb is committed to being innovation-led, and this approach has shaped our trajectory. We have journeyed a considerable distance from where we began, expanding not only in employee strength but also in diversifying our service offerings.

In all this time, the adrenaline that coursed through our veins on the first day of business hasn’t dwindled. We still exult in pushing boundaries and making the impossible a reality for our clients.” says Radixweb COO, Mr. Dharmesh Acharya.

“So what is the secret to our success, you ask. A combination of innovation, drive, and curiosity to look beyond what is established and drive value from yet unexplored avenues. Over the past twenty years, we have consistently challenged the status-quo and have continued to reinvent the future. Radixweb is turning 21, and in my humble opinion, it is an impressive milestone, especially in the constantly metamorphosing competitive market environment of Software development.

This year marks the beginning of a new phase for Radixweb, one in which we will create new services, transform our operations and continue to deliver value to our customers while remaining aligned with the demands of the new normal,” Mr. Acharya says.

Mr. Varix Patel, VP Operation, joins in with, “It’s not just about celebrating the occasion. It’s about celebrating the people who made it all possible. We are celebrating the unrelenting dedication and pure Xcellence of our team, their passion for performing, and tireless zeal for innovation. We are celebrating Radixians.”

When asked about how it all came through, Mr. Acharya smiles- “I believe that transparent decisions, clear plans, and a calm, controlled, intuitive approach are cornerstones not to be trifled with. We acknowledge that we might not know what the future holds but we can assure both our employees and clients that Radixweb is manned and battle-ready. I would say we are preparing to launch another 20 years of innovation together while cherishing first 20.”

The countdown has begun, and Radixweb is all set push on even further. Stay tuned for cherishing memories by Radixians and exclusive plans for the coming years!
