# OnPrintShop Announces Partnership with Ricoh Canada Inc.

Mar 14, 2012

OnPrintShop, a truly affordable web to print solution and RicohCanada, a leading provider of document solutions, have joined hands to provide turn-key storefront solutions to RicohCanada customers.

OnPrintShop is a key solution of Radixweb, a leading IT company providing business solutions globally for over 11 years. OnPrintShop was developed by serving over **250 global clients** over a period of 6 years. The solution has become popular with printers because it is **highly affordable** , has a **customizable store front** , has **PDF based design templates** , and has a **user friendly admin panel** . OnPrintShop is available in both subscription and licensed model.

Ricoh Canada is a wholly owned subsidiary of Ricoh Americas Corporation a leading supplier of office automation equipment and electronics. Headquartered at Toronto, Ontario, Ricoh Canada offers fully integrated hardware and software products that help businesses share information efficiently and effectively and enable customers to control the input, management, and output of documents.

**Ricoh customers can take advantage of the following key strengths of OnPrintShop:**

  * Highly affordable
  * Complete package for SME printers
  * High customization possibilities
  * Support for third party integrations
  * Client Support

According to the terms of this new partnership, Ricoh can offer OnPrintShop to its customers in Canada. This would help SME printers of Canada realize the benefits of web to print. For OnPrintShop, the partnership strengthens their position in the web to print market.

“We are happy to partner with RicohCanada, as Ricoh evaluated us with w2p solution providers worldwide on solution capabilities, stability, service, user interface, and pricing. We now look forward to developing a stronger relationship with Ricoh by providing quality solution and after sales service to its clients”, states **Mr Dharmesh Acharya, Product Head, OnPrintShop-Radixweb** .

In addition to a web to print store front, OnPrintShop offers a wide range of other services to printers as well including internet marketing and SEO, template recreation, custom template designing, and more.

“We now plan to expand our services aggressively in the Canadian market with the help of a highly recognized partner like Ricoh”, says **Mr Naresh Bordia, VP Sales & Marketing, OnPrintShop-Radixweb** .

To learn more please contact the OnPrintShop team at www.onprintshop.com
