# Ricoh Canada Showcasing OnPrintShop at Graphics Canada for the Second Consecutive Year

Nov 7, 2013

Ricoh Canada, a prominent company in the Printing industry & reseller partner of OnPrintShop will be demonstrating OnPrintShop - Web to Print Solution from November 21 to 23 at Booth 1240.

It is for the second time in a row that Ricoh Canada will be showcasing OnPrintShop, a comprehensive web-to-print solution at Graphics Canada, Canada’s largest Graphic Communications and Printing show with 200 exhibitors representing over 400 companies. The overwhelming response of the previous exhibit and commendable synergy between Ricoh Canada and OnPrintShop since 2011 forms the foundation of the return to the exhibition.
