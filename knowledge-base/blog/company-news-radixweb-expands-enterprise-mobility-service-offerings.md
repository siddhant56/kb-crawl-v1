# Radixweb Expands Enterprise Mobility Service Offerings

Apr 12, 2016

**Radixweb Expands its Innovative Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) Offerings to Help Enterprises Gain Competitive Advantages, Improve Workforce Productivity and Simplify Work Process**

Radixweb, a global software development and IT outsourcing company announced it is expanding its Enterprise Mobility Services Portfolio to help organizations to simplify IT workflow with a unified, end-to-end mobility management solutions.

## Radixweb’s New Suite of Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) Services include

### Mobility Strategy and Consulting

### Custom Enterprise Mobile App

### Application Design and Migration

### Enterprise Mobile & Cloud Enablement

The new offerings will complement the innovative Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) services the company has already introduced in the market.

Mobile technologies have released businesses from fixed location, opened immense value, and unlocked solutions to further help businesses grow and transform. Radixweb is offering flexible, comprehensive, custom end-to-end mobility/BYOD solutions development services. The mobile enablement solution by Radixweb delivers industry-leading management and security technology, and enables remote access to corporate resources, real-time reporting, user self-service and alerts. Radixweb works with businesses throughout its mobility journey to help to maximize the potential of its mobile implementation.

“Radixweb has delivered us with a market ready solution that is making big splashes with some internationally recognized brands. We are happy with Radixweb for seamlessly understanding our requirements, having a highly qualified team of developers, and delivering a timely and market-ready solution. I think it's their approach to client relationship management is distinguishes them from other providers”, said Colm O’Neill COO at IT Company, Ireland.

“We have seen how mobility is driving better performance – responding more quickly, getting more work done in remote locations and seamlessly collaborating. However, embracing enterprise mobility comes with its own challenges and depends on how companies grab mobility which makes a difference between growth and failure. Radixweb thrives being the IT partner who helps companies to leverage best of mobility solutions while taking care of its primary pain points like security, data safety, privacy compliance, system integration and more”, said Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Head, Radixweb.

Throwing light on company’s recent expansion of mobility portfolio, he commented, “Mobility is extremely critical to enterprises who are looking to stay competitive and enhance their business ROI. With the expansion of our Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) offerings, we will help organizations to operate and manage applications that adhere to the process and policy requirement of the organization, enable seamless mobile application management, and yet answer the unique demands of mobile workfare. Helping companies to unlock the opportunities offered by mobile devices by resolving challenges in their mobility journey and gain long-term business growth and competitive advantage is the key to Radixweb’s enterprise mobility service offerings.”
