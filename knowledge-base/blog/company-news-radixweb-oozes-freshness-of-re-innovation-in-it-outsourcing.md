# Radixweb Oozes Freshness of Re-innovation in IT Outsourcing

Apr 17, 2017

Driven by the invigorating approach to lead the competition from the front and grabbing the pulse of the technology driven age, Radixweb displays top-form of leadership by evolving its website to outreach demands of New-Age IT

In the techno world, the time to be “Tech-Wow” is always now! It is inevitable for leaders to synchronize groundbreaking strategy with the eyes and brains of the audience. Moving ahead with this thought, Radixweb sculpts its website from scratch in revitalizing tactic to strike the perfect chord with “what” consumers actually look out for. The new digital identity has a vitalizing dash of crisp content, peppy visuals and interesting features.

Put simply, the company readily paves the road to be more than Future ready - in the technology landscape.

“Bringing tech revolution and molding technology innovation tailored to needs has been our USP. This aesthetically rich brand identity is a gesture to show our mettle. As a tech partner, Radix continually re-thinks to simplify the most difficult needs and helps to climb the highest rung in the success ladder for our current and future global clientele,” says Dharmesh Acharya, COO- Radixweb.

Echoing the thoughts of the technology think-tank at Radixweb, Pratik Mistry- Sr. Business Head, states, “Without doubt, technologies are evolving like never before. At Radix, freshening up our website is a testimony that we are more than ready to grab the technology evolution by its horns and ride it smoothly for our customers. It is one of the many things we do to attain every idea, brief and expectation put forth before us”

Speaking about the must-look aspects of the new website, Sarrah Pitaliya, Digital Marketing Manager, quips, “The skill to constantly “Stay Wow” has been the crux of our success story. I believe, it is evident to breathe newness in the story of web presence with stunning visuals, easy-to-use navigation, and engaging concepts by letting creativity graze ideas freely. The ultimate result for Radix ‘Gutsy look’ unleashes a fresh breeze of innovation to our services par excellence and showcases our brand identity in a visually delightful way.”

Whether it is for the new-age offerings, ecstatic visuals, innovative thought-leadership or never-seen-before concepts, Radixweb will surely be a new destination to look out for new ideas and its technology executions.
