# COVID-19 is raging, but so is our battle against the disruption

Mar 22, 2020

**In the face of the COVID-19 pandemic, Radixweb Showcases Rigorous Commitment to drive business continuity while safeguarding stakeholders.**

A letter to our employees, customers, and partners

Firstly we would like to wish you safety and health as these are our priorities. It is of vital importance to us that our employees, customers, partners and communities feel supported as we all work through this global crisis. We would also like to take this opportunity to assure you that your project/business/career is in good hands. Even in this critical time, there will be no perceivable hindrance or disturbance in our core systems and the services we provide.

As we know, the world is grappling with an issue of enormous scale and human impact, and our hearts go out to all who have been affected by the outbreak of COVID-19. We write this in light of the COVID 19 pandemic and the response plans we at Radixweb have implemented to maintain business processes and continuity.

Radixweb as a company has implemented key measures to protect our employees and they are well equipped to protect client business. The impact of COVID-19 is forcing us all to manage turbulence at the intersection of our personal and professional lives in ways we haven’t before. The ability to nurture the employee, customer and partner experience at an organizational level during this time is critical. Our payoff is the innovation that will pull our organization through tumultuous times.

  * Radixweb has moved to 100% remote working. All employees have been rigged with mission-critical equipment to ensure smooth business operations and on time delivery of projects without business interruption. Here is a glimpse at how we at Radixweb are shifting our base of operation to our homes.

In these unprecedented times uncertainty runs amok, but we would like to assure you that the team at Radixweb is here to help. Our assistance shall extend to continued delivery of existing projects, covering for staff who are no longer available for work or providing advice on how to continue business operations even through the current disruption.

  * In every decision Radixweb makes, we strive to balance our most urgent mission – to be here for our people when they need us most – with ensuring the safety of our employees, customers and partners.
  * Radixweb has been serving the industry for the past 20 years and we are well equipped to adapt to the needs of the day. We are pressing ahead on all projects utilizing remote tools when and as needed to offer seamless business continuity.

We at Radixweb will continue to stay committed to your business and help you bring value oriented output even in these unprecedented times. As we navigate this global pandemic, we will continue to build technology solutions optimized for the business environment of the future. Do feel free to reach out to us with any questions or concerns that you might have. Radixweb, as always, is at your service.

**P.S - We're doing our best to flatten the curve to stop the spread of Novel Coronavirus in these challenging times, and we hope that you are also taking adequate precautions to stay safe. Remember to wash your hands often and for twenty seconds, don't touch your face, and practice social distancing.**

**Stay safe, stay healthy.**
