# Radixweb Announces its Global Expansion Plan with New Office in Frisco, US

Aug 20, 2023

 _**Quick Run-through:** We are pleased to announce our latest expansion plan of opening a new Frisco office in the United States, after successfully delivering software solutions to 3000+ global clients. The Frisco office will serve as an innovation hub for better client engagement, localized support, and addressing the dynamic technological requirements of our diverse clientele in this region._

We, at Radixweb, are ready to expand our global footprint with the establishment of our second innovation hub in the US located at 6136 Frisco Square Blvd Suite 400, Frisco, TX 75034. This strategic presence marks a significant step in our expansion plan, ensuring our commitment to deliver maximum ROI and IT value to our US client base.

As a premier offshore software development service provider, we are set to be a catalyst for being responsive and easily accessible to our US clientele for better meeting their dynamic needs. Despite, our new office leverages diverse local talent that not only delivers cutting-edge solutions but also bridges the cultural gap and strengthens our partnership with key industry leaders.

What can the industry expect from our new venture? A strong focus on localized client support, better engagement with real-time data analytics, and impactful custom software development services to redefine the local client experience.

Since our inception in 2000, we have extended our IT consultation and development services across 25 global markets, realizing the digital transformation needs of innumerable global businesses. Driven by our contribution towards innovation in technology development, we have consistently been acknowledged as the most trusted ‘offshore IT Consulting Services provider.’

"We are exceptionally pleased to open our new Frisco office which is a significant milestone in our regional expansion strategy," said Dharmesh Acharya, Chief Operating Officer, Radixweb. " Most importantly, we are truly grateful for the trust that our global clientele has extended over these 23 years. It is a strong testament to our vision of embracing the journey of the next century in Frisco, better serve our clients, and continue our rapid growth prospects.”

Recently, we marked our 23rd year of operation with an annual pledge to “Vision Customer Xcellence” - a transformative upskilling campaign for our internal stakeholders, empowering them with relevant and modern tech capabilities to create exceptional customer experiences.

Pratik Mistry, Radixweb’s EVP, Tech Consulting, who spearheads the operations of the new US office further added, “we are beyond delighted to share that our expansion decision comes at an opportune time as US industries are expected to dominate the global digital transformation market and seek reliable partners to help them stay competitive. Furthermore, the region is enriched with a diversity of local clients and many of the potential client-base that helps us strengthen partnerships with key industry leaders and build their business eminence. Due to that, this strategic region will prove a beneficial boon in expanding our diverse talent pool, global support department, and software product engineering services.”

Headquartered in Ahmedabad, India, we have marked our presence in several strategic locations, such as Canada, Australia, Morocco, and California in the US. Our expansion plan comes in an exciting series of developments in terms of raking up our capabilities in SaaS development services and mobile app development services – including our advent as a Microsoft Solutions Partner in Digital and App Innovation Azure.
