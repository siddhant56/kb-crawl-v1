# OnPrintShop Web to Print Storefront to Participate in Graph Expo 2011

Jul 19, 2011

OnPrintShop web to print storefront from Radixweb will make its debut appearance at Graph Expo 2011, Chicago, USA at Stall No 967. Slated to be held from September 11-14, 2011, Graph Expo is touted as the graphic communications industry’s top trade exhibition for the Americas.

OnPrintShop is a prepress process solution from India-based Radixweb. Breaking the status quo of the printing industry, OnPrintShop automates prepress processes. The web-enabled solution reduces the ordering lifecycle of a print business while adding 0% to the infrastructure cost. The solution has a Flash based online designer studio that allows the end users of print houses to design their own products online and personalize them.

OnPrintShop allows print houses to collect orders online, track print jobs, and deliver a print-ready PDF file – all through a web browser. Its advanced e-commerce suite supports multiple languages, currencies, payment gateways, and shipping options. The solution also offers a corporate module with secure storage for digital assets. But what makes OnPrintShop a real hit is the customization possibilities and availability in both dedicated and hosted (SaaS) versions.

After a successful run at PrintPack India 2011, IndiaSoft 2011, and CeBIT 2011, OnPrintShop will look to steal the show at Graph Expo also. “We are looking forward to Graph Expo. We consider this to be a great opportunity for us to touch-base with some elite printers from the Americas”, comments **Mr. Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb** . This being the fourth event for OnPrintShop in 2011 the solution appears to be on a high. “We would definitely look to repeat our success of PrintPack, IndiaSoft, and CeBIT at Graph Expo. In all previous events we had quite diverse audience segments. But we still earned a lot of fans. We are now eyeing the Americas and are confident that we shall be well received”, informs Mr Acharya.

But print service providers is not the only segment OnPrintShop is targeting at Graph Expo. “We are interested in forming some meaningful business coalitions at Graph Expo. So we will definitely focus on some B2B networking as well”, revealed Mr Acharya.
