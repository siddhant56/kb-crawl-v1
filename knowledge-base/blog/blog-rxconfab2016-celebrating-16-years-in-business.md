# RXConfab2016 - Celebrating 16 Years in Business

Sarrah Pitaliya

Published : Jul 14, 2016

Founded in the year 2000, which makes 2016 as the year of completing 16 years of operations. It’s a long and memorable journey so far with a number of employees crossing the 350 mark and having worked on 2500 projects and having 1500+ clients from 25 countries spread over 5 continents – a milestone worth to share.

With the motto of, identify the need and persevere to meet it - The team believes relationships are built on respect and hence our clients, partners and employees are viewed as associates and treated with professionalism and respect. We give utmost importance to clarity and transparency and believe in simplifying things for our customers. We do not see ourselves merely as an outsourcing partner but as an extension of the business, delivering cost savings.

> Heading ahead with golden past, shaping the future together. Radixweb gearing up to celebrate its sweet 16.
