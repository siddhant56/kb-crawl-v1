# Radixweb Develops Cloud-Based Recruitment Software for the New Business Ecosystem

Apr 21, 2022

"Our customer wanted to use cloud-based recruitment tools to take advantage of automation and avoid the issues that arise with a human mistake." Mr. Dharmesh Acharya, COO of Radixweb, says, "They easily sidestep problems of installation, hosting, and data security by leveraging domain experience of our technocrats."

The ability to create custom cloud-based recruiting software allows businesses to expand beyond basic recruitment tasks such as administration and database management. It also expands the possibilities for integrating new attendance and time tracking modules.

Mr. Pratik Mistry, VP of Sales at Radixweb, adds that "Custom cloud-based software solutions enable your firm to establish a continuous feedback loop that allows managers and workers to connect instantaneously, as well as 360-degree reviews that cover both internal and external sources."

There has been a considerable growth in the number of employees who work from home in recent years. In today's world, virtual team collaboration software and cloud app development services have never been more important. Companies can engage with prospective employees who want to attend screening from their homes using Radixweb's cloud-based recruitment tools. A continual flow of information keeps prospective employees informed about the company and its activities, resulting in soft benefits like high retention rates and hard ones like screening and project time monitoring.

Companies can be confident that if they allocate cloud-based recruitment software development resources to their biz-ops, the ratio of business activities to staff numbers will decline considerably. The organization now requires fewer staff as a result of the software's automation. This implies it saves money on employee pay while still completing projects on time.

What's hot in the tech sector right now is the expansion of recruitment software's capabilities to include post-recruitment operations. Radixweb's recruitment software was at the heart of the cloud-based program's unique automation and transformations, which included staff tracking, payroll, time and attendance, performance reviews, and streamlined administration.

Business logic demands that when you, as an enterprise, decide on a specific software solution, you have to evaluate the cost it would take to deploy to solution and clearly build an understanding of your existing and projected operating costs. ROI is of crucial importance. Just make sure that you look at it not from a myopic perspective but through a long-term lens. As a parting note, the efficiency and potential scalability that a software development company can offer match and exceed even the harshest and most conservative ROI projections.

When deciding on a software solution, it goes without saying that you should weigh the cost of deployment and usage against your existing and projected operating costs without the software. But don't forget to think about the efficiency and potential for expansion that the tools a software development services company can provide.
