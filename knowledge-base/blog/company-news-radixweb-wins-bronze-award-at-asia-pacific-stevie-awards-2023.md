# Radixweb Takes Home the Innovation in Technology Award by Stevie® at the Asia-Pacific Awards 2023

Apr 19, 2023

 _We are thrilled to announce that Radixweb has been honored with the**Bronze Stevie ® Award for Innovation in the Technology category at the prestigious 10th annual Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards 2023**. We are proud to receive this recognition for our exceptional contribution to the evolving technology industry, commitment to innovation, excellence, and customer satisfaction._

Since our inception in 2000, we have established ourselves as a trailblazer in the world of custom software development and IT consulting. We have a deep-seated culture of constant upskilling and innovation which is reflected in our unwavering commitment to meeting the evolving needs of our clients. With an intense focus on the latest trends and futuristic advancements, we constantly leverage front-line technology, industry expertise, and customer-centric solutions that can help our clients stay ahead of the competition.

Over the last 23 years, we have leveraged cutting-edge technologies, including artificial intelligence, blockchain, and many more. With our advanced technology consulting services, we have helped industry-leading players in the global IT market grow sustainably, leading them towards supreme business evolution.

Radixweb has brought home the Bronze Stevie Award after competing with more than 800 nominations from the Asia-Pacific region this year. Our powerful, open-source, and PWA-ready framework, TezJs, has been praised for its ability to deliver high scalability, performance, and precise solutions.

"We are incredibly proud to have TezJS recognized as a truly amazing and ground-breaking technology", said Dharmesh Acharya, CEO at Radixweb. "This award is a testament to the dedication and innovative minds of our entire team in providing remarkable technology solutions to our clients. Over the last six months, we have received three technology innovation awards from top-tier global institutions. At Radixweb, we are constantly pushing the boundaries with futuristic tactics to deliver exceptional customer experiences, and this award is a validation of our efforts. Our mission is to bring our clients intricate software dreams to life."

At Radixweb, we have been at the forefront of leveraging future-ready expertise and the performance-driven Jamstack framework known as TezJS. Embracing innovation, TezJS is a game-changer in the era of web development, providing an exceptional user experience that is sure to satisfy. With a notch-high focus on fast and scalable front-end development, this unique solution commits to 70x higher page load speed, 20x faster build time, and doubling SEO optimization. As noted by the jury during the assessment process, we have a "proven track record of constant innovation alongside TezJS as a high-performing, efficient, and ideal-fit solution that is integral to a great customer experience."

With a highly skilled and proficient team of developers, designers, expert consultants, and marketers, we have never shied away from taking on unique and complex challenges to turn ambitious ideas into successful products. Our commitment to provide consistent, high-performing, and scalable software solutions has earned us a reputation as a game-changing force in the tech industry, with over 3000+ businesses benefiting from our expertise.

By seamlessly blending cutting-edge technology with strategic foresight, we empower businesses to achieve success that sets them apart from their competitors.

And with this award, Stevie has recognized Radixweb's way of strengthening the global dev community for a mutual growth spurt by developing a next-gen framework enabling amazing web experiences, faster front-end development, a zero-learning curve, and extreme security.
