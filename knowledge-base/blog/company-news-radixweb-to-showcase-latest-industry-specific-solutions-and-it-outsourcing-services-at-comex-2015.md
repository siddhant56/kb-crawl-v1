# Radixweb to Showcase Latest Industry Specific Solutions and IT Outsourcing Services at COMEX 2015

Apr 26, 2015

Radixweb is participating in COMEX as a leading IT Outsourcing software vendor and exhibitor; its existence will get the visitors closer to the latest innovations across the technologies with competitive solutions at booth #522

Radixweb, the industry leading provider of IT offshore development, today announced showcasing its globally recognized software development and IT outsourcing services along with Industry specific solutions at upcoming COMEX 2015 - April 27 – May 1, 2015 Muscat, Oman.

COMEX entices top-level decision makers by addressing the requirements of IT professionals to acquire the latest knowledge in order to make cognizant business decisions. Radixweb to exhibit its next-generation technology solutions highlighting the steadily vital role that technology plays in shaping business decisions.

Being a well-established IT services provider globally, the company is taking concrete steps towards increasing its footprint in Middle East Region. Booth visitors will be witness to next-generation IT solutions and will get a chance to have one-on-one discussion with Industry veterans, helping them understand ways and means to improve their business’s productivity and Return on Investment (ROI) by automating their business using advanced technologies.

The company’s special focus for the event is to exhibit it's widely accepted custom software development, industry specific software solutions and outsourcing services that help visitors to define clear strategy to optimize and automate their business processes by leveraging the next generation IT development.

Exhibiting its latest innovations and the technology capabilities for:

  * Custom Application Development
  * CMS & e-Commerce Systems
  * Website Design & Development
  * Enterprise Systems and more

Booth representatives will be present to feature their innovations developed using advanced Microsoft .Net technologies.

Show specials for COMEX 2015 - Exclusive live demonstration of its globally recognized Industry specific software solutions -

  * Insurance Software Solutions
  * Field Service Management Software
  * Printing MIS Software Solutions

“COMEX 2015, the leading ICT event in Oman attracts thousands of IT and Business professionals globally and the exhibition floor offers great occasions for innovative technology companies like Radixweb to demonstrate their robust technology solutions and interact with the attendees. Radixweb has been known for its widely endorsed offshore software development and IT outsourcing services along with the competitive industry specific solutions for business globally. Our next move is to take definite steps towards growing our footprint in Middle East Region. We are delighted to showcase our expertise in It Solutions, Development & Customization, Industry Specific Software, Graphic Design and Image Processing to booth visitors. COMEX 2015 is an ideal platform and forum for demonstrating our technical specialties for varied business out there, and hence, I am very thrilled about the show”, commented Pratik Mistry, Sr Business Head, Radixweb.

See you @ booth #522 COMEX 2015!
