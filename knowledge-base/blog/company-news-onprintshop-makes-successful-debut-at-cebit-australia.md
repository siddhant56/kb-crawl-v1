# OnPrintShop Makes Successful Debut at CeBIT Australia

May 28, 2012

OnPrintShop web to print solution made a successful debut at recently concluded CeBIT Australia 2012. Following an extremely power packed drupa 2012 at Düsseldorf, Germany, OnPrintShop showcased web to print in a whole new light at CeBIT Australia.

With large number of visitors and exhibitors, CeBIT Australia is one of the most important events of Australian business calendar. Thus it gave us an opportunity to showcase OnPrintShop to a premier crowd. We exhibited a web to print solution that was simple, affordable, and highly customizable. We displayed our latest online designer studio with advanced PDF engine and CorelDraw engine, our newly launched online photo album and calendar solution, and vendor/print broker module. Our visitors wanted to know more about our advanced variable data printing solution and multiple stores with central admin. We also stirred excitement with a glimpse of our mobile to print solution.

We had launched web to print consultancy at drupa. We offered the same at CeBIT Australia as well. Our guests had pre-booked their visits with us and shared their basic details. Based on this, we gave personalized consultations to them. We arranged a personalized solution demo for them and discussed their requirements in detail. We shared strategies on boosting business using web to print. We further discussed customizations that we can do in our solution to meet their unique business needs.

OnPrintShop also used CeBIT Australia as a business networking event. We participated in keynote address of the event and post-event press conferences. We promoted web to print through print and electronic media. We met up with technology companies and explored different hardware and software integration possibilities.

Now post event, it's time for action. We extend a hearty thank you to all our visitors and get down to delivering on the commitments we have made. We shall also look for ways to improve our solution based on the insights gained at the event.

Next we shall focus on **Graph Expo 2012** , scheduled to be held at **Chicago, USA from October 7-10, 2012** . Graph Expo is one of the top trade exhibitions for graphic communications industry of Americas.

We request Graph Expo visitors to pre-register at https://www.onprintshop.com/graphexpo for a personalized solution consultation and demo.
