# Radixweb Declared nopCommerce Partner

Aug 5, 2012

India-based IT services provider Radixweb has been declared an official nopCommerce partner. This partnership status adds one more certification to Radixweb’s already impressive wall of credentials.

nopCommerce is an open source e-commerce framework for ASP.Net 4.0. It has a highly stable and usable shopping cart that can be customized for different business needs. The flexibility and scalability of nopCommerce has made it a very popular e-commerce solution development framework.

For Radixweb, who is a Microsoft Gold Certified Partner, nopCommerce was a natural extension of services. Specializing in .Net development platform, Radixweb develops custom e-commerce applications with robust shopping carts. We develop nopCommerce solutions with features like:

  * Dynamic product galleries
  * Shopping cart
  * Payment & shipping gateway support
  * Customer accounts
  * Easy to use backend

We use nopCommerce when the applications have special needs of:

  * Product listing and tagging
  * One-page checkout for online shopping
  * Real-time currency exchange rates
  * Mobile version of the website for mCommerce
  * Support for different taxes
  * Discount, coupons, and reward points facility

The nopCommerce partnership validates our abilities to customize and develop special features for typical business requirements. It means that we can develop tailored modules and plugins for nopCommerce. It recognizes our specialty of integration custom designs and custom shopping carts in nopCommerce solutions. The partnership status also connects us with a global network of other solution partners. This broadens our exposure and gives us an opportunity to engage in global knowledge exchange.

The partnership also means that we can offer quick and easy upgrades to our customers. We can upgrade nopCommerce solutions with the latest features in rapid and cost-effective manner. It enables us to provide timely and effective technical support to our clients. We can also integrate third party APIs in nopCommerce solutions for added business value.

nopCommerce partner is the latest in Radixweb’s string of latest achievements. Not long ago the company has achieved Sitefinity and Kentico partner recognitions. This shows that Radixweb has diversified into Microsoft COTS technologies as well for commercial solutions. In addition to this Radixweb is ISO certified for quality processes. We are also a registered member of D&B for our knowledge repository.

To know more about our nopCommerce development services visit https://radixweb.com/nopcommerce-development.
