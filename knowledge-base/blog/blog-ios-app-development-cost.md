## FAQs

### How long does it take to develop an iOS app?

The average time to build a basic iOS app would take about two to four months. For medium-level iOS app development can take up to four to six months; complex iOS apps can take more than nine months.

### Is there any price difference in developing apps for iOS or Android?

There is a decent price difference between the average cost to develop an iPhone app and an Android app. The average iOS app development cost is around $10,000-$250,000. And the average Android app development cost can vary from around $20,000-$300,000. But again, these figures may differ based on several factors, such as app complexity, features and functionalities, development team, etc.

### How much does it cost to hire an iOS developer?

Hiring a junior-level iOS developer would cost around USD 30 - USD 60 per hour. And hiring a more experienced and senior-level iOS developer would cost you around USD 60 - USD 100.
