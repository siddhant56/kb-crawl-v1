# Radixweb Cherishes the Year 2017 Milestones with Gratitude

Nov 29, 2017

As the Year of AI- 2017 Comes to an End- Radixweb Takes a Moment to Marvel at the Technology Milestones Achieved by the Adolescent Company

We are at it again. Like every year, around this time companies are on the threshold- they look back to see how radically technology has transformed through the last 365 days and look forward to discern the right strategies to deliver beyond expectations for the customers. Radixweb fits the bill with gratitude in tow.

2017 has been highly fructitious for the IT Outsourcing and Software Development company with a trail of note-worthy achievements. The year began with a coveted recognition for their technocrat abilities and experience as they bagged the champion slot in Top .Net developers 2017 by Clutch. The company has built an enviable portfolio to craft rich technology solutions from .Net language. For the past 17 years, the company has delivered master-class services to business of all sizes across the globe.

Making indelible footprints in the Middle East region, Radixweb seized tremendous success at the biggest ICT event- COMEX in Muscat for third year in a row. The visitors thronged the booth for bouquet of futuristic technology tailored to meet specific business needs.

In spring, Radixweb drenched itself in a fresh new coat of colours, visuals and emotions. Transforming its digital identity with an invigorating approach, the company displayed top-notch leadership with an evolved website to outperform demands of new-age IT.

> For Radixweb, a prominent name in the field of global IT Outsourcing and Services, this year has been an ideal mix of Whiz-bang technology innovation and bringing sleek digital transformation for its global clientele

“Technology is all around us. It is evolving at a blink and you miss speed. We make a difference by not waiting and wondering about what our next step will be when digital innovations change things; rather, in this era of smart enterprise- we help you stay clearly ahead and anticipate what’s next? We are behind the wheel and shape technology to fit customer needs, big or small. Get a new disruptive direction with Radixweb. Choose to be disruptor this time,” says Dharmesh Acharya, COO at Radixweb.

How to choose to be a disruptor was best signified when the company toured the USA for two months in the last quarter of the year to bring “In-Person” innovation for its clientele. The top brass at Radixweb firmly believes that market leaders have to move beyond their screens, calls and meetings to ‘stay-in-touch’ with clients to craft the vision together and improve business partnerships.

“Our clients and teams are the rock reason behind the success that our company has witnessed over the past 365 days. It is delightful to see how easily our collaborations have wafted across the test of time and technology in a crispy way. Their unwavering commitment to our partnerships and relationships are the differentiators- yes, we are overwhelmingly thankful to our customers,” adds Acharya.

On the home-turf, 2017 marked Radixweb’s journey to adolescence as it starred into eighteen. Eulogizing glorious years of smooth sail in the choppy waters of technology, the teams celebrated the voyage with pomp and splendour.

“It’s been a helluva ride this year, we expect 2018 to be smashing better,” gleams Acharya as he signs off.For companies looking to transcend to disruption with the sunrise of the New Year, say a Happy Hello to Radixweb!
