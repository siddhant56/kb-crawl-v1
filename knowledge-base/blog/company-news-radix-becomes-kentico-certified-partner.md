# Radix Becomes Kentico Certified Partner

Feb 11, 2013

Radixweb, India-based mature IT services provider, has become a Certified Partner of Kentico – a web content management system vendor. The certified partner status adds another feather to our cap and recognizes our expertise in developing robust and scalable Kentico applications.

Kentico is an advanced and flexible web content management system with versioning and workflow management and permission based editing. There are 16,000+ websites that use Kentico for CMS. It is being used by popular global names like McDonald’s, Audi, Samsung, Fujifilm, and Vodafone.

Radix uses Kentico to develop high-end solutions like interactive enterprise websites, online e-commerce stores, intranets, extranets, web portals, and community websites.

As a Certified Partner, Radix joins a network of 1200+ Kentico partners from 80+ countries. We get technical assistance and support from Kentico’s technical team as well as partner network. Certified partner recognition also means we offer superior quality of development, integration and consulting services for Kentico.

“Radixweb had attained Kentico’s partner status in 2012. And within a year we have become Certified Partner. This speaks a lot about our ability”, states **Pratik Mistry, Global Business Head, Radixweb** .

What benefits do our customers get because of this Certified Partner status?

  * Technical expertise of highest level
  * Latest set of features in their application
  * Licensed use of latest upgrades and releases
  * Adherence to global best practices
  * Rapid development and reduced time to go live
  * Indirect support of global Kentico partner network
  * All-in-one solution with flexibility and scalability

A Kentico Certified Partner gets better global exposure and access to Kentico’s knowledge base. We get easy access to Kentico’s whitepapers, case studies, and other documentation which is again a value addition.

Radix has been awarded the certified partner recognition for its technical expertise in ASP.Net, industry experience, and quality standards. We develop Kentico solutions with a wide range of features like image galleries, forums and blogs, document management with WYSIWYG editor, online polls and newsletters, multilingual support, integration with web analytics. But what really sets us apart from our competitors is our ability to customize Kentico. “Custom development is our core strength”, reveals Mr. Mistry.

We develop and integrate custom features in Kentico to meet unique business requirements of our clients. We specialize in:

  * Designing & integrating special themes & templates
  * Developing unique modules & plug-ins
  * Programming special functionalities
  * Integrating third party APIs
  * Modeling typical business workflow

Our Kentico development portfolio is available on https://www.kentico.com/Partners/Radixweb.

One more aspect that gives us a competitive advantage as Kentico Certified Partner is our development approach. We follow the DevOps as a Service methodology to efficiently manage a project through its entire lifecycle.

**Analysis:** We start by analyzing the requirements of our clients and understanding their business objectives.

**Design:** We design a website that is stunning, representative of their business, and user-friendly.

**Develop:** We develop and customize the website in Kentico CMS to meet their business goals.

**Integrate:** We integrate third party tools and APIs if needed by their business workflow.

**Train:** We give admin panel walkthrough to help our clients understand how to manage the website and its content.

**Handover:** On completion we hand over the application source code and all intellectual property.

**Optimize:** We share online marketing strategies with clients to help them optimize their website.

**Support:** We support and consult our clients on Kentico and other open source Dotnet technologies.

“We do not see ourselves as just developers and programmers. We are business partners of our clients. So we offer consultations that help them select right technology and get maximum return out of their investments”, says Mr. Mistry.

In addition to Kentico Certified Partner, Radix has a long list of credentials which include Microsoft Gold Certified Partner, Adobe Solution Partner, ISO Benchmark, Sitefinity Partner, nopCommerce Partner, and D&B Registered Member.
