# The Human Element in Tech Transformation: Hear My Story of Driving Change and Adoption

Pratik Mistry

Published : Feb 7, 2024

 _**15-Second Summary:** In this thought-provoking article, our EVP Mr. Pratik Mistry shares an intimate and personal exploration of the human component within the tech sphere. Drawing upon his years of experience leading Radixweb, the author delves into the pivotal role played by human-centric values in reshaping tech transformation._

As someone who is knee-deep in the mayhem of tech advancements and digitization initiatives, I’ve got a little confession to make.

We all have been witnessing cross-industry inspirations from around the globe, whether it’s adopting AI-powered software in retail firms or integrating IoT devices in manufacturing processes. Yet, to my notice, within this fervent pursuit of acquiring breakthroughs and enterprise digital transformations lies the struggle to retain something quintessential – the human element.

From where I stand, investing in new technologies alone doesn’t shape a software solution provider into a genuinely human-centric business. There's a fine line between adopting tech-driven solutions for the sake of it and leveraging them purposefully to create business value.

Across industries, market niches, and office walls, people crave the human touch and relatable experiences. The desire for genuine connections leading to responsiveness about pressing business concerns is something that transcends the value of any tech jargon or corporate fancy talk.

So, how do we shift our business models to prioritize this human-focused approach? How do we infuse authenticity into every interaction, channel, and touchpoint?

The answer might lie in reconnecting with our primal senses—the touch, the feel, the intuitiveness that draws people in. Organizations embracing this ethos tend to witness elevated customer responsiveness, deeper relationships, and heightened client retention.

My realization has been fueled by navigating cultural differences, delivering sustainable outcomes, and steering client successes at Radixweb. And, it’s this understanding that has inspired me to pen down my thoughts on the interplay of technology and humanity in digital transformation.

## The Art and Science of Cross-Cultural Awareness

As an Executive Vice President, Technology, my days are a blend of strategy, innovation, and most importantly, understanding the pulse of our clients’ needs.

Our existing process in North America laid the groundwork. Sure, I had been shuttling back and forth earlier, but being invariably stationed here was a deliberate move. My role goes beyond being just physically present to being the face of our core values and management perspectives in a space where our clients feel most comfortable.

In all honesty, we wanted to bring that familiar face; the voice that echoes our pledge loud and clear. I can engage with clients more meaningfully, promptly address their concerns, and showcase not just our solutions but our ethos and dedication firsthand.

It's a proactive stance and a conscious effort to go the extra mile and strengthen our presence where our clients are.

From this vantage point, there are moments when I pause and wonder about how we’ve come a long way understanding, respecting, and celebrating the differences that make us stronger. Sure, it comes with its challenges, dealing with time zones, language barriers, and differing work etiquettes, but that’s the beauty of it – embracing the differences and a wealth of insights that shape our approach to client success.

But how does this connect with tech transformation, you would ask?

For one thing, technology knows no borders, yet it must speak the language of those who use it. As we craft our software solutions, there's the infusion of this understanding. Every line of code and each digital product bears the touch of the human factor - blending modern technology with a profound awareness of client needs and cultural subtleties.

In this context, I must talk about the contrast between supportive and proactive mindsets that not only form the crux of how we incorporate the human factor in tech transformation but also determine the depth of our engagement and the resilience of our solutions:

**Supportive Mindset**| **Proactive Mindset**
---|---
We gather client feedback to improve our services and solutions.| We anticipate and address client needs even before they arrive.
We embrace change and encourage our teams to adopt new approaches.| We carefully lead the change and make room for innovation.
We acknowledge cultural differences and consider their impact on operations.| We champion cultural diversity to create inclusive strategies.
It's our job to resolve client queries.| Predicting pain points in the client’s business process is one of our priorities.
We care about long-term goals and sustainable outcomes| We balance immediate crisis resolution with a strategic eye on the future.

## Beyond Quick Fixes to Sustainable Transformation

Speaking of long-term success, tech solutions aren’t just a patchwork but a catalyst for generating value. We're not in the business of slapping band-aids on problems, which is what many people assume when they outsource for cost savings. Instead, we dive headfirst into the bigger picture: what keeps our clients up at night.

When a client approaches us with pain points, sure we’ve got the tech chops to patch it up, but we take a step further. Instead of solving the problem at hand, we try to flip the script and redefine the vision for the better.

You'll find several ordinary development firms that solely tick off tasks on a checklist. But that's not how you do it. You need to go above and beyond to build trust and deliver tech solutions that stand the bumps, the highs, and the test of time.

Our mantra is not to measure success by the number of problems fixed but to transform challenges into opportunities and propel the business towards IT sustainability and growth.

So, how deep are you willing to dig? Are you ready to explore beyond the surface and reshape your organization's destiny?

## Embracing Holistic Solutions over Cost-Cutting

Let's talk turkey here. We've already busted the myth that outsourcing is solely about slashing costs. Yes, that’s part of the equation but there’s a whole world of possibilities beyond that. The recipe for success is a flavorful mix of human-centricity, problem-solving, and innovation.

Think about how often do we get caught up in dollars and cents, forgetting that behind every transaction lies a human story? So, while we crunch numbers and brainstorm groundbreaking solutions, we’re also creating a connection, an experience that resonates with the client on a personal level.

It's about addressing the bottlenecks that might not show up on the balance sheet but weigh heavy on their daily grind.

Our shift from cost-cutting to a creative powerhouse stems from this ethos. After all, technology is meant to make lives easier, more efficient, and more meaningful. And that’s where the human touch becomes pivotal.

## Human-Centricity as the Backbone of Change and Adoption

Embracing change and an adaptive mindset is the way of business. We don’t shy away from pushing boundaries and challenging the status quo. However, innovation doesn’t only come from groundbreaking ideas but by infusing creativity into every aspect of problem-solving. How can we leverage technology not just for the sake of advancement, but to genuinely impact lives in a positive way?

Technology isn’t the be-all and end-all. It's definitely the catalyst for change, but the heart of purpose-led transformation lies in understanding and catering to the human element. It should enhance human experiences, not diminish them.

Such an approach fuels curiosity, encourages experimentation, and helps adopt and adapt to novel technologies and methodologies.

## Reinforcing Tech Transformation with Human Touch

As Neil Gunther rightly pointed out, “Considering the speed at which pretty much everything in our lives is changing, we, the human factor, remain as the essential governing factor. We can’t follow the steep path if the rate of change is too great.”

This realization calls for leaders to prioritize the integration of human elements with technological evolution. Technology, while awe-inspiring in its capabilities, should augment the innate qualities of empathy, understanding, and problem-solving inherent in human interaction.

As we stand at the nexus of change and adoption, I urge you to form a vision in which technology doesn’t just transform industries but elevates human experiences, fosters connections, and drives meaningful change.

Here's to a future where technology and humanity go hand in hand to forge a path towards progress and possibility.
