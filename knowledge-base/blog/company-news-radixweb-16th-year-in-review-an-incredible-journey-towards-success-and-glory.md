# Radixweb 16th Year in Review: Journey towards Success and Glory

Jul 19, 2016

What a splendid year it was for Radixweb! It was a year of growth, achievements, awards, and recognition. We were, we will and we are unstoppable and the journey continues.

We are fortunate enough to have built a firm having people who are skillful, ambitious and dedicated to transforming how the organization all over the world do business every day. We would not have been here without our team, partners, and clients.

These people are the one who inspires us on a daily basis and makes the work we do as a company worth it. Every year we get more and more excited during our annual anniversary event, where the impact resonates for many days. The personal stories of individuals whose careers have flourished after joining Radixweb enthrall us every year.

In honor of their incredible successes and growth we saw as a company in 2015-16, we would like to take a moment to celebrate with Radixwebs 16th year in Review. In the past year:

## Collection of Radixweb Moments of Year 2015-16

## More Highlights Served in Words below Which Digs it Deep Inside:

  * We were part of Graph Expo 2015 for fifth consecutive year and launched Web2Print solution major upgrade v5.0 along with number of innovations in the print industry.
  * We participated in Comex 2016 at Muscat, Oman and it was a pleasure to meet our clients from middle-east region and strengthen our relationship.
  * We made our presence felt at HKTDC ICT Expo 2016 at Hong Kong and showcased cutting-edge technology services and solutions along with OnPrintShop, which is the most advanced web-to-print solution.
  * At drupa 2016 held in Germany, we bagged the Social Media Champion award twice and also launched new Web-to-print solutions.
  * An annual business trip to Europe was organized as we are committed towards excellence and believe in enduring client relationship.
  * Apart from the business trips we also immersed ourselves in activities like picnic, sports and celebrating festivals.

In coming years we aim to continue our commitment and excellence while advancing the innovation frontier and we are unstoppable to do so. Honoring the past, moving ahead to sustainable future.

But as we stated earlier, our year in review is not just about us; it’s also about our partners, clients, and team mates. So, Join us in celebrating our 16th anniversary, #RXConfab2016.
