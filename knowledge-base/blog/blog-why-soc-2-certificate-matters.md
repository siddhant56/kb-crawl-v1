## What Is SOC 2 Certification?

SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2) is a standard developed by the AICPA. It evaluates how well a company protects client data based on five principles:

  * Security,
  * Availability,
  * Processing integrity,
  * Confidentiality, and
  * Privacy.

To get certified, an independent auditor reviews the company's policies and operations.

## Proving It Over Time, Not Just on Paper

We’ve always believed that trust comes from consistency, not one-time checks. That's why we went for SOC2 Type II compliance auditing.

Well, Type I is a one-time assessment.

It checks whether a company has the right controls in place at a specific moment. This is a good starting point, but it doesn’t tell you whether those controls actually work over time.

Type II, which we comply with, is far more detailed.

It looks at how well a company follows those controls over a longer period (typically six months or more). It answers a more important question: Is the company consistent in how it protects your data?

Some of our policies that make us enterprise-ready, globally aligned and helped us achieve the certification:

  * Strict role-based access controls in place. Only people with a valid reason can access sensitive data.
  * Ongoing training for every employee to ensure security awareness is part of our daily routine.
  * Incident response plans that allow us to quickly detect, contain, and resolve potential threats.
  * Real-time monitoring tools that watch for unusual system behavior or unauthorized access.
  * Review and assess third-party vendors regularly to make sure their practices align with ours.

None of these systems were put in place just for the audit. They’ve been a part of our internal culture and operational model for years. The certification simply proves that we do what we say and that we keep doing it every day.
