# Radixweb’s Insurance Software Solutions and Software Product Development Competency Proven a Big Hit at COMEX 2015, Oman

May 7, 2015

**Radixweb Demonstrated Wide Range of IT Outsourcing Services & Innovative Industry Solutions, Ranging from Insurance Software Solutions to Print MIS Systems and Software Product Development at COMEX 2015**

Radixweb, the industry leading IT Outsourcing Company successfully grabbed the attention of visitors towards its innovative Software Product Development Competency and Insurance Software Solutions at COMEX 2015 in OMAN last week.

COMEX 2015, the region’s leading ICT trade fair was concluded with pleasant chaos among hundreds of technology vendors and more than 120,000 visitors including wide participation of private and public Institutions at Oman International Exhibition Centre last week.

“The COMEX ICT event breaks new ground each year showcasing the demand to innovate and easily transform. With a clear focus on building a techno-centric cities, Oman's commitment towards this gives us a concrete business opportunity to expand our footprints into the Middle-East region and work closely to deliver software product development best practices,” remarks Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb.

Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Head at Radixweb commented, “This year COMEX brought a remarkable government and private sector technology innovators and decision makers on the floor looking for best-in-class technology products and solutions. Radixweb aims to lead the way towards technology advancement and business automation by catering a IT solutions that thrives on continuous improvements and deployment of state-of-the-art technology changes. We look forward to have a strong presence in Middle East Region with long term business tie-ups and partnership.”

Adding further he stated, “The five-day event brought fruitful occasions for us to showcase our innovative industry specific solutions and software product development competency, delivering a competitive-edge to entrepreneurs and organizations at the global level. Radixweb Insurance Software Solutions and Industry benchmark for Software Maintenance Services proved to be an eye catching on the exhibition floor. A large number of event visitors were attracted to our booth for having a live preview of our unique offerings.”

Conference attendees appreciated and enjoyed demonstration of innovative Industry Software Solutions and IT Outsourcing services at Radixweb booth.
