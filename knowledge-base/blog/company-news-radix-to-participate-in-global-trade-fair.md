# Radix To Participate In Global Trade Fair

Jan 18, 2010

Radix, one of the leading IT services company from Ahmedabad, India, is going to participate in CeBIT, the world’s largest trade fair for the digital industry organized by Deutsche Messe, from March 2 to 6, 2010 at Hannover, Germany. Radix has been allotted Stand A58 in Hall 4 of the sprawling exhibition grounds.

Radix has been in the offshore IT services and development business for a decade, catering to a global clientele in almost all areas of software and web development. Announcing its decision to participate in the event that will draw the cream of the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) world, the company stated that the event would provide a versatile platform for it to display its technical expertise and competence.

Held annually since 1986, CeBIT 2010 will cover the following avenues:

**Business IT** will witness major players providing solutions spanning across BPO, CRM, ECM, ERP, Enterprise Applications, Open Source, Software as a Service, Software and System Management and Internet and Mobile Solutions, among others.

**ICT Infrastructure** will exhibit hardware and network product developers/dealers in the areas of Accessories, Peripherals, Graphics, Office/Conference Equipment, Imaging and Photography and Network Software and Services.

**Future ICT** section will address global trends like where the digital world is headed and how will future technologies change the shape of our economy and society, with reference to research, environment, mobility and user experience.

Conferences will provide an international forum for specialists to share information, exchange ideas, and compare notes. It will include a series of keynote speakers, guided tours, panel discussions and roundtables.

By participating in the event, Radix will demonstrate its experience, expertise and technological capabilities in software development, embedded development and business-specific software solutions for different industry verticals. An ISO 9001:2000 and Microsoft Gold Certified Partner, Radix offers services in software development, technology solutions, custom application development and IT consulting.

CeBIT 2009 witnessed attendance of 4300 exhibitors from 69 countries and some 400,000 visitors. CeBIT 2010 expects to see more.
