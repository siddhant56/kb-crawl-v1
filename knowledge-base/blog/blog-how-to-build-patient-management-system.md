## What is a Patient Management System?

A patient management system is software used by healthcare providers for the management of patient records, billings, follow-ups and other operational work.

For the organizations who implement PMS, it enables them to concentrate on nurturing patient relationships, rather than being weighed down by documentation and other chores. The impact of this can be seen on the healthcare IT solutions market which is forecasted to rise at a CAGR of 15.24% during 2024-2029.

Before the advent of patient management solutions, previous treatment records of a patient could take a lot of time to access. Now, with a single click, all medical and other patient-related information can be easily obtained. Thereby introducing drastic changes in the functional aspects of hospitals and improving patient management.

## Three Major Reasons to Opt for Patient Management Software Development

Having known what a PMS is, now it’s time to understand what drives healthcare organizations to adopt online patient management software development.

**1\. To Amp-Up Operational Efficiency**

Software for patient management helps healthcare organizations automatize routine tasks such as scheduling, billing, and records management. Hence, healthcare providers have ample time to focus on patients instead of managing several documents and filling out medical forms.

Integrated systems and a tight control environment for workflows and documentation ensure the most recent information is on hand. Hence, this reduces the time needed to order and execute the process and mitigates errors. All in all, you can definitely smoothen out the workflows to attend to more patients, given you hire the right custom healthcare solution partner to develop an ideal PMS for your organization.

**2\. To Control Expenses and Errors-Propensity**

The use of PMS results in lowering costs and the number of errors. Automation entails several processes that do not require many administrators, reducing payroll costs. Besides, it minimizes the possible use of documentation, saving on paper and other stationery expenses. As the billing and insurance claims are integrated into the software, proper and timely payments are assured.

Therefore, the losses that parties incur due to denial of the claims and delayed payments are well addressed. Reducing the amount of paperwork also helps cut costs and avoid several errors concerning the records of the patients, the number of charges, and any other activity that requires handling numbers.

**3\. To Enhance Patient Satisfaction and Standard of Care**

PMS helps improve the patient’s satisfaction status and the quality of services. Patient management is an important task, and solutions significantly enhance these aspects. Some of the factors include patient portals that allow patients easy access to their records and even appointments and the ability of patients to communicate with their providers.

Among the advantages of adopting practices like automated alerts for appointments and medication is patient health status enhancement. Also, bureaucratic procedures take less time, resulting in quicker delivery of services and thus making the patients happier. Combined with improved patient engagement and best communication with patients, it introduces a higher quality of treating patients and a more positive attitude of patients towards clinics and hospitals.

## Top Patient Management System Features You Must Look Out For!

The hospital staff may be occupied with attending patients and unable to give enough time and resources to other administrative work. In such cases, the patient management system is a boon as it integrates many features for healthcare professionals, including physicians, optometrists, dentists, etc.

**1\. Medical Records:** PMS helps healthcare professionals gather, analyze, and access patient records. It enables you to revise, update, and record medical records to generate alerts and make follow-up appointments. These medical records include different information formats such as patient files, scan reports, lab results, prescriptions, etc.

**2\. Appointment Scheduling:** Patients can conveniently book an appointment, reschedule, or cancel it through the online portal. It is a source of real-time information, enabling healthcare providers to manage their calendars efficiently. It helps limit no-show rates through automated messages and confirmations. This feature increases organizational effectiveness while increasing the client's satisfaction due to a friendly and flexible way of scheduling appointments.

**3\. Health Network:** The health network feature can connect all the providers and enable easy information sharing. It delivers care in collaboration with other health systems and networks, providing providers with complete views of patients' medical records and management plans. This interdependency further improves patient outcomes as more decision-makers give the correct information to manage the patient's case.

**4\. Patient Portal:** The patient portal brings patient engagement and safely isolates them with their health records, lab results, and treatment plans. It enables patients to contact the clinician for consultation and subsequent follow-ups, thus increasing patient care and satisfaction. In the same way, some of the convenient services range from payments of bills and prescription refills to educational materials that are accessible through the patient portal, ultimately enhancing healthcare management.

**5\. Automatic Reminder System:** Such a system provides notification of appointments, prescription refills due, and the next preventive care activity. This is valuable since it reduces missed appointments and improves patients' adherence to treatment plans. This increases the efficiency in the delivery of healthcare. Notifications can be set to cover the frequency of email/SMS or phone calls so that patients can be reminded through their selected mode of communication.

**6.Revenue Cycle Management:** The patient revenues cycle can be defined as the process through which all interactions with patients in terms of revenues are completed in the hospital. This feature improves a healthcare facility's financial outcomes by ensuring that the codes used for claims are appropriate. Periodic financial reports presented in the software and other economic data supports strategic decision-making to enhance the management of financials and ensure healthcare institutions' financial stability and profitability.

## How to Develop a Successful Patient Management System?

Developing patient management software can be a successful venture with the perfect tools and approach. Here are the basic steps you need to follow to do so:

**1\. Identify Your Objectives** \- First things first, determine what you want your PMS to accomplish. Think thoroughly and list the key functionalities you want your software to perform.

**2\. Research Compliance Regulations** – Gain a basic knowledge of HIPAA and other data security standards in your region. It will help you ensure data security and patient confidentiality from day one.

**3\. Choose the Ideal Tech Stack** \- Choosing the ideal stack will help minimize compatibility issues, ultimately making the development process easier, causing fewer delays, and preventing added costs.

**4\. Develop an Intuitive UI** \- Your software should be easy to navigate for both healthcare providers and patients. Create wireframes and mockups to visualize the UX before diving into development.

**5\. Implement Necessary Software Capabilities** \- Start with bare, then integrate more advanced features and functionalities based on user requirements.

**6\. Rigorous Testing** – Perform usability testing to ensure your app is bug-free and easy to use. To collect real-world feedback, you can also consider beta testing with selected healthcare experts.

**7\. Launch** \- Once everything is fine, it’s time to launch! But yes, don’t just let the bygones be bygones; keep your channels open for user reviews.

**8\. Ongoing Support and Maintenance** – Plan on frequent updates as they fix issues and help stay compliant with new protocols.

## Implementing Patient Management System Software: How Much Does It Cost?

As with most types of healthcare software, the cost will largely depend on the specific patient management software functions, assistance, and adjustments required. Then, one can describe any requirements to the software developers. Hence, the cost of the patient management system software involves the whole development process and can significantly differ based on several factors, which are as follows -

**1\. Primary Software:** PMS with basic functionalities for a small clinic or a medical practice. The cost may begin at about $200 - $500. These systems usually perform simple operations such as generating bills, setting appointments, and record-keeping patient information.

**2\. Mid-Range Software:** The approximate cost should fall between $500 - $1500 per month for even more convenient patient management software with expanded possibilities like EHR/EMR, medical billing, and patient portals. These mid-range systems are often implemented in specialty offices or large clinics.

**3\. Enterprise Solutions:** For the described needs of large healthcare organizations, such as hospitals or healthcare networks, it is possible to require reliable and flexible software solutions. The monthly cost for these enterprise-level systems could be from $1500 to $5000 or more, depending on the degree and the level of customization required.

**4\. One-Time Cost:** Depending on the given features and the program scalability, some suppliers offer a one-time license fee in which the program is sold to the license. This cost may amount to a few thousand dollars to tens of thousands of dollars.

**5\. Additional Expenditures:** One must also add other usage costs such as the initial implementation cost, cost for any required training of users, the additional recurring cost for continual support and maintenance, and the potential one-time or recurring cost for integrating the software with the organizations different types of medical software.

## Factors to Consider While Choosing the Right Patient Information Management System

Choosing or developing the ideal patient information management system for your organization is a challenging decision to make. In this respect, it is crucial to keep the following critical factors in mind:

**1\. Master the Basics:** Check whether the software can handle services for calling and scheduling, issuance of bills, and patient history.

**2\. User-Friendly:** Select simple-to-use software for your users, you, and other employees of the organization.

**3\. Workflow Compatibility:** See to it that the system will not complicate your clinic's operations but will instead improve it.

**4\. Reliable Support:** Choose software that offers a straight and easy approach to contact customer service.

**5\. Seamless Data Migration:** Make sure to hire trusted healthcare software developers who enables the transfer of existing data easily.

**6\. Comprehensive Records:** Make sure that adding or managing patient records is possible and easy.

**7\. Integration Capabilities:** In particular, there is a need to check the application's compatibility with the companies that your organization collaborates with.

**8\. Opportunity for Queries:** Make sure you are okay with asking about the flow of the conversation at any point.

**9\. Data Security:** The system should also have adequate security standards to retain sensitive information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does patient management software work?

Patient management software streams different patient information, such as medical records, appointments, etc., on an easy-to-use digital platform. It allows healthcare practitioners to centralize workflows, enhance patient communication, and amp up complete patient experience.

### Can PMS be personalized to meet the requirements of distinct healthcare facilities?

Yes, PMS can be tailored to match the needs of various healthcare facilities. It can be customized in the form of personalizing the software’s features, UI/UX, and workflows per the healthcare organization’s demands. Healthcare firms can collaborate with software developers to analyze software preferences, leading to the development of custom-fit solutions offering better efficiency, security, and compliance.

### What challenges come at time of PMS implementation and in what ways can they be addressed?

PMS implementation can have severe blockages, including pushback to change, data migration concerns, and system establishment issues. These challenges can be duly addressed by actively incorporating stakeholders in the process from the start. Resistance to change can be eliminated by offering proper training and support to the staff. At the same time, the healthcare organization may finely work with the software vendor to mitigate data migration and transition issues.
