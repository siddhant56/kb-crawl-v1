## AI in Healthcare Statistics by Patients

When it comes to patients or users, they are more scared of leveraging the advantages of new technology. However, some patients have experienced positive outcomes of AI for cancer treatment and therapy.

**60% of Americans are not comfortable with their provider relying on AI-powered technologies.**

In a recent study, 11,004 US citizens were surveyed, and it was discovered that 6 out of 10 were uncomfortable about their healthcare professional using artificial intelligence (AI) to diagnose them or suggest treatments.

**Around 60% of Americans believe AI will not lead to better health results.**

Although 38% of participants thought that utilizing AI would lead to better health outcomes, 33% disagreed, and 27% had no opinion.

Concerns about AI's potential to harm the relationship between patients and providers (57%) and its potential to compromise patient record security (37%) are also present.

**75% of Americans think AI will be used in healthcare in the coming days.**

Artificial intelligence is a relatively new idea for a large portion of the population. 70% of people, including those who have heard a lot about AI, still think the healthcare sector will move too quickly and do not fully understand the risks to patients.

**When it comes to skin cancer screening, 65% of US adults say they would either definitely or probably want AI to be utilized.**

One area where the public feels AI would be helpful is skin cancer screening, with 55% of respondents thinking it would be even more accurate than traditional screening. Interestingly, while uneducated minorities tended to be more scared of such an application, educated young men were the biggest proponents of AI.

**Talking to an AI chatbot is more appealing to one in four Americans than seeing a real therapist.**

Telephone therapy was the first, followed by messaging apps. These days, more and more people are using AI chatbots, like ChatGPT, to receive therapy without the need for human intervention. Now that AI therapy is so widely used, 40% of Americans would rather use it than see a real therapist in person.

## Future of AI in Healthcare Statistics

How does AI transform the healthcare industry? Let’s unravel the statistics of the future of AI in healthcare.

**With predictive AI solutions, there could be a 50% reduction in hospital admissions.**

According to a Clare Medical study, hospital admissions could be decreased by implementing artificial intelligence (AI) strategies to identify older individuals who are more likely to encounter "medical events". This reduces expenses, preserves lives, and makes hospital beds available for other emergencies.

**AI models can predict cancer patient survival with 80% accuracy.**

An artificial intelligence (AI) model created by researchers at the University of British Columbia can recognize patient features from notes taken by oncologists and predict cancer survival with 80% accuracy.

In an effort to improve results, this enables medical professionals to recommend patients to support services earlier or to pursue more aggressive treatment plans.

**AI is twice as fast as humans in ruling out suspected heart attacks.**

The effectiveness of treating heart attacks was much improved by a recent trial conducted by the British Heart Foundation and the NIHR. With 99.6% accuracy, CoDE-ACS was able to rule out a heart attack in twice as many patients as it could in humans.

This ensures that they are discharged or receive alternative care, freeing up staff members to attend to patients with a higher risk.

**AI can detect early signs of dementia with the most accuracy.**

CognoSpeak, an artificial intelligence tool, can identify Alzheimer's disease 90% of the time using patient language and speech patterns, which is comparable to traditional methods.

**Algorithms that create counterfactuals are as accurate as the top 25% of physicians.**

Associative algorithms, which identify diseases that have a significant correlation with a patient's symptoms, have proven successful for AI.

As opposed to the top 44% when utilizing associative algorithms, a counterfactual algorithm that employs causal reasoning was demonstrated to be as accurate as the top 25% of physicians.

**Precision medicine powered by AI will be dominated by oncology and neurology.**

More than 31% of the precision medicine market's revenue share in 2022 came from oncology (cancer diagnostics). Over the next ten years, there will be a rise in neurological conditions, including dementia and epilepsy, which are expected to dominate the market.

**AI robot surgery could reduce hospital stays for patients by over 20%.**

According to research, an artificial intelligence robot lowered complications in orthopedic surgery five times more than surgeons operating alone. Additionally, this may lead to a 21% decrease in patients' time in the hospital after surgery. Subsequent savings are estimated at $40 billion per year.

**AI nursing assistants could reduce 20% of maintenance work.**

AI-powered nurse aides are predicted to save $20 billion annually by cutting down on the 20% of time nurses spend on patient maintenance duties. Sensely's "Molly" chatbot illustrates this, asking patients questions, evaluating their symptoms, and directing them to the right resource.
