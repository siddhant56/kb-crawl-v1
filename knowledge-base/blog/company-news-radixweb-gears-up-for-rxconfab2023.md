# Radixweb Gears Up to Celebrate RXConfab2023 with Vision Customer Xcellence

Jul 2, 2023

Summary: _We are gearing up for our annual celebration RXConfab2023 on 21st July with**#CelebrateInnovation**. Our pledge for 2023 is a shared purpose of **Vision Customer Xcellence** which is aimed at crafting a well-rounded diversity of connected experiences with a disruption-ready team, upskilled with relevant industry certifications._

Not even a month is left for Radixweb’s annual celebration RxConfab2023, which is all set to take the centre stage on July 21st. And like all the other years, the annual pledge has been dropped recently - #CelebrateInnovation, which ideally is an extension of Radixweb’s larger-than-life business promise Vision Customer Xcellence.

Being a ‘culture capital’ Radixweb has consistently nourished a tradition of innovation and constant learning across the hierarchies in the organization. The founders, forever learners themselves, have instilled across the teams, the need to constantly keep oneself updated as per relevant market skills. At Radixweb, learning never stops. And skilled professionals are further trained as per changing market needs, for them to emerge as true disruption-ready professionals.

Radixweb prides itself to be a people-first organization, placing Customer Experience and Employee Experience at the forefront of the curve. With Vision Customer Xcellence, the superlative aim is to lead from the front and build a tribe of experienced and certified business-minded technologists who can address business-critical concerns with a nimble, digital-first approach.

Radixweb through its two decades of journey has built for itself trailblazing digital capabilities that has been tested across 25+ markets. Through its culture of innovation and constant learning, the visionaries of the firm have shaped up a team of 650+ disruption-ready professionals whose appetite for challenging technical advancement is profound.

Vision Customer Xcellence is another step ahead in Radixweb’s pledge to serve as a true people-first business. The COO, Dharmesh Acharya mentions, “Our people are our lifeblood. And by people, we mean both, our internal and external stakeholders, our teams and our clients. We are committed to cater a 360-degree value to all our associates.”

He adds, “For our clients, we want to become true instruments of growth for them- supporting their technology pursuits with our purpose-led strategies and nuanced development capabilities empowered by industry approved learnings. As for our resources, we want to broaden their horizon of growth and bring home a sense of personal achievement for them. With certified industry learnings, Vision Customer Xcellence would unlock their individual success stories – the results of which would pan out on a broader spectrum. “

Radixweb is one organization that has astounded waves in the tech landscape through its pursuit of innovation. Sustaining and expanding in the offshore software development market for over two decades, it has carved its niche as one of the ‘most trusted’ software partners of choice.

Speaking about his broader vision, CEO, Divyesh Patel states that, “Throughout the course of two decades in business, we have envisioned our true success in the achievements of our clients. Apart from being highly inclusive of our client’s business aspirations, we have always gone out of our ways to deliver what was traditionally expected of software partnerships.

We aim to further this lead, build an extremely gratifying CX with the help of newly acquired digital capabilities. We are targeting the core strengths of our teams to reimagine new business roles. Thus, safeguarding the interests of our clientele and leading them towards a sustainable digital growth. By instilling a new sense of purpose within our teams, we aim to build a new era of relevancy.”

As a software development company of repute, Radixweb has always fostered a collaborative approach in its development journeys. From hiring the best-in-class talent, to adopting digitally nimble technology, accessing supportive infrastructure to encouraging creativity and innovation – the team stands supported with a well-rounded organizational process.

The 2023 annual pledge of Vision Customer Xcellence is a drive to reimagine the true capabilities of the team with relevant industry learnings. Extending an all-out support to its clients is an inherent culture at Radixweb. **#CelebrateInnovation** exalts this culture into an organizational DNA that promotes overall purpose-led growth for everybody involved.
