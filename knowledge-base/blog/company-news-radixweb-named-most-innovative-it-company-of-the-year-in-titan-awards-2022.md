# Radixweb as The Most Innovative IT Company of the Year by Titan Awards

Nov 20, 2022

 _**The Titan Business Excellence Awards Jury has named us as the Most Innovative IT Company of The Year 2022. The premium award channel has recognized our efforts in decoding stability in the area of web development through our open-source framework, RxWeb**_

Drumrolls please! Because we have made it big – Again! The Titan Business Excellence Awards 2022 session has awarded us the title of the ‘Most Innovative IT Company of The Year’. This recognition comes in because of our contribution to the global tech community in terms of streamlining web development processes with our brain child RxWeb.

Developed originally in 2012 by our tech masters, RxWeb is a sturdy open-source, clean-code framework based on Angular and .Net Core, which addresses code stability, data breach, and evolving tech integration concerns. By reinforcing performance, scalability, flexibility, best practices, and security in the application development process through a transparent dev model RxWeb operates with the core idea of developing robust enterprise software.

We received robust recognition from Angular in early 2020 and have further added stupendous functionalities to it spanning around microservices, serverless and domain-driven designs. RxWeb has also passed JWT-based authentication and OWASP security compliance which means your web development is in safe hands! And what more, our clients have miraculously reduced their enterprise software development time starkly by 50%, integrating better code stability and most importantly, bringing in 60,000 hours of net benefit in developing digital software solutions.

Speaking about this magnificent win, here’s what our COO, Mr. Dharmesh Acharya said, “Looks the stars are super aligned in our favor in 2022. We extend our thankfulness to the Titan Business Awards for recognizing our efforts in streamlining web development for the global players. On this occasion, I must comment that receiving this phenomenal opportunity to present the ingenuity of our brainchild RxWeb on this broad spectrum is a result of our relentless drive for innovation.”

He also said that, “What began as an initiative to fast-track our internal development process, has now emerged as a radical tool to help thousands of businesses and developers. RxWeb has emerged as a literal translation of our core goal – enabling businesses find value with every tech investment. And thorough this framework, numerous businesses have built scalable, resilient and sustainable growth prospects.”

We believe that being lauded beyond the status quo of the categories and scoring a ‘gold’ status because of our transparent business processes, stupendous work culture and our commitment to build credible digital ecosystems has been one of our best wins this year.

As a software partner for leading global businesses, the Titan Awards is another testament of our commitment towards the global digital space, empowering one and all with open-source, easily accessible high-quality product and top-notch service offerings.
