# Make the Best Business Decisions Ever: 6 Strategies to Master the Art

Divyesh Patel

Published : Sep 20, 2023

As a seasoned leader in the IT industry, I am aware of the impact of small and big decisions that come my way every day. And it is necessary to make every decision thoughtfully because even a single blunder can lead to the loss of millions.

So, how do you make decisions that work? I believe decision-making is an art that every IT leader needs to master. Your decisions are your strategy to navigate amidst the fluctuations and disputes of the market. Hence, they should be well-informed, thoughtful, and sound.

Better and more successful decisions on a consecutive basis differentiate the best leader from the average. In a report I came across, I read that C-Suite respondents spend >30% of their time on decision-making, while more than a quarter spend a significant chunk of their time making decisions. So, it is not a skill to be taken lightly but an art wherein time and resources must be invested. But this art needs to be learned and implemented well.
