 _**Quick Overview** : Companies need to conduct product testing before launching a product to maintain their reputation. Prototype and MVP are two of the best strategies for product testing. In this article, we will explain the differences between them so that you can choose the one that best suits your next project's development._

As a business owner, you must realize how important it is to have product validation, right? Your project's success will depend on how your client perceives and accepts it. In the IT world, businesses use a prototype, or MVP, to test and validate ideas, features and functionality. Both strategies effectively determine whether stakeholders and the intended audience will support the project!

While MVP helps you focus on functionality and user feedback for your projects, prototype emphasizes visual representation. Plus, both methods are vital and efficient in terms of saving money and time. Now, the burning and crucial question is which strategy to choose from?
