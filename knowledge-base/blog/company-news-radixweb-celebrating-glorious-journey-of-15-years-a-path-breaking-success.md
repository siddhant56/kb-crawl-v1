# Radixweb Celebrating Glorious Journey of 15 Years - A Path-Breaking Success

Jul 20, 2015

Before 15 years Radixweb was an infant in IT industry. It was the startup of company with few industry veterans who came together to explore the IT outsourcing market. From 2000 to 2015, the small venture grown strongly and steadily and became one of the most reliable and leading IT outsourcing services providers. The journey was started with lots of struggle. However, with the valuable support of loyal clients, talented employees and valuable partners, Radixweb has successfully achieved several milestones.

Radixweb founded with the vision of ‘Client Satisfaction’. Initially the firm was working with the embedded development. And currently we have extended our portfolio in IT consulting and software development services, off- the-shelf solutions, customization and development services, embedded engineering services, Industry specific solution, Graphic designing and Image Processing services utilizing cutting edge technologies bringing high value to our clients. Quality application development is the benchmark for the Radixweb in which we never compromise in delivering authenticated IT outsourcing services to the clients.

Let’s have a drive of each year and look at the milestones achieved by Radixweb year by year.

  * 2000 - 2004 Establishment & Understanding Global IT Market
  * 2005 - 2009 Innovation & Expansion & Excellence
  * 2010 - 2015 Strong Market Presence with Inclusive IT Expertise
