# Radixweb Helps US Enterprises Navigate Growing Data Privacy and Security Expectations

Jan 28, 2026

Frisco, Texas, USA, Date: 29th January, 2026

Data is at the heart of how US enterprises operate today. From customer records and financial transactions to internal systems and analytics, businesses are handling more sensitive information than ever before. And as that volume grows, so does the pressure to keep it safe. At Radixweb, we understand that this challenge is no longer theoretical. It’s immediate. And it’s expensive when things go wrong.

Industry reports show that the average cost of a data breach in the United States is $9.36 million, the highest anywhere in the world. For many organizations, that kind of loss isn’t just a financial setback but can interrupt operations, damage credibility, and weaken customer trust overnight.
