# Radixweb Achieves Microsoft Gold Certified Partner Status

Aug 7, 2009

Radixweb, India’s premiere offshore software development company proudly announces that it has received the Microsoft Gold Certified Partner status within Microsoft Partner Program with competencies in mobility solutions, data management solutions and custom development solutions.

Being a Microsoft Gold Certified Partner, Radixweb receives a rich set of benefits from Microsoft such as access to latest software resources, training material and support. This in turn lends Radixweb a competitive edge by enabling it to provide superior and latest technology services to its customers.

“Today, customers are looking for software companies who can build partnerships and bridge the gaps between their business demands and technology capabilities,” said **Vimal Dagli, Vice President-Sales** , Radixweb.

"With the Gold Certification, Radixweb will enjoy increased trust among its customers. Today, Microsoft recognizes Radixweb as a new Gold Certified Partner for its expertise in providing customer satisfaction using Microsoft products and technology. Achieving the highly coveted status has been an exciting and thrilling experience for Radixweb,” said **Dharmesh Acharya, COO** , Radixweb. “We are also recognized as a Small Business Specialist by Microsoft which will help Radixweb cater cost effective solutions to the SMEs in a timely fashion,” informs Mr Acharya.

The Microsoft Partner Program was launched in December 2003 and represents Microsoft’s ongoing commitment to the success of its partners worldwide. The program offers a single, integrated partnering framework that recognizes partner expertise, rewards the total impact that partners have in the technology marketplace, and delivers more value to help partners’ businesses be successful.

Besides Microsoft .NET technology expertise, Radixweb also holds technical expertise on open source technology like PHP, MySQl, Joomla, Drupal, Zend framework, system programming and embedded development. Till date, Radixweb has developed 500+ customized software development applications on various technologies. Radixweb has also developed web to print solution with excellent online designer studio capabilities. Radixweb is headquartered at Ahmedabad, India and holds representative office in California, USA.
