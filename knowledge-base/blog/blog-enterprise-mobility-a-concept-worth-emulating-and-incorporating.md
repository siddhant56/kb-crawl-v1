## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is enterprise mobility strategy?

An enterprise mobility strategy is a blueprint that guide businesses in seamlessly integrating a wide range of mobile services into their regular organizational processes. It helps improve organizational productivity, fight cyber threats, reduces cost and addresses data governance challenges.

### What is an example of enterprise mobility?

At the heart of enterprise mobility is the need for enabling possible business tasks across mobile devices. A good example for it could be a scenario where a resource uses his laptop or PC to upload a crucial business document to the cloud, and then replay the same on an Ipad or any other mobile device at a remote site.
