# Radixweb Prepares for CeBIT 2012

Jan 29, 2012

Radixweb, an India based IT services provider, is all set to make its third appearance at CeBIT 2012, Hannover, Germany. We can be visited at **Stall D-44-12 Hall 6** from March 6-10, 2012.

Following last year’s modus operandi, Radixweb will showcase its technical potential and abilities in four major groups – web applications, desktop applications, mobile applications, and systems programming. This year, Radix will also unveil its recently developed mobile applications. The company also plans to reveal its latest R&D in embedded technology. And then there would be the business ready solutions for different industries at display at the Radix stall.

Another featured highlight of the Radix stall would be the free technology consultations and project discussions made available by the Radix team. “Yes, we will be sitting down with our visitors and talking to them about the latest in technology and how they can use it. We would discuss their requirements and make suggestions. In fact, we plan to use CeBIT as a knowledge exchange platform”, comments Naresh Bordia, Vice President, Business Development at Radixweb.

Radix has already had previous two CeBIT appearances. 2010 was the debut appearance while 2011 was the learning curve. And now in 2012, the company looks to up its ante. “Been there done that – that’s how we look at CeBIT 2012. We are confident of handling any unexpected surprises. In fact, we might have a few surprises up our sleeves”, signed off Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb.

CeBIT 2012 would be an interesting outing for Radixweb. Watch out for Radix at CeBIT 2012!
