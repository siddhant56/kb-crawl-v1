# Radix is Offering Complementary Passes to CeBIT 2012

Feb 5, 2012

Radixweb, India’s premiere IT services provider, is offering complementary passes to CeBIT 2012, digital industry’s biggest fanfare to be held at Hannover, Germany. Visit Radix at **Stall D-44-12 Hall 6** .

For Radixweb, this is the third appearance in a row at CeBIT. During CeBIT 2010 and 2011, they had a steady flow of walk-in visitors. And now at CeBIT 2012, they plan to spend good amount of time with their visitors and engage in project consultations. They can book the time of the day when they would like to discuss their requirements with Radix’s project consultants and win complementary visitor passes.

Radixweb has been consistently delivering quality and reliable IT services to a global audience for more than 11 years now. Over the years, they have mastered the art of custom development for web applications, desktop applications, mobile applications, and embedded systems. They have developed a very impressive portfolio of business ready solutions for industries like printing, real estate, travel and hotel, schools and education, and insurance. For a long time they have been the pioneers of PHP development. They have also been Microsoft Gold Certified Partners for Microsoft technologies. And now they have gained an edge in COTS customization technologies, Ruby on Rails, mobile technologies, and open source frameworks.

Radix has also been providing image editing services through one of its divisions for more than a decade now. The service is particularly popular for its ability to download 2000+ high quality images per day and maintain a turnaround time of 24 hours.

You can have the opportunity to see some of the Radix solutions live in action, discuss project requirements, and explore different technologies.
