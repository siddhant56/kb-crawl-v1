# Radixweb Earns Platinum at the 2026 TITAN Business Awards for Outstanding IT Software

Apr 9, 2026

Texas, USA – Date: 10th April, 2026

Radixweb has been awarded the Platinum Honor at the 2026 TITAN Business Awards for Outstanding IT Software in the Information Technology category. Platinum is the highest tier of recognition TITAN offers, reserved for entries that demonstrate the strongest combination of technical excellence, business impact, and innovation, as assessed by an international panel of senior professionals through a structured blind judging process.

The 2026 season of TITAN attracted over 5,100 entries globally, with winning entries evaluated through a structured blind judging process by an international panel of senior professionals, assessed solely on merit across business leadership, operations, strategy, and innovation.

"This recognition means a great deal to us, not because of what it says about a single project, but because of what it reflects about how we work across every engagement," said Dharmesh Acharya, COO, Radixweb. "We have always believed that software quality is non-negotiable, and it is gratifying to see that validated at this level."

## We Did Not Set Out to Win an Award

We set out to build trustworthy custom software solutions that deliver intended outcomes. Software that our clients can depend on when it matters, grow with over time, and come back to when the next challenge arrives. That has been the same since we started in 2000.

Over 25 years, we have completed more than 4,200 projects for over 3,000 clients across 25 countries. Every one of them came up with its own challenges, its own constraints, and its own definition of success. What stayed the same across all of them was how our teams approached the work, with the same rigor, the same accountability, and the same intent to get it right. We think that consistency is why 97% of our revenue comes from repeat clients. When the work holds up, people come back.

Our 650+ engineers, AI specialists, and cloud architects do not operate at a different standard depending on the size or visibility of a project. Whether it is a complex enterprise software platform, a cloud-native application, or an AI-integrated solution, the approach is the same. The Platinum Honor from TITAN is an outside perspective confirming what we already hold ourselves to internally.

## What Consistent Quality Actually Looks Like

We hold ISO 27001:2022 and ISO 9001:2015 certifications, maintain SOC 2 compliance, and operate as a Microsoft Gold Partner. We do not mention these for the sake of a credentials list. They reflect how seriously we take the responsibility of building software that real businesses depend on. Software where a failure is not just a technical problem, but a business one.

Quality, in our experience, is not something you can apply at the end of a project. It has to be built into how a team works from the first conversation. It is built into how requirements are challenged, how architecture decisions are made, how edge cases are handled before they become incidents. That is the culture this award belongs to.

"Our teams bring the same level of commitment to every project, the same attention to architecture, performance, and long-term value," said Pratik Mistry, EVP – Technology Consulting, Radixweb. "This award is a recognition of that culture, and it belongs to every person at Radixweb who shows up with that mindset every day."

## What We Are Building Toward

The way businesses use software is changing faster than it has at any point in our 25 years. AI, cloud, and automation are not future considerations for our clients. They are present realities they are navigating right now, often under pressure and with limited room for error.

We are deepening our capabilities across AI, ML, and data engineering so we can help clients move faster, make better decisions with their data, and build platforms that do not need to be rebuilt every few years to stay relevant. That work is already underway across many of our current engagements.

This recognition is something we are genuinely proud of. But more than anything, it is a reminder of why we work the way we do and a push to keep raising the bar.

Twenty-five years in, the standard we hold ourselves to is still the same one we started with. We just have more evidence now that it was the right one to choose.

## About the TITAN Business Awards

The TITAN Business Awards is a leading international business awards program organized by the International Awards Associate (IAA). It recognizes organizations and professionals that demonstrate measurable impact, operational excellence, and sustained business growth. The award honors those who challenge conventions, inspire progress, and shape the future of global business across industries worldwide.
