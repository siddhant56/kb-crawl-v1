# Radix Wraps Up Successful CeBIT Australia 2012

May 31, 2012

Spirits were high and enthusiasm contagious as Radix successfully wrapped up CeBIT Australia on May 24, 2012. Large number of attending visitors and participating exhibitors proved once again why CeBIT Australia is termed as an important event of Australian business calendar.

For Radix this was debut participation at CeBIT Australia. But we were ready to handle all the action. From day one itself, we had visitors walking into our stall wanting to know about our technology outsourcing and consulting services. We had appointments pre-booked for the event. So we carried out personalized consultations with these appointments. We discussed Dotnet applications and our technical expertise. We also had visitors who were interested in mobile application development. We showed them some of our mobile apps live in action. We showed them apps developed for iPhone, Android, and Windows Mobile. We also had a mobile app developed in PhoneGap to display at CeBIT Australia. Our team fielded questions on how much custom development we can do in web and mobile applications.

We also displayed our ready-to-use business solutions for different industries. OnPrintShop, our web to print solution for global print industry, became extremely popular. We had launched a whole new set of upgraded features and capabilities at drupa 2012 prior to CeBIT Australia. These special features turned the tables for us at CeBIT Australia as well. In addition to this, we had two more solutions that were unique and interesting for the visitors - audit compliance solution with web and mobile counterpart and asset tracking and inventory management solution.

CeBIT Australia was also an excellent business networking event for Radix. We participated in keynote address of the event and post event press conferences. We shared views on next generation technology and how it would shape up our world. We shared our own R&D on embedded technology and some unique integrations we have achieved over the past year. We explored various strategic alliance and expansion opportunities as well.

We would like to thank all our CeBIT Australia visitors for making this a memorable debut for us. We hope you had a pleasant experience with our team. We would now focus on fulfilling the commitments that we have made. We have also gained a lot of learnings from CeBIT Australia. We would implement these learnings to serve you better in future.
