# Galaxy Automation Participates in IPMEX 2013 & Presents OnPrintShop Web2Print

Oct 22, 2013

We are very happy to share Galaxy Automation SDN BHD our reseller partner will be showcasing OnPrintShop web2print solutions - Standard, Online Photobook and Trade Printer at IPMEX 2013, Malaysia - **Booth 2198** from October 24th to 27th, 2013.

IPMEX, Malaysia has emerged as successful renowned Printing, Paper, Packaging Machinery exhibition with 20,000 plus visitors expected.

OnPrintShop web2print is a flagship product of Radixweb and has developed wide range of solutions to help SME PSPs achieve their online objectives. OnPrintShop offers License (ownership) and Subscription Solution with flexibility to customize and add on features as needed.

“We have a strong presence in South East Asia supporting Malaysian, Singapore, Honk Kong, Chinese and Korean clients who have recognized us for our personalized support and user friendly solutions”, states Dharmesh Acharya, COO, OnPrintShop-Radixweb.

Galaxy Automation, ISO 9000:2008 certified company, has gone from strength to strength and served local market since 1995. They are exclusive distributor of innovative and technologically advanced digital printing & office automation products with international distinctions.

“As we enjoy total sales and technical support direct from international principals, Galaxy Automation is able to ensure top-notch pre and after-sales support to its customers”, states Jimmy Tan Boon Leong, Senior Product Executive, Galaxy Automation SDN BHD.

The Galaxy Group of Companies has been awarded with numerous awards. These include the Golden Bull 2004, the Asia Pacific Entrepreneur Excellent Award, and the NPC Productivity Award 2004.

**Key Web2Print Solutions Presented by Galaxy Automation (Booth 2198)**

  * Multilingual web2print solution in subscription and license
  * Online photobook software solution (No download required)
  * Trade printer solution (Reseller Network)
  * Multi domain with central admin solution
  * Custom web2print solution

**Key OnPrintShop Strengths**

  * Multilingual web2print storefront with integrated order management system
  * Intuitive online designer studio with PDF template engine & print ready PDF output
  * Retail and B2B or private corporate stores to manage ordering & reordering
  * Highly user-friendly admin panel to manage orders, products, content, customers
  * Online marketing tools & reports to reduce operational cost
  * Personalized support, store setup, & interactive trainings

Galaxy Automation SDN BHD has partnered with OnPrintShop to offer one stop solution to its clients with web2print needs. Galaxy will offer affordable and highly customizable web to print solutions from OnPrintShop to their SME printers in Malaysian market while OnPrintShop will be responsible for support and solution enhancements. OnPrintShop will be offering its consistently evolving upgrades and personalized services to Galaxy Automation customers as well.
