# OnPrintShop Web to Print Solution @ CeBIT 2012

Feb 1, 2012

OnPrintShop web to print solution is back at **Stall D-44-12 Hall 6** of CeBIT 2012, Hannover Germany. CeBIT, the digital industry’s biggest expo, is slated to be held from March 6-10, 2012.

OnPrintShop, a web based solution from Radixweb, allows you to take your print business processes online and optimize them. It’s a simple to use solution that maximizes revenue and minimizes cost for a printer. It is also an ever evolving solution that has a steady flow of upgrades. Ever since its last appearance at CeBIT, OnPrintShop has added a lot more to its web to print solution. “OnPrintShop is getting better over time. We have a dedicated R&D team consistently taking inputs from our clients worldwide to understand their needs and provide solutions for the same”, reveals Naresh Bordia, Vice President, Business Development, OnPrintShop-Radixweb. “We have developed robust integrations with several third party tools as well”, adds Mr Bordia.

The OnPrintShop-Radixweb team shall be conducting live solution demos at Stall D-44-12 Hall 6.

While the R&D team of OnPrintShop has focused on enriching the technical aspect, the strategic level has focused on handholding and support aspect for OnPrintShop. “We have established a tiered support system for our customers. We are offering detailed solution walkthrough, support documentation, videos, and live chat support to our customers”, informs Mr Bordia.

Over the past year, OnPrintShop has also entered into strategic alliance with RICOH Canada. “We are proud to be RICOH Canada’s chosen partners for web to print solution. We are going to use this partnership to deliver turnkey yet affordable web to print solution to the Canadian market”, states Dharmesh Acharya, COO, OnPrintShop-Radixweb.

The other USP of OnPrintShop is that they offer multiple services under single roof. “We offer services for designing, template creation, image editing, and SEO consultancy. Additionally, we have a dedicated technical team that can integrate Microsoft technologies, open source frameworks, embedded technologies, networking solutions and whatnot”, adds Mr Bordia. “We have also developed our solution to be flexible enough to accommodate changing business requirements quickly”, he states.

Given all this, OnPrintShop certainly becomes a participant to look out for at CeBIT 2012.
