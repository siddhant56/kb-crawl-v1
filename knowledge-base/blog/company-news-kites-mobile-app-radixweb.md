# Kites - New Mobile App From Radixweb

Feb 23, 2012

Radixweb has released a new mobile app Kites – Soaring High. The application is compatible with iPhone, iPod touch, and iPad. The application falls in the utilities category and is available on app store.

Kites is an application developed by Radix for kite enthusiasts. The application shares information on kites and the role they play in the cultural dynamics of a country. The application explains history of kites and the different types of kites popular at a global level.

And for people who are interested in India’s fascination with kites, this is the app. Kites sheds light on ‘patang baazi’ – the kite fighting tradition of India. It explains the different types of manjas used in kite fights and the different kite fight techniques. The application also takes the users on a tour of popular kite markets of the country. The app focuses on kite markets that are known for their turnover as well as traditional existence. A user can also find information for the country’s famous kite museum right here in the app.

And then there are the kite festivals that find their place in the app. Users can find details of kite festivals of international calibre hosted across the country right here in the app. They can find info for Ahmedabad’s international kite festival, Jodhpur’s desert kite festival, Cambay’s beachside kite festival, and more. All this info is accompanied by a colorful gallery of images and videos. The users can experience the thrill of kite flying through this gallery.

Interestingly, this app belongs to a long list of other apps released by Radixweb. Their mobile apps include mobile BI solutions and m-commerce solutions. They have developed MatchMe - a memory game and PersonalSMS – an application that allows users to personalize SMS templates. The company has custom developed apps like iPhone GPS, Quality Check Tool, and MenusStamp on demand for its customers. And then there is their web to print solution, OnPrintShop, which is again compatible with iPad.

Radix has a complete team dedicated to mobile application development. They develop apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows Mobile, Nokia Mobile, and Blackberry.
