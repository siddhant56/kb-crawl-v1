# Radix Becomes Sitefinity Partner

Apr 19, 2012

Radixweb, an IT services provider from India, has acquired partnership with Sitefinity, an ASP.Net based content management system (CMS). This places Radix in the elite global network of Sitefinity Partners.

Sitefinity is an ASP.Net based CMS developed by Telerik for enterprise usage. This web-based CMS caters to end users as well as developers. On one hand, it renders a memorable and exciting experience to end users. On the other, it provides a flexible architecture to developers to extend functionalities and even custom develop them.

So then what does being a Sitefinity Partner mean for Radixweb? Firstly, we become a recognized member of a global community consisting of more than 200 partners. Radix has already been working with Telerik controls for more than 3 years. So this partner status certifies us as Sitefinity experts. It certifies the superior quality of our Sitefinity development, integration, and consulting services. Our partner status allows us priority access to Sitefinity’s upgrades and third party components for integrations. We get access to a wider array of Sitefinity components. We also gain access to several product licenses which means we offer a more authenticated development. We also get wider exposure and better promotion due to this partner status.

But most importantly, we get priority support from Telerik’s technical team. In case of a technical roadblock Telerik’s very own technical team supports and guides us. As a result, we offer rapid development and deployment of applications at reduced cost.

In addition to Sitefinity Partner, Radix is also a Microsoft Gold Certified Partner and Kentico Partner. We specialize in Dotnet development platforms ASP.Net, VB.Net, & C#.Net. We offer services in COTS custom solutions and Microsoft’s enterprise integration services. We develop enterprise applications, web-based business management systems, e-commerce applications, and networking communities.
