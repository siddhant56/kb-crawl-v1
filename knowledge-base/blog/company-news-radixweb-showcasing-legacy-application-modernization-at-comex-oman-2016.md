# Radixweb Showcasing Legacy Application Modernization at COMEX OMAN 2016

Mar 29, 2016

**Seasoned IT Services and Software Development Company to Showcase a Wide Range of Legacy Application Modernization Services that Facilitate Driving Better Value for Business by Shifting IT Spend from High Maintenance to Greater Innovation at COMEX OMAN 2016, April 12-16, 2016, Booth #517**

The need for greater agility, shrinking expertise for obsolete technologies, high maintenance costs are primary challenges with legacy applications. The missing mobile, social, and cloud advantages forcing companies considering application modernization.

The IT department is expected to drive value for money by reducing operation costs and capital expenditure wherever possible. Migrating and modernizing legacy systems make application environment more responsive, competitive and efficient. Be it portfolio assessment, re-architecture or re-hosting, Radixweb to be the single application modernization partner helping company’s overcome IT challenges and drive increased ROI.

> As per state of IT report by Spiceworks in 2016 – Companies Expected to Use 56% of its IT Budget Migrating to Cloud Applications or Upgrading its Legacy Application to Latest Platform

At booth #517, COMEX Oman, Radixweb team will help companies to redesign a roadmap to modernize legacy applications & make it adaptive to latest technology like mobility, virtualization, cloud computing.

"We consider our collaboration with Radixweb a major success, Radixweb proved themselves indispensable when it came to the successful redevelopment and launch of our collections platform, which has received overwhelmingly positive feedback from all stakeholders involved. Radix has an exceptional project management approach and maintains constant communication throughout the development process. If you’re looking for a company that consistently exceeds your expectations in your legacy application modernization project, look no further”, said Jana L. Bauer, MIS Director at Commercial Collection Agency, USA.

“With the growing cost of maintaining legacy applications and the need to enable a mobile workforce, companies are getting aggressive about legacy application modernization. CIOs finds modernization of its legacy application as a major challenge. Blending 15 years of industry experience Radixweb manage to reduce budget and execution timeline of application modernization project through a global delivery model to help companies migrating and modernizing its legacy systems to latest technology platforms. The full life-cycle scope includes Portfolio Assessment, Application Re-engineering, Cloud enablement, SaaS enablement etc. Radixweb is helping organizations define a systematic approach to its modernization journey to drive greater agility and create an adaptive business by stripping out maintenance costs on its application portfolio”, said Pratik Mistry, Sr. Business Head, Radixweb.

“COMEX Oman draws technology leaders from around the world where they are more considered how technology can help resolve business challenges and deliver greater value. We are poised to deliver a range of technology services which are aiding companies to leverage advanced technology to gain increased flexibility & scalability and reduced costs of maintenance”, he further commented.
