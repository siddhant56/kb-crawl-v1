### Scaling Products Without Compromising Performance

Fintech platforms need to meet different regulatory requirements like PCI-DSS audits, GDPR data residency, and regional standards (PDPA, MAS TRM). As products scale or organizations approach compliance later in development, it leads to heavy rework and audit risks. 70% of fintech startups miss deadlines due to regulatory rework costing $50K-$200K extra.

At Radixweb, our engineers enable zero post-launch remediation and allow fintech providers to expand into new markets by addressing regulatory expectations during system planning. Our team builds compliance-first fintech system architectures with a 100% audit pass rate. We also embed compliance verification into CI/CD pipelines, so that every code commit is automatically checked against PCI-DSS, SOC2, and region-specific standards.
