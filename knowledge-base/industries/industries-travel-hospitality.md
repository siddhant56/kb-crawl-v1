## Frequently Asked Questions

### What types of businesses do you serve in the travel and hospitality industry?

We’ve built products for large hotel groups, regional travel marketplaces, B2B consolidators, OTAs, and experience providers. Whether you're running multi-location operations or managing a niche segment, we customize our travel and hospitality industry solutions to fit your scale, legacy systems, and digital maturity.

### Can you develop a custom hotel management system for multi-property chains?

Yes, and we go beyond PMS basics. Our hospitality software development services, Help businesses handle shared services across properties (like group revenue ops), manage varied tax rules across locations, and allow corporate-level oversight with localized autonomy.

### How long does it take to develop a custom travel software solution?

Depends on how clearly your business logic is defined. If we’re talking about a fully integrated B2C platform with search, bookings, CRM, and partner modules, expect 4–6 months. But we often phase it smartly, launch with core functionality in 10–12 weeks, and expand post-MVP.

### How do you ensure secure payment integration for travel bookings?

We implement tokenization, enforce PCI-DSS compliance, isolate payment workflows, and include fraud flags for high-risk patterns (like unusual booking volumes or location mismatches) in our travel software solutions.

### Do you offer mobile app development for travel agencies and hotels?

Yes, but not just for bookings. For travel and hospitality IT solutions, we build mobile experiences that reflect the full guest or agent journey - pre-booking discovery, in-stay requests, cross-sell prompts, post-trip feedback loops. For internal ops, we’ve built apps for staff scheduling, housekeeping dispatch, and concierge services.

### Can you build B2B and B2C travel marketplaces with vendor and user panels?

We have built multi-tenant platforms that support layered access - agents, admins, vendors, end-users - all with configurable workflows. You will have custom commission engines, vendor onboarding, inventory mapping, payout automation, and dynamic pricing. B2B and B2C logic often conflict. We resolve that through architecture that treats each user group as a distinct business function.
