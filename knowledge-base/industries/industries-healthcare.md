## Our Security and Compliance Practices for Healthcare Software Development

### Compliance Standards

### Security Measures

### Compliance-Driven SDLC

#### HIPAA

Under HIPAA guidelines, we implement access controls, audit trails, encryption, and secure data flows to protect health information.

#### GDPR

We implement consent-driven data handling, explicit consent, deletion and retention policies, and regional hosting to support EU patient data rights.

#### HL7 / FHIR

Interoperability in our healthcare software is achieved by creating UIs and APIs that exchange clinical data using HL7 v2/v3 messages and FHIR resources.

#### DICOM

Our team manages medical imaging data using DICOM standards for secure image storage, routing, viewing, and metadata consistency in imaging workflows.

#### ICD-10 / CPT

To support coding accuracy and reimbursement processes, we align clinical documentation and billing workflows with ICD-10 and CPT standards.

#### HITECH

HITECH Act requirements we address through enhanced security controls, breach detection processes, and high-end protection of electronic health records.

#### FDA 21 CFR Part 11 (SaMD)

We implement validated systems, audit trails, electronic records, and controlled electronic signatures for regulated medical software.

#### SOC-2 Readiness

Security and availability controls are implemented by our developers to prepare systems for SOC-2 assessments and ongoing operational assurance.
