### What is real estate software development?

Real estate software development is a process of creating a wide range of proptech solutions for real estate professionals, agencies, and companies. Property management system, Real Estate CRM, marketplace, virtual tour, HOA management, and construction management software are some popular real estate software types in the market.

### What is a CRM for real estate?

Real estate CRM is designed to streamline, automate, and manage customer relationship processes like contact management, lead tracking, automated communication (emails, texts, etc.), task management, etc.
